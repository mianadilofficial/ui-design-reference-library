Inside Plinth

Five parts under one sheet of fabric.

Pick a part to see where it sits and what it does.

Dial

Machined aluminium with 38 detents you can feel. Turn it for volume, press it to pause, hold it for a second to mute the microphones, double-press it to tune the room again.

Room tuning

Nine seconds, then the bass behaves.
