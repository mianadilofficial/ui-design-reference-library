Northern Thai kitchen on Tamarind Lane, Bangkok

Khao soi worth crossing the canal for.

Twelve tables in a shophouse with a sixty-year-old tamarind tree out front. We grill over longan-wood charcoal and pound one curry paste a day by hand, so the menu is short and follows the market.

The dish people book for

Khao soi gai ข้าวซอยไก่

Egg noodles in a coconut curry broth made with our own paste, a chicken drumstick braised until it falls from the bone, and a nest of noodles fried to order on top. Pickled mustard greens, shallots and lime on the side, so you set the sourness yourself.

The menu เมนู

Northern food from Chiang Mai, Lampang and the hills around them, priced in Thai baht with service included. Most dishes use pork, fish sauce or shrimp paste. Dishes marked vegan use none of them, and where only one version is vegan, the dish says how we make it.

  • Vegan
  • Heat, from none to 3 flames

  • Nam prik num น้ำพริกหนุ่ม

    ฿160

    Green chillies, shallots and garlic roasted over charcoal and pounded with salt into a smoky, rough relish. Served with pork crackling (khaep mu, แคบหมู) and steamed vegetables.

    • House favourite
    • Heat 3 of 3: Hot

  • Nam prik ong น้ำพริกอ่อง

    ฿150

    Minced pork and tomato cooked down with a dried chilli paste into a mild, sweet and sour dip, with pork crackling, cucumber and long beans to scoop it.

    • Heat 1 of 3: Mild

  • Tam makhuea ตำมะเขือ

    ฿130

    Long green eggplants grilled until soft, pounded with roasted chilli, garlic and shallot, and finished with a boiled egg and fresh herbs.

    • Heat 2 of 3: Medium heat

  • Kaeng hang le แกงฮังเล

    ฿260

    Pork belly and shoulder simmered with ginger, garlic, tamarind and a dry spice mix until soft. A Northern curry with Burmese roots, rich rather than hot.

    • House favourite
    • Heat 1 of 3: Mild

  • Kaeng khae แกงแค

    ฿200

    A herb and vegetable curry with no coconut milk: wild betel leaves, acacia shoots, pea eggplants and chicken in a peppery broth. The mushroom version uses a paste without shrimp paste and is seasoned with soy instead of fish sauce.

    • Vegan with mushrooms
    • Heat 2 of 3: Medium heat

  • Kaeng khanun แกงขนุน

    ฿220

    Young jackfruit and pork ribs in a light, sour broth with tomato and acacia leaves. No coconut milk.

    • Heat 1 of 3: Mild

  • Larb khua ลาบคั่ว

    ฿220

    Minced pork, liver and crackling stir-fried with a Northern dried spice mix of makhwaen pepper, long pepper and dried chilli, then tossed with mint, sawtooth coriander and fried shallots. With sticky rice and raw greens.

    • House favourite
    • Heat 3 of 3: Hot

  • Sai ua ไส้อั่ว

    ฿180

    Northern pork sausage with lemongrass, makrut lime leaf, galangal and dried chilli, grilled slowly over longan-wood charcoal.

    • Heat 2 of 3: Medium heat

  • Jin tup จิ๊นตุ๊บ

    ฿210

    Beef grilled, beaten flat with a mallet and grilled again, served with a roasted chilli dip and sticky rice.

    • Heat 2 of 3: Medium heat

  • Hed yang เห็ดย่าง

    ฿160

    King oyster mushrooms grilled until they char at the edges, with nam prik num made with salt instead of fish sauce.

    • Vegan
    • Heat 3 of 3: Hot

  • Khao soi gai ข้าวซอยไก่

    ฿220

    Egg noodles in a coconut curry broth with a braised chicken drumstick, topped with a nest of crispy fried noodles. Pickled mustard greens, shallots, lime and roasted chilli paste on the side.

    • House favourite
    • Heat 2 of 3: Medium heat

  • Khanom jeen nam ngiao ขนมจีนน้ำเงี้ยว

    ฿180

    Fermented rice noodles in a pork rib and tomato broth with pork blood tofu and dried red cotton tree flowers (dok ngiao), topped with crispy garlic. Lunch only.

    • Heat 2 of 3: Medium heat

  • Khao kan jin ข้าวกั๊นจิ๊น

    ฿150

    Rice mixed with minced pork and pork blood, wrapped in banana leaf and steamed, with fried garlic, cucumber and spring onion. Dried chilli comes on the side.

    • Not spicy

  • Tamarind ice cream ไอศกรีมมะขาม

    ฿110

    Made with coconut milk and tamarind from the tree out front, finished with a pinch of salt and chilli sugar. Sour first, then sweet.

    • House favourite
    • Vegan

  • Khao taen ข้าวแต๋น

    ฿90

    Sticky rice crackers sweetened with watermelon juice, fried, and drizzled with cane sugar.

    • Vegan

  • Khanom jok ขนมจ๊อก

    ฿90

    Sticky rice flour dumplings filled with coconut and palm sugar, wrapped in banana leaf and steamed.

    • Vegan

Sticky rice comes in a woven basket, ฿30 a basket. Ask us to turn the heat down on any dish; we can do it on everything except the larb.

Find us

27 Tamarind Lane, off Market Road, Bangkok
Email us

  • Train, 6 minutes on foot

    Market Road station, exit 2. Turn right and walk 400 m along Market Road, then turn left into Tamarind Lane at the yellow pharmacy.

  • Canal boat, 3 minutes on foot

    Get off at the temple pier and walk up Tamarind Lane away from the water. We are the fourth shophouse on your right, under the big tree.

  • Bus

    Buses along Market Road stop at the morning market, 150 m east of the lane.

  • By car

    The lane has no parking. The market car park on Market Road is free after 17:00.
