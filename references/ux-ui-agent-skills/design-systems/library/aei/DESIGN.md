## Overview

AI Energy (บริษัท เอไอ เอนเนอร์จี จำกัด (มหาชน), `https://www.aienergy.co.th/th`, title "AI Energy", 288 visible elements, 2774px tall) describes itself on the home page as a producer of renewable energy in the form of biodiesel made from palm oil, using a distillation and biodiesel-production system, with products for sale in Thailand and abroad. Its product navigation lists biodiesel, refined glycerin, cooking oil and by-products; the "ผลิตภัณฑ์ของเรา" (Our products) band shows four white product cards with bottles and palm fruit photographed on white.

The page opens on a slider photograph of the plant's tiered tower and white storage tanks under a blue sky, with the greeting "ยินดีต้อนรับ เอไอ เอนเนอร์จี" (Welcome, AI Energy) in 50px uppercase white type and green slider dots. Below, an "เกี่ยวกับเรา" (About us) section sets three slanted tiles at left (a green production-capacity tile with a globe and the figure 1,150,000, an orange business-groups tile and a green responsibility tile) against a text column and a grey "ข้อมูลเพิ่มเติมเกี่ยวกับเรา" button at right. A grey "ข้อมูลสำหรับนักลงทุน" band follows with three document cards (latest news, financial statements Q2 2569, annual report Form 56-1 One Report) each with a coloured top line and a stock photograph, then a "ข่าวสาร" news row of SET-notification thumbnails and a row of line icons.

A cookie consent bar (`cookieconsent`) was hidden by the extractor; it is not brand UI.

The extraction recorded no root CSS variables (rootVars is empty), so the system is plain class-based CSS: the palette above lives directly in component rules, with IBM Plex Sans and Kanit as the fonts named in the CSS.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use white cards on grey (#ebebeb) and pale (#f8f8f8) bands with the noticeable bottom-right shadow.
- Do reserve orange #f57d00 for the contact action and green #008c39 / #006e2d for hover, tiles and footer.
- Do set body text in IBM Plex Sans 300 at 14px/23.8px in warm grey #685d5d.
- Do show a coloured top rule on each investor document card.
- Don't use heavy weights for small captions; the page uses weight 100 and 200 there.
- Don't document the cookie bar as brand UI.
- Don't round buttons beyond 2.5px.

4. Cards: white, 5px radius, strong bottom-right shadow, tab-shaped action bar.
5. Hero with a slider photograph and a 50px uppercase white greeting.
6. Measure new components before adding tokens; states not recorded stay "not measured".
