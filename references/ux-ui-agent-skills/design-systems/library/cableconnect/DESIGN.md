## Overview

Cable Connect, written "CConnect" in its logo ("WELCOME TO CCONNECT!"), is a Thai internet and ICT service provider; the measured page is its home page, https://www.cableconnect.co.th/ (title "Cable Connect", `lang="th"`). The site sells leased lines, hotspot, enterprise internet and IGMP/IPTV services, and its text is Thai with English headings. The desktop capture is 1,280px wide and 6,071px tall. The first capture caught the hero slider before its slide had rendered (a plain red field); the page was re-extracted once and the second capture shows the slide, which is what this document describes.

The look is a corporate telecom template in red and white. Red (`#dd3333` for headings, `#d73b37` for the button, `#d12525` for the hero) is the only brand colour and is paired with white pages, a light grey (`#f5f5f5`) section, dark grey body text, and a gold (`#ffb536`) accent for one outlined button and the "Contact Us!" label. Headings are uppercase Kanit in a light weight with a short red rule and a small grey Thai subtitle beneath. Photography shows call-centre staff, city skylines and devices, often cropped to circles.

The capture, from the top: a thin red bar with the opening hours ("Mon – Fri: 9AM – 7PM") and round dark social icons; a white header over a faint grey triangle pattern with the red-and-black circular CConnect logo at the left and "Contact Us!" in gold above a large phone number ("+662 106 5252") at the right; a navigation row in Thai (หน้าแรก with a red home icon and red underline for the active page, ข่าวสาร, บริการของเรา, เกี่ยวกับเรา, คลังรูปภาพ, ข้อเสนอพิเศษ, ติดต่อเรา) and "SEARCH", separated by thin vertical rules; a red hero slide with a diagonal cut, the white uppercase headline "TAKING CARE OF YOU", "24 HOURS EVERYDAY", a gold-outlined "VIEW DETAILS" button, a photograph of headset-wearing staff and small slide thumbnails at the lower right; four columns with red circular icon badges and red uppercase titles ("12 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE", "BEST INDUSTRY EXPERTS", "FAST & EFFECTIVE SOLLUTIONS", "FLEXIBLE PRICING SYSTEM"); the heading "WHAT SERVICE ARE YOU LOOKING FOR" over a carousel of four circular photographs (Leased Line, Hotspot, Enterprise, IGMP (IPTV)) with red square arrow buttons at both edges; "WELCOME TO CCONNECT!"; a light-grey about block with the logo, a red "MORE ABOUT OUR COMPANY" button and a values list (RESPECT, DIVERSITY, TRANSPARENCY, INTEGRITY); a "WHY WORK WITH US" circular diagram; "RECENT NEWS & EVENTS" image cards; "REFERENCE CUSTOMER" tiles; and a footer with contact information, the logo and a repeat of the navigation.

The microcopy mixes Thai paragraphs with English uppercase headings, and some English labels contain typos ("SOLLUTIONS", "EVERTDAY" in the slide); one news card still shows template placeholder (lorem ipsum) text.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system or brand guideline for Cable Connect was found; the opened page https://www.cableconnect.co.th/ links to its own service and news pages and to social profiles, not to brand assets. The site is a WordPress build: its `<meta name="generator">` tags read WordPress 4.7.38, WooCommerce 3.2.6, WPBakery Page Builder, Slider Revolution 5.4.6.3.1 and LayerSlider 6.10.2, and the main stylesheet is the The7 theme (`/wp-content/themes/dt-the7/css/main.min.css?ver=5.5.0.1`). Font names confirm the stack: `icomoon-the7-social-icons`, `icomoon-arrows-the7`, `revicons` (Revolution Slider), `vcpb-plugin-icons` and `vc_grid_v1` (WPBakery), FontAwesome and fontello.

The extractor recorded zero `:root` custom properties, so brand values appear as literal values in the compiled styles (reds, and Kanit as the typeface from Google Fonts: `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Kanit:400,700,500|Open+Sans+Condensed:700`). The measured values in this file are therefore the source of truth; plugin colours and the WordPress default link blue are not brand tokens.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:
- Use red `#dd3333` for uppercase section headings in Kanit 300 at 40px, each with a short red rule and a small grey subtitle.
- Use `#d73b37` for square-cornered buttons (29px tall, white uppercase label); use the gold `#ffb536` outline for the one hero action.
- Set Thai body copy in Kanit 15px/23px in `#3d3d3d`.
- Put red circular icon badges above feature columns and crop service imagery to 220px circles.
- Keep sections on white and `#f5f5f5`.
- Give navigation links `#595959` with a `#dd3333` hover.

Don't:
- Don't round the buttons; the measured buttons have no radius.
- Don't use plugin or WordPress default colours (`#21759b`, `#5472d2` and similar) as brand colours.
- Don't use gold for fills; it appears only as a border, label and the "Contact Us!" text.
- Don't use heavy shadows; the measured shadows are 1px and a 6px-spread bottom shadow.

4. Add the red hero slide with a 79px white uppercase headline, a gold-outlined button and a photograph.
5. Add four icon columns, the circular service carousel (220px circles, red square arrows) and the grey about block.
