## Overview

LottieFiles (https://lottiefiles.com/) is the platform around the Lottie animation format: a library of free and premium animations, Lottie Creator, plugins for Figma and After Effects, and developer tooling. The home page sells motion with motion — its hero, "Great designs come alive with motion!", sits beside a cluster of cheerful, chunky 3D characters, and nearly every section shows an animation, a product screenshot or a customer creative.

The page alternates light and dark: a white hero with a teal "Get started for free" and a white "Get in touch" button; a four-up row of feature chips (Tiny, Motion Tokens, Interactive, Dev ready); a grey logo strip ("Trusted by 16 million+ designers and developers from over 280,000 companies worldwide"); a carousel of customer cards (Walmart, The Washington Post, Canva, CNN); "World's largest customizable animation library" with a search field and a grid of animation thumbnails; a dark "Your motion creation, simplified" product panel with tabs (Lottie Creator, LottieFiles for Figma, LottieFiles for After Effects); "A modern motion workflow built for teams and enterprises" with grey bento cards; "Digital Asset Management"; a black stats bento ("Lottie, the industry standard for motion design": 10x, 40%, 5x, 3x and file-size bars); an integrations cloud of app icons; a bright yellow "Unlock your creative potential with AI" band holding a dark feature grid; "Design teams love LottieFiles" testimonials; a mint-blob "Create, collaborate and ship." banner; and a black footer.

Copy is upbeat and benefit-led, in sentence case.

## Colors

LottieFiles publishes its palette on its brand assets page (https://lottiefiles.com/brand-assets): primary **Mint #00DDB3**, accents **Blue #3A86FF**, **Yellow #FFBF00**, **Red #FF5531**, **Green #06BD1B**, **Orange #FF8000** and **Lime #CAE100**, with black and white as base colours, and the rule "we only use solid colors, and gradients are not allowed." On the measured home page, the UI itself runs on a zinc neutral scale with a darker teal for actions:

- **canvas #ffffff** and **page-largest #f4f4f5** (zinc-100) — white page and the light grey of bento cards and bands; #f4f4f5 is the largest background by DOM area.
- **ink #09090b** (zinc-950) — headlines, nav and button text; it is also **surface-dark**, the fill of the dark panels and footer, with **surface-dark-2 #18181b** for cards inside them.
- **ink-muted #71717b** — the most-read text colour, used for paragraphs; **ink-faint #9f9fa9** on dark backgrounds.
- **primary #019d91** — the measured fill of "Get started for free" and "Sign up", with **on-primary #fafafa** text. It is a deeper teal than the brand Mint; Mint #00ddb3 is measured only as a small fill on the page; in the capture, mint-coloured blobs illustrate the "Create, collaborate and ship." banner.
- **surface-yellow #f0b100** — the AI band.
- **border #e4e4e7** (`--border`, zinc-200) — the outline of secondary buttons; **hairline-on-dark #ffffff80** — card borders on dark sections.

## Typography

The brand page states: "LottieFiles brand font is DM Sans" — DM Sans Medium for headings, DM Sans Regular for body. The site loads it as **DM Sans LF** (400–700), alongside **Inter LF** (400–700) for UI controls.

- **display** — DM Sans 96px / 500 / 99.84px, −4.8px tracking (−0.05em). Section heads at 64px use −2.56px (−0.04em).
- **subheading** — 32px / 500 / 38.4px, −0.96px (product names such as "Lottie Creator").
- **feature-title** — 20px / 500 / 22.8px, −0.2px.
- **body** — DM Sans 20px / 400 / 28px, −0.2px, in #71717b. The 20px/−0.2px pair covers 3,121 characters; 16px is the most frequent size overall.
- **button** — Inter 16px / 500 / 24px; **nav** — Inter 14px / 500 / 20px (14px UI text carries +0.14px tracking).

Both families are free (DM Sans and Inter on Google Fonts); the "LF" names are self-hosted copies.

## Layout

At 1280px the page is 12,409px tall. The header is a single row: logo, Products, Integrations, Tools, Customers, Learn (dropdowns) and Pricing as 36px buttons, then a search field, language selector, "Log in" (outlined) and "Sign up" (teal). The hero is a two-column split. Most sections are a centered heading over a bento grid of rounded cards in two or three columns; the customer and testimonial rows are horizontal carousels.

Spacing: gaps of 8px, 16px, 24px and 12px, with 64px between larger blocks; paddings of 32px (most common), 8px, 24px, 16px and 80px. Section padding reaches 160px and 224px at the largest breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Shadows are minimal and shadcn-style: buttons carry a layered value whose only visible layer is `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05) 0px 1px 2px 0px`; one element uses `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 1px 3px 0px, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 1px 2px -1px`. Depth comes from alternating white, zinc-100, black and yellow bands, and from 3D characters and screenshots inside cards. On dark sections, cards are outlined with 50% white (#ffffff80) instead of shadowed.

## Shapes

16px is the dominant radius (121 elements), followed by 24px (42) and 8px (37); 12px on the hero CTA, 6px on header buttons, and 48px on three large containers. Fully round pills (computed 3.35544e+07px) appear on 17 elements. The CSS includes `--radius-4xl: 2rem`.

## Design System Architecture

LottieFiles publishes brand guidelines at https://lottiefiles.com/brand-assets ("Official LottieFiles Brand Assets: Logos, Colors, & Guidelines"): the logo lockup (symbol left, wordmark right, always full colour), wordmark and logomark rules, a list of don'ts (no stretching, recolouring, rotating, placing over images, drop shadows, or editing the symbol's corners), the Mint primary and six accent colours, DM Sans as the brand font, and Google Drive downloads for logos, product logos and brand imagery.

The web UI is built on Tailwind with shadcn-style tokens visible in the CSS: `--border` and `--color-zinc-200` in oklch, `--color-muted-foreground-inverse`, Tailwind colour ramps (`--color-teal-400`, `--color-pink-500` …), `--radius-4xl`, `--blur-lg` and `--ease-in`. Hover states are recorded in oklch (for example the nav button going from oklch(1 0 0) to oklch(0.967 0.001 286.375)).

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use DM Sans Medium for headings with tight negative tracking and DM Sans Regular for body; Inter for small UI.
- Keep the UI on the zinc scale (#09090b, #71717b, #f4f4f5, #e4e4e7) and use #019d91 for primary actions.
- Use the brand colours as solid fills only, as the brand page requires.
- Build sections as bento grids of 16px–24px rounded cards, alternating light and dark bands.
- Let animations and 3D characters carry the personality.

**Don't**
- Don't use gradients in brand colour areas; the brand page forbids them.
- Don't recolour, rotate, shadow or stretch the logo.
- Don't use heavy shadows; a 5% 1px–2px shadow is the maximum on controls.
- Don't set body text in pure black; it is #71717b on white.

## Responsive Behavior

The extractor's mobile pass at 390px recorded: the h1 at 48px / 500 / 55.2px with −1.92px tracking (96px on desktop), the feature titles at 16px / 500 / 18.56px (20px on desktop), 16px as the dominant size, and 32px as the second-largest heading size. No header links or menu toggle were detected in the mobile pass. The stylesheet's recorded media queries are `max-width 410px`, `max-width 640px` and `min-width 1024px`. Other mobile behaviour was not measured.

4. Lay sections out as bento grids: #f4f4f5 cards with 16px radius on light bands, #18181b cards with #ffffff80 borders on dark bands.
5. Add the #f0b100 AI band and a Mint #00ddb3 illustration accent.
6. Close with the #09090b footer.
