## Overview

Continental AG's home page (title "Home | Continental - Continental AG"; meta description "Our Goal: Healthy Mobility - clean, safe and connected. Our heart beats for this.") is a corporate portal for a tire maker and industrial-technology group. The h1 is "Welcome to Continental", followed by the company statement "Continental is a leading tire manufacturer and industry specialist that develops and produces sustainable, safe and convenient solutions for automotive manufacturers as well as industrial and end customers worldwide." The navigation tree runs through Press, Careers, Investors, Sustainability, Products & Solutions (Tires, ContiTech), Company and Stories, plus a country selector and an EN / DE language switch. The measured page is 1280px wide and 5296px tall.

The capture shows a consent dialog ("Consent to Cookies & Data processing", with four switches and the buttons "Accept all", "Reject all" and "Settings") over a hero slider and the Welcome block. It is a third-party consent overlay set in Arial, not brand UI, and it is not documented here. Beneath it the page reads as a long, calm editorial scroll on white: an "Annual Report" feature (a large warm-orange abstract image titled "Charting New Paths. 2025 Annual Report" with a short caption and a small round "Find out more" link), a "Share Price Chart" pill at the right, a full-width band for "The Continental Tires Garage" ("Two Wheels, One Passion: What Makes a Good Bicycle Tire?") with a video thumbnail and a warm tinted photograph, a "Career at Continental" row of three image cards, a "Sustainability at Continental" band over a coastal-road photograph with three light-gray KPI cards (Greenhouse gas intensity 0.18, Resource use 26.3, Representation of diversity 7.5) whose numerals are in the brand orange, a "Continental in Facts and Figures" row of three story cards, a "Follow us on" row of social links (Facebook, TikTok, Youtube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, RSS) and a light-gray footer with four columns (Contact Us, Quick Access, Websites, Terms & Conditions) and the line "© 2026 Continental AG".

The voice is plain corporate English with product and project names as proper nouns. Section titles are sentence case ("Career at Continental", "Sustainability at Continental"). The brand's single accent is a warm yellow-orange, applied to buttons, the circular "Find out more" icons, the KPI numerals and the logo plate.

## Design System Architecture

No public design-system documentation or brand-guideline page is linked from the home page or from the press "Media Library" page (https://www.continental.com/en/press/media-library/, opened; it lists pictures, videos and footage), so no official design system is documented. The site is a TYPO3 build (compiled stylesheet at `https://cdn.continental.com/typo3temp/assets/compressed/merged-….css`, about 42 KB) on a Bootstrap 4 base: the `:root` block carries Bootstrap's `--primary: #007bff`, `--breakpoint-sm: 767px`, `--breakpoint-md: 1023px`, `--breakpoint-lg: 1279px`, `--breakpoint-xl: 1680px`, `--font-family-sans-serif` and `--font-family-monospace`, plus site header-height variables (`--header-height-xs: 56px`, `--header-height-sticky-lg: 64px`). The brand colors, sizes and radii are hard-coded in component rules rather than exposed as variables. Fonts are served from the site's own `_assets` directory with `font-display: swap`.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:
- Keep the page white with black text, light-gray `#f0f0f0` bands and a warm `#ffa500` for pill buttons, icons and KPI numerals.
- Set text in ContinentalStagSansW: 52.5px / 600 for the main headline, 39.375px / 500 for band titles, 20px / 400 for body with 0.2px tracking.
- Make buttons 48px-tall pills (24px radius) with black labels and a leading chevron.
- Use full-bleed photography with a washed warm tint for storytelling bands.
- Show proof as large orange numerals in light-gray cards.

Don't:
- Don't use Bootstrap's default blue (`#007bff`) or the consent dialog's Arial type as brand elements.
- Don't put white text on the orange: the measured label color is black.
- Don't add heavy shadows; measured shadows are 3px to 5px soft glows.
- Don't round cards and photographs; only controls are round.
- Don't document the cookie consent dialog or its switches as components.
