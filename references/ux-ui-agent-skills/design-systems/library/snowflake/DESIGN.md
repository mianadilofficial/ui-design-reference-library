## Overview

Snowflake is the cloud data platform company, measured at snowflake.com/en (page title "Snowflake AI Data Cloud"). The measured h1 is the two-word display "CODE WORK", which the capture shows as a blue "CODE" and a grey "WORK" separated by a toggle chip, over the line "Bring agentic AI to all your data". The hero copy says "Ask anything with Snowflake CoWork. Build anything with Snowflake CoCo. Welcome to your one-stop shop for everyone in the enterprise." Two pill buttons offer DISCOVER SNOWFLAKE COCO (filled blue) and MEET SNOWFLAKE COWORK (white with a blue label).

The capture, top to bottom: a slim black announcement bar ("Expedition. Free, virtual, Nov 3 to 6. Technical tracks for practitioners, outcomes for leaders." with a "Register now" underlined link), a light blue gradient hero with a dark terminal window (Snowflake CoCo, Cortex Code v1.0.15) beside a lighter product window, a sticky white navigation bar (snowflake logo, Product, Solutions, Why Snowflake, Resources, Developers, Pricing, a globe icon, search, SIGN IN, a blue-outlined CONTACT SALES pill and a filled START FOR FREE pill), an event card (World Tour, "FIND A CITY"), a "PLATFORM" eyebrow with the two-tone heading "SIMPLIFY ENTERPRISE DATA AND AI" (black then blue) and product mock-ups, a three-column value section (EASY, CONNECTED, TRUSTED) with blue line icons, an ebook card ("Turn your AI investment into revenue proof", DOWNLOAD NOW) and the section "THE AI DATA CLOUD".

The voice is enterprise-confident: terse all-caps headlines, then plain explanatory paragraphs.

## Design System Architecture

No public design-system page was found. The research opened https://www.snowflake.com/en/brand-guidelines/, which returned a 404 page, so no brand-guideline claim is made. The shipped CSS exposes a token layer through root variables: --spacing-01 to --spacing-07, --small-border-radius, --small-border-width, --normal-icon-size 24px, --ui-* and --text-* color names, and a --summit-* set for event styling. Tokens in this file come from the measured computed styles.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use #249edc pills with uppercase Texta labels for every primary action.
- Do set display headlines in Texta weight 900 with tight negative tracking.
- Do alternate white, pale-blue and navy sections.
- Do give cards a soft cool-grey layered shadow.
- Don't use #0000ee or Times; they are browser defaults.
- Don't mix more than two colors in a heading (black plus blue is the measured pairing).
- Don't use sharp corners on buttons.
