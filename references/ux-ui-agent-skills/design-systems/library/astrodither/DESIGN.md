## Overview

ASTRODITHER is a single-purpose WebGL piece rather than a conventional website. Its page title is "ASTRODITHER — Robert Borghesi LAB — [SIGNAL. LOST. BEAUTY. FOUND. DIGITAL. CHAOS.]", and the page's own meta description, read from the served HTML, calls it "an audio reactive Threejs /WebGPU experiment by Robert Borghesi on dithering and fluid simulation with TSL". The served document contains two screen-reader-only headings ("AstroDither — Robert Borghesi LAB" as the h1 and the description as an h2), a hidden main-content container and a fixed UI layer, all generated by the Astro framework (the generator meta tag reads Astro v5.13.7) with Tailwind-style utility classes.

What the measurement captured is the entry gate. The viewport is near-black (#080808) and shows one centered label, "[:: CLICK TO ENTER + ENABLE AUDIO ::]", in bold monospace over a semi-transparent black box, above a small cluster of pixel glyphs: squares, outlined squares, plus signs and dots in pink, violet and white arranged in an irregular vertical column. The main experience sits behind that click. The extractor captured only the gate; a recorded Playwright probe (SOURCES.json `_probes`) then clicked through and measured the interface layer that appears afterwards: a top row with the author's "@dghez_ LAB" logo, a hairline divider and a bold LEARN MORE link on the left, "ASTRO" plus a changing word on the right; a bottom row with a running timer and a speed control; and a slide-in information panel. The WebGL scene behind that layer did not render in the headless browser, so its imagery, colours and motion remain not measured.

The tone is cryptic and typographic: square-bracketed phrases, double-colon dividers, and a slogan built from five declarative fragments.

## Colors

The palette measured on screen is two flat values and one overlay.

- **Canvas** `#080808` is the body background and the `theme-color` meta value.
- **Ink** `#f7f8f9` is the root variable `--color-white`; it colors all 174 measured characters. **Primary** is set to the same value because no button, link or accent was detected; the single interactive prompt is white text.
- **Scrim label** `#00000080`, black at 50%, is the fill behind the entry label (9 area units).

The root variables also name `--color-black` as #000. The pink, violet and white glyph colors in the capture are drawn in a canvas element, so they were not extracted as CSS values and no hex is recorded for them. Nothing else in the CSS corpus is a chromatic color: its measured color list is transparent, #000000, #f7f8f9, #00000080, #ffffff and #080808.

## Typography

One family is loaded: Azeret Mono as a variable font covering weights 100 to 900. The root variable `--font-main` is `"Azeret Mono", monospace`, applied as the utility class `font-main` on the html element, and `--bodyFontSize` is 1.2rem.

- Display (the entry label): Azeret Mono 700, 11.9467px / 17.92px, letter-spacing -1.19467px (-10% of the size). On the 390px mobile pass it measured 12px / 18px / -1.2px.
- Body (page text): Azeret Mono 400, 11.9467px / 17.92px. Of the 174 measured characters, 137 are weight 400 and 37 are weight 700.
- UI label (LEARN MORE): Azeret Mono 700, 11.9467px / 17.92px, uppercase, no extra tracking. It is the body size made bold, not a separate step.
- Sidebar text: Azeret Mono 400, 10.24px / 15.36px; sidebar headings the same size at 700, uppercase. This is the utility `text-12` (1.2rem) inside the panel.
- The CSS corpus also lists 19.2px and 16px as declared font sizes (2 uses each), which were not seen on a rendered element.

Every size is fluid. The shipped stylesheet sets `html { font-size: clamp(1px, calc(10 * (100vw / var(--size))), 14px) }` with `--size` 390 below 650px and 1500 from 650px, so one rem equals 10px at a 390px viewport and 8.5333px at 1280px. Body text is `var(--bodyFontSize)`: 1.2rem on small screens, 1.4rem from 650px and 1rem from 2001px. That is why the measured body is the odd value 11.9467px at 1280px (1.4 × 8.5333) and 12px at 390px (1.2 × 10), and why the sidebar is 10.24px at 1280px (1.2 × 8.5333). Reproduce the formula, not the pixel values, or the whole interface will drift at other widths. Letter-spacing on the entry label is the utility `tracking-[-0.1em]`, which is where the -1.19467px comes from.

Font substitutes: Azeret Mono is an open-source Google Font, so no substitution is needed; any monospace with a similar width (such as JetBrains Mono) is a fallback after the declared `monospace`.

## Layout

The page is 800px tall at the 1280px viewport; the entry label is centered horizontally in the capture, with the glyph cluster directly below it. Root variables define `--containerPadding` 2rem, `--gridGutter` 2rem and a base `--spacing` of .1rem, and the UI layer's classes include `site-max`, `fixed`, `w-full h-full` and `pointer-events-none`, so the interface is a fixed overlay above the canvas with no scrolling content. On the gate, the only measured padding is 8.53333px on the label (10px on mobile).

After entry, the probe measured the overlay at 1280px: `.site-max` padding 38.4px top and bottom (`sm:py-45`, 4.5rem) and 34.1333px left and right (`--containerPadding` 4rem). Inside it, two `.main-grid` rows sit at the top and bottom edges, pushed apart by `flex-col justify-between`. Each grid has 12 columns of 85.3281px with a 17.0667px gutter (`--gridGutter` 2rem). The top-left cluster spans columns 1 to 4; the "ASTRO …" title starts at column 9 (x 853.33px); the timer sits at bottom left and the speed control again starts at column 9. Everything is pinned to the four corners of the viewport and the centre is left empty for the scene. The remaining spacing values come from the `--spacing` unit of .1rem multiplied by the utility number (`mx-10`, `mb-5`, `mb-20`, `py-30`), so they scale with the root size.

## Elevation & Depth

No shadows or borders were measured: the corpus lists `box-shadow: none` for 2 elements and no border colors. The label's depth cue is its 50% black fill over the moving field. The default Tailwind transition timing function is declared as cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1), with a default duration of .15s.

The one layered surface after entry is the information panel. Behind its text, a fixed 682.66px square (`w-800 h-800`, 800px at 390px) anchored at the top-left corner applies `backdrop-filter: brightness(0.45) blur(2px)`, darkening and softening the scene rather than covering it with a fill. Both that backdrop and the text are revealed by a radial-gradient mask from the top-left corner: closed, the mask stops at -20% / 0% (nothing shows); open, the probe read 50% / 70% on the backdrop and 80% / 100% on the text. The transition between those endpoints is driven by a `--size` custom property; its timing was not measured. A 1px rule at 50% opacity separates the links from CLOSE. No shadow appears anywhere.

## Shapes

No border radii were measured anywhere on the page and the label has a 0px radius. The visible shapes are the pixel glyphs themselves (filled squares, outlined squares, plus signs and dots), drawn by the WebGL layer and not by CSS.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system, brand guideline or token package was found. The pages opened were the home page (the served HTML and the extraction); the HTML links to a Google Tag script, a favicon set, a web manifest and Astro-built script and stylesheet files, and to no design documentation. The twitter:creator meta tag names the author's handle. The de-facto system is a small set of root variables (`--color-white` #f7f8f9, `--color-black` #000, `--font-main`, `--bodyFontSize` 1.2rem, `--containerPadding` 2rem, `--gridGutter` 2rem, `--spacing` .1rem, `--font-weight-bold` 700) on top of Tailwind's defaults (`--tw-drop-shadow-alpha`, `--default-transition-timing-function`). The shipped stylesheet ([index.pdvcenzl.css](https://astrodither.robertborghesi.is/_astro/index.pdvcenzl.css)) carries the banner "tailwindcss v4.1.13", so the system is Tailwind v4 utilities driven by a handful of custom properties: two colours, one font, a fluid root size, a container padding, a gutter and a .1rem spacing unit. Component styles that are not utilities (`.sidebar-bg`, `.sidebar-info`) are scoped with Astro's `data-astro-cid-*` attributes. There are no semantic colour tokens, no dark/light theme switch and no documented component inventory.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the canvas at #080808 and text at #f7f8f9.
- Do set all text in Azeret Mono, with bold for prompts and tight negative tracking.
- Do write prompts in square brackets with double-colon dividers.
- Don't add color to the CSS layer; chroma in this piece comes from the rendered glyph field.
- Don't invent navigation or footer elements; none were measured.
- Do size everything in rem from the fluid root (`clamp(1px, calc(10 * (100vw / var(--size))), 14px)`) so the interface scales with the viewport.
- Do pin interface text to the four corners on the 12-column grid and leave the centre to the scene.
- Do use hairline dividers in `#f7f8f9` (0.84px at 1280px, 1px at 390px), and a 50% opacity rule inside the panel.
- Do darken the scene behind overlays with `backdrop-filter: brightness(0.45) blur(2px)` instead of an opaque panel.
- Don't add rounded corners, shadows or filled buttons; every interactive element measured is plain text.
- Don't describe or reproduce the WebGL scene from this document; it was not measured.

## Responsive Behavior

A 390px mobile pass measured the entry label at 12px / 18px with -1.2px letter-spacing, padding 10px, 264px wide and 38px tall. After entry, the probe at 390px measured: overlay padding 22px top and bottom and 20px left and right; a 6-column grid with a 20px gutter (the stylesheet switches to 12 columns at 650px); the header and timer text at 12px / 18px; the information panel 350px wide with 30px vertical padding, its text at 12px / 18px, heading margins 5px and paragraph margins 20px, the label column 100px; the divider 1px wide with 10px side margins; and the backdrop square at 800px. The stylesheet's breakpoints are 650px (root `--size`, container padding, 12 columns, `sm:` utilities) and 2001px (body size 1rem, `3xl:` utilities); the 2001px state was not measured. Orientation changes and touch behaviour: not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with a #080808 field and one centered white label in bold Azeret Mono 11.9467px.
2. Put a 50% black box behind the label with 8.53333px padding and square corners.
3. Draw a column of small square, outlined-square, plus and dot glyphs under it.
4. After entry, hide the label and show a fixed overlay with 4rem side and 4.5rem vertical padding on a 12-column grid: logo, hairline and bold LEARN MORE top left; ASTRO + word at column 9; timer bottom left; speed control at column 9.
5. Build the information panel as text over a `brightness(0.45) blur(2px)` backdrop revealed by a top-left radial mask, at 1.2rem.
6. Run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=astrodither`.
