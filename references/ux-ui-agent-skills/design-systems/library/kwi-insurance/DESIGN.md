## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use navy #0b233f for buttons, the footer and feature panels.
- Do keep headings and buttons in uppercase Kaisei Opti with 1px letter-spacing on the display size (45px).
- Do keep corners square (0px).
- Do use #e5f2ff for light section backgrounds.
- Do not use the Bootstrap blue #337ab7 as a brand colour.
- Do not describe the back-to-top button as brand UI.
- Do not add rounded pill buttons.
- Do not add warm accent fills beyond the red dot in the logo.

5. Use dark navy panels with white pictograms for product lists.
