# sweetgreen

## Overview

sweetgreen.com reads like a farmers' market chalkboard rebuilt in a quiet, editorial grid. The page
background is a warm cream (`#f4f3e7`) instead of white, and nearly every character of text is a single
near-black green (`#0e150e`). Two brand colors carry all of the action: a deep forest green
(`#00473c`, which is also the color of the SVG wordmark) and an electric lime (`#e6ff55`). The lime is used
almost exclusively as the fill of pill-shaped calls to action and product badges, so the eye jumps to it
over full-bleed photography.

The homepage capture opens with a slim utility row ("Enable Accessibility"), a 72px cream header with
uppercase nav links split left and right of a centered lowercase `sweetgreen` wordmark, and a dark-green
`ORDER` pill at the far right. Below sits a full-bleed hero carousel: a top-down photo of a salad in its
signature bowl, a small uppercase kicker ("MOLLY'S PICKLE CAESAR SAL"), an 80px SweetSans headline set
tight on 68px leading ("Pickle-Forward") in cream, and a lime `ORDER NOW` pill.

Under the hero, a menu carousel is introduced by three category tabs set in the serif Grenette at a light
200 weight ("Salads / Warm Bowls / Sides") — the only place the serif appears — with the active tab in ink
and the rest in a muted grey (`#8c8c82`), marked by a small dot. Product cards are 384px square
top-down food photos with 20px corners, an uppercase 24px title, an ingredient list at 18px, an allergen
line and an "Order now →" text link.

Further down, sections switch background to a pale sage (`#d8e5d6`, the site's `.bg-cucumber` theme) and a
tan (`#e8dcc6`), with very large 70px SweetSans headlines ("All the ways we keep your workplace working")
beside tall photography with rounded corners.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cream canvas `#f4f3e7`, never pure white for the page; white `#ffffff` appears only on overlays.
- One ink color `#0e150e` for almost all text (3100 of ~3260 weighted characters).
- Lime `#e6ff55` pills with forest-green `#00473c` labels as the primary CTA; hover inverts to forest fill with cream text.
- Everything typographic is weight 400 except emphasized feature links (700) and the Grenette tabs (200).
- Display type is tight: 80px on 68px, 70px on 59.5px — line-height below 1.
- Pills (1000px) for buttons, 20px for images and badges, 10px for inputs, 24px for floating panels.

## Colors

### Brand & Accent
- **Forest** (`#00473c`, `primary`): wordmark color, nav `ORDER` button fill, `.btn--primary` fill in the
  site CSS, and the hover fill of every CTA. Labels on lime pills are set in this color.
- **Lime** (`#e6ff55`, `lime`): primary CTA fill (`ORDER NOW`, `FIND OUT MORE`, `LET US KNOW`), product
  badge fill ("Online only"), carousel arrows, and the label color on the forest nav `ORDER` pill.
- **Spinach** (`#2d6b52`, `spinach`): hover fill declared for `.btn--primary`, `.btn--light` and the
  translucent button variants in the site CSS.

### Surface
- **Canvas** (`#f4f3e7`): `--body-color` / `--color-body`; the body, header and footer background.
- **Cucumber** (`#d8e5d6`): `--em-color` and the `.bg-cucumber` section theme; the largest-area background
  measured on the homepage (the "sweetgreen for work" band).
- **Tan** (`#e8dcc6`): second-largest section background on the homepage.
- **White** (`#ffffff`): only on floating overlays (the consent panel) and `.btn--light` variants.

### Text
- **Ink** (`#0e150e`): `--text-color` / `--color-text`; body, headings, nav, links, input text and borders.
- **Ink muted** (`#8c8c82`): inactive category tabs.
- **On-primary** (`#f4f3e7`): cream text on forest fills and on hero photography.
- **Placeholder** (`#b2b2b2`): `--placeholder-txt`.

### Semantic / State
- **Focus ring** (`#484d48`): `.btn--base:focus-visible { outline: 1px solid #484d48; outline-offset: 2px }`.
- **Outline** (`#e59700`): `--outline-color: rgb(229, 151, 0)` declared on `:root`.
- `--footer-color: #A72700` and `--footer-text-color: #F4F3E7` are declared on `:root`, but no rendered
  element on the measured homepage carried `#a72700`, so it is not a token here.
- No error/success palette is published or visible on the homepage; none is declared.

## Typography

### Font Family
- **SweetSans** — the display cut, used for hero and section headlines at 80px, 70px and 40px. Served as
  `SweetSans` from `/static/fonts/SweetSans-Regular.woff2` (regular and bold faces declared).
- **SweetSansText** — the text cut, used for everything else (body, nav, buttons, card titles, inputs).
  Served as `SweetSansText` (regular + bold). Per [MCKL Type](https://mckltype.com/custom/sweetgreen), the
  family was made with Collins and includes "a dedicated text version optimized for smaller sizes."
- **Grenette** — a serif by Colophon Foundry, used at weight 200 only for the menu category tabs, with
  fallback `Georgia, Cambria, Palatino, 'Times New Roman', Times, serif`.

### Principles
- A single regular weight does almost all the work; hierarchy comes from size and case, not boldness.
- Uppercase is reserved for UI chrome and product names (nav, buttons, card titles, kickers, badges).
  Headlines are sentence case.
- Display line-heights are below 1 (68/80 = 0.85, 59.5/70 = 0.85); body copy sits at 1.25 (`--line-height-body: 1.25`, 18px on 22.5px).
- Product names get positive tracking (0.72px at 24px); the Grenette tabs get strong negative tracking (-2.25px at 48px).
- The site's own scale is fluid: `--font-size-h1` interpolates `calc(40px + 40 * (100vw - 481px) / 543)`
  between breakpoints and lands at `5rem` (80px) on desktop; `--font-size-h2` lands at `4.375rem` (70px).

### Note on Font Substitutes
SweetSans, SweetSansText and the Grenette build served here are licensed brand fonts. MCKL Type states
SweetSans was built on its [Owners](https://mckltype.com/custom/sweetgreen) typeface as a foundation, so
Owners (commercial, MCKL) is the documented relative. Grenette is commercially available from Colophon
Foundry ([MyFonts listing](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/grenette-variable-font-colophon-foundry/)).
When neither is licensed, keep the site's own fallback stacks (system-ui sans / Georgia serif) rather than
picking a lookalike.

## Layout

### Spacing System
Measured gaps on the homepage cluster at 8px (dominant), 5px, 15px, 10px, 24px, 40px and 43px; paddings
at 40px (dominant), 16px, 60px, 43px, 12px, 30px and 24px. Tokens: `xxs` 5px, `xs` 8px, `sm` 16px, `md`
24px, `lg` 40px, `xl` 60px, `gutter-desktop` 40px, `section-gap` 43px.

The site's own spacing variables (from `screen.css`): `--spacing-xxs: 0.4375rem`, `--spacing-s: 1.25rem`,
`--spacing-m: 1.875rem`, `--spacing: 1.4375rem` → `2.6875rem` (43px) on wider screens, `--spacing-l:
2.5rem` → `4.375rem`, `--spacing-xl: 3.375rem` → `6.25rem`, `--h-spacing: 1rem` → `2.5rem` (40px page gutter).

### Grid & Container
- `--grid: repeat(6, minmax(10px, 1fr))` on small screens, `repeat(12, minmax(10px, 1fr))` from the first
  desktop breakpoint, with `--gutter: 1rem` → `1.5rem`.
- `--maxwidth: 90rem` (1440px); `--maxwidth-narrow: 40.5625rem` for text columns.
- Header height `--site-header-height: 3rem` on mobile, `4.5rem` on desktop; measured header 72px.
- The menu carousel spans 1200px at a 1280px viewport with three 384px cards visible plus partial peeks.

### Whitespace Philosophy
Generous, even breathing room: 40px page gutters, large section padding, and photography that runs edge to
edge. Text blocks are left-aligned in narrow columns next to big images; nothing is boxed in cards with
borders or shadows.

## Elevation & Depth

The brand surface is flat. No brand component on the homepage carries a box shadow; depth comes from
photography and color bands.

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | none | Page, sections, cards, buttons |
| Overlay | `rgba(14, 21, 14, 0.4) 3px 3px 32px -10px`, 24px radius, white | Floating consent panel (OneTrust) |

The only measured shadow belongs to the third-party consent panel; treat it as an overlay pattern, not a
card style.

## Design System Architecture

sweetgreen publishes **no public design system**, component library, token package or brand portal. Searches
recorded in `SOURCES.json` ("sweetgreen design system github npm tokens", "sweetgreen brand guidelines press
kit logo colors", "sweetgreen newsroom press media assets", "wearecollins.com sweetgreen", "sweetgreen brand
identity Sweet Sans typeface Grenette design") returned only third-party aggregators. What follows is the
de-facto system read from the site's own stylesheet, [screen.css](https://www.sweetgreen.com/static/css/screen.css),
plus the type foundries' pages.

- **Token architecture** — a flat CSS custom-property layer on `:root`, no primitive/semantic split. Color
  roles are semantic names: `--body-color`, `--text-color`, `--em-color`, `--border-color`, `--footer-color`,
  `--button-color`, `--button-hover-color`, `--button-hover-text-color`, `--outline-color`,
  `--placeholder-txt`. Type: `--font-size-h1`…`--font-size-h6`, `--font-size-b1`, `--font-size-card`,
  `--line-height-body: 1.25`. Space: `--spacing-xxs`, `--spacing-s`, `--spacing-m`, `--spacing`,
  `--spacing-l`, `--spacing-xl`, `--gutter`, `--h-spacing`, `--maxwidth`.
- **Theming** — `color-scheme: light` only; no dark mode is declared. Section themes are classes that
  re-assign the same variables, e.g. `.bg-cucumber { --body-color: #D8E5D6; --text-color: #00473C }`.
  Button variants re-assign `--button-*` variables (`.btn--primary`, `.btn--light`, `.btn--light--spinach`,
  `.btn--spinach--lighter`, `.btn--cucumber--almost-transparent`). Variant names use produce words
  (cucumber, spinach) in the CSS.
- **Type & spacing systems** — fluid type: each `--font-size-*` interpolates with
  `calc(min + delta * (100vw - 481px) / 543)` between 481px and 1024px, then fixes at desktop values
  (`--font-size-h1: 5rem`, `--font-size-h2: 4.375rem`, `--font-size-h3: 3rem`, `--font-size-b1: 1.25rem`).
  Spacing follows the same pattern (`--spacing-l: calc(40px + 30 * (100vw - 481px) / 543)`). Grid is 6
  columns mobile / 12 columns desktop.
- **Component model** — BEM-style class names: `.btn`, `.btn--primary`, `.btn--base--small`,
  `.menu-item__image`, `.menu-item__badge--online-exclusive`, `.hero__heading`, `.collection__heading`,
  `.field-oneline__input`, `.top-bar__order-btn`, `.site-footer__contact-us`. Component CSS is split into
  per-component files (`componentCollection.css`, `component2Up.css`, `componentSimpleCard.css`, `footer.css`).
  Inventory observed on the homepage: header/top-bar, hero carousel, menu carousel with tabs, menu item,
  badge, collection (headline + links + image), 2-up, simple card, newsletter field, footer.
- **Accessibility** — `.btn--base:focus-visible` outline 1px `#484d48` with 2px offset; global
  `--outline-color: rgb(229, 151, 0)`; a "skip to content" link and an "Enable Accessibility" toggle
  (class `UsableNetAssistive`) at the top; `prefers-reduced-motion` media queries in CSS. Ink `#0e150e` on
  canvas `#f4f3e7` is near-maximal contrast. No published contrast or target-size policy was found.
- **Delivery** — static CSS served from `/static/css/` with timestamped query strings; fonts self-hosted from
  `/static/fonts/` as woff2. Typefaces: SweetSans by [MCKL Type](https://mckltype.com/custom/sweetgreen)
  with Collins; Grenette by Colophon Foundry
  ([MyFonts](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/grenette-variable-font-colophon-foundry/)). No npm package,
  Figma kit or versioning is public.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use cream `#f4f3e7` as the page and ink `#0e150e` for text.
- Make the main CTA a lime `#e6ff55` pill with a forest `#00473c` uppercase label, padding `15px 60px 13px`.
- Invert CTAs on hover to forest `#00473c` fill with cream `#f4f3e7` text.
- Set headlines in SweetSans 400 at 80px/68px or 70px/59.5px, sentence case.
- Set product names in uppercase SweetSansText 24px with 0.72px tracking.
- Use Grenette 200 only for the category-tab style, with -2.25px tracking at 48px.
- Round product photos 20px and show food top-down.
- Alternate section bands in `#d8e5d6` and `#e8dcc6`.

### Don't
- Don't use pure white as the page background.
- Don't bold headlines; the system runs at weight 400.
- Don't add drop shadows to cards or buttons.
- Don't use lime for text on cream — it is a fill color (it appears as text only on the forest pill).
- Don't use square-cornered buttons; every `.btn` is a 1000px pill.
- Don't introduce extra accent hues; the palette is forest, lime, spinach, cream, sage and tan.

## Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints
From `screen.css` media queries: `30.0625em` (481px), `36.875em` (590px), `48.0625em` (769px), `64em`
(1024px), `80em` (1280px), `90em` (1440px); max-width queries at `30em`, `48em`, `63.9375em`, `79.9375em`.

### Type at 390px (measured)
- Hero headline 80px → 40px (line-height 34px); section headline 70px → 40px (34px).
- Category tabs 48px → 20px (24px, tracking normal).
- Card title 24px → 20px (24px, 0.6px tracking).
- Body 18px → 16px (20px).
- CTA unchanged: 46px tall, `15px 60px 13px`, pill.

### Touch Targets
Primary pill 46px tall; nav ORDER pill 30px tall; carousel arrows 40px; `.btn--base` sizes 32/40/48px.

### Collapsing Strategy
Grid moves from 12 to 6

### Image Behavior
Hero photography stays full-bleed; menu cards remain a horizontal carousel with partial peeks.
