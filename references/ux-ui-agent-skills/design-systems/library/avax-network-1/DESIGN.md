## Overview

The queued URL, https://www.avax.network, answers with a 301 to `https://www.avalanche.com/` (page title "Technology Built for Business | Avalanche"), so the measured site is the same brand under its current domain: **Avalanche**. The page is 7055px tall at 1280px wide with 742 visible elements. It is a dark, enterprise-facing marketing page: a charcoal canvas, a single hot red, enormous uppercase heavy type, monochrome photography, and rounded dark panels that slide horizontally.

From the top, a 59px charcoal header holds the red "avalanche" logo, "Industries", "Build", "Ecosystem", "About". The hero is a monochrome photograph of a businessman with his feet on a desk, perched on a rocky mountain in mist; over it, the red uppercase headline "TECHNOLOGY BUILT FOR BUSINESS" at 109.658px, a grey two-line lead ("From global payments to tokenized assets to consumer experiences, Avalanche carries the workloads real business depends on. Live today, built for whatever comes next."), two square buttons ("Get In Touch" in dark with a red arrow, "Start Building" in red with an arrow), and a column on the right with a news card for the UAE identity-system story (red "Enterprises" tag, "9.14.2026 / By Avalanche / 7 Minute Read", uppercase headline, "Read More"). A strip of partner logos in white runs under it. The next section is a rounded dark panel numbered "01" with the headline "PREDICTABLE PERFORMANCE AT SCALE" (the last words in red) beside a grey paragraph, an image of a stone facade breaking into dust, and neighboring panels peeking in at the right; below is a full-width red rounded panel. Later sections, read from the page, include industry and audience cards, an email sign-up with a PDF, a media section ("Real stories Real impact"), a developer call to action, news, and a "Get In Touch" contact form with use-case chips (Payments, Capital Markets, Settlement, Real World Assets, Private Credit, Fan Engagement, Ticketing, Governments). The voice is confident, declarative and fragmentary: "Live today, built for whatever comes next."

## Design System Architecture

Avalanche's brand assets are published through an "Avalanche Brand & Media Kit" linked from https://www.avalanche.com/about/press, which points to https://support.avax.network/en/articles/4132288-avalanche-brand-assets; that article is an index of Google Drive folders (brand guidelines, brand assets, leadership photos) that could not be opened, so no values are taken from it. The trademark policy at https://www.avalanche.com/legal/trademark-usage-policy gives text-use rules and no colors. There is no public UI design system. The shipped stylesheet is a Tailwind-style variable set (97 `:root` properties): `--color-new-neutral-100` to `-950` (`#e1dfe9`, `#d8dedf`, `#c6cccd`, `#a2afb2`, `#888c8d`, `#6d7071`, `#383838`, `#212020`, `#1d1d1d`, `#131314`), `--color-new-red` `#e22b35` with `-100` `#fbdbdb` and `-600` `#b72129`, older `--color-neutral-*` and `--color-red-*` palettes, semantic `--color-success` `#4ade80` and `--color-error` `#dc2626`, `--space-*` and `--radius-*` clamp scales, easing functions (`--ease-out-quart` cubic-bezier(.165,.84,.44,1), `--ease-in-out-quart` cubic-bezier(.8,0,.2,1)) and `--font-aeonik`. The footer credits "Wonder Makers" as the site's creator.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do set all headings in Aeonik 900, uppercase, with tight leading; use red `#e22b35` for the single keyword or last line.
- Do keep the charcoal ladder (`#0a0a0a`, `#1d1d1d`, `#212020`) and use `#c6cccd` for body text.
- Do keep buttons and fields square with a 48px height; keep panels at the 20.57px radius.
- Do use black-and-white or monochrome photography and let red carry the only color.
- Don't add shadows; none were measured.
- Don't make buttons black text on red at rest; the resting label is white.
- Don't introduce a second accent; the older `#ff394a` red is declared but unmeasured.
- Don't round the square controls.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with fields: `#0a0a0a` page, `#1d1d1d` header and panels, `#000000` footer, `#e22b35` for the red panel and buttons.
2. Set type in Aeonik: 900 uppercase for display (109.658px), headline (42.5536px) and heads; 400 for lead (15.1456px) and body (13.1328px).
3. Build buttons 48px tall, square, red with white labels and a trailing arrow; hover to black label and black border.
4. Round only the large panels, at 20.57px.
5. Re-measure with `node web/scripts/extract-tokens.mjs --slug=avax-network-1--<page> --url=https://www.avalanche.com/<page> --force --no-motion` before adding pages; then `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=avax-network-1`.
