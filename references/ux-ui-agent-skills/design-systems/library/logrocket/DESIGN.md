# LogRocket — logrocket.com

## Overview

LogRocket's home page (https://logrocket.com, title "Session Replay | Product Analytics | Error Tracking | LogRocket") sells AI-assisted session replay and product analytics. Its look is a white page bracketed by rich purple: a violet hero at the top, a purple customer-story band in the middle and a purple closing CTA at the bottom, each sprinkled with small white four-point stars and soft light gradients. Between those bands the page is white and airy, with near-black text and pale lilac panels.

The hero sits on a deep purple gradient (a thin `#430a6d` strip runs above it). The white header carries the rocket logo, Products, Solutions, Pricing, Customers, Blog, Docs, a translucent **Log in** pill and a white **Get a demo** pill. The left column holds the 62px vinila headline "AI session replay that catches issues before your users do", a short paragraph and two pills (translucent **Start for free**, white **Get a demo**); the right column shows a product UI panel (Issues, Analytics, Session Replay). "Trusted by 3,000+ customers" logos sit at the bottom of the hero. Below: "Meet Galileo, the AI that watches every session for you" with a purple sparkle eyebrow and an "Explore Galileo AI ⊙" link, a lilac "Rated #1 for session replay and analytics" panel with G2 badges and a quadrant chart, a product tab strip (Product Analytics, Issues, Session Replay, UX Analytics, Error Tracking, Performance Monitoring — active tab black), feature rows with purple sparkle bullets, a purple band of three customer-story cards, a "Get started in minutes" block with a Mobile/Web segmented control, an integrations section on warm `#f9f6f5`, an "Outlooks for every insight" blog row, a purple "Create seamless, issue-free user experiences" CTA with two dark purple cards, and a five-column footer.

- **Hairlines** — `#17141b33` and `#17141b1a` borders on cards and tabs.

## Typography

Two families, both variable (100–900):

- **vinila** for headlines, buttons and many labels: hero 62px / 60.76px at 400 with -1.86px tracking; section headlines 52px at -2.08px and 44px at -0.88px; 24px weight 500 feature titles ("Product Analytics"); 15px / 22.5px for button labels; weights 350 and 450 also appear.
- **Inter** for running text and UI: body 18px / 26.1px at 400; tabs 16px / 22.4px at 500; nav 16px / 24px; tags 13px / 17.55px at 500 with -0.13px.

Measured sizes by character count: 20px, 15px, 24px, 16px, 18px, 13px, 14px, 52px, 44px, 62px, 32px. The CSS also defines `--font-caveat` for a handwritten accent. vinila is a commercial typeface; when unavailable, the stack falls back to `ui-sans-serif, system-ui`. Inter is open-source.

## Layout

At 1280px the hero is 851px tall with content from x = 8 to x = 1272 in two columns (text left, product panel right). The main white band runs 6,038px with up to 6 columns; the purple customer band is built from stacked gradient strips (`#774cbd`, `#8052c3`, `#8958c9`, `#8053c3`, `#774bbd`), followed by white, `#f9f6f5`, white and a media CTA band. Centred section heads alternate with left-aligned two-column feature rows. Gaps cluster at 12px, 8px and 16px with 78px between columns; paddings at 8px, 16px and 32px, with 72px, 88px and 96px section padding and 72px / 40px footer padding.

## Elevation & Depth

No drop shadows were measured. Pills and cards are outlined with inset 1px rings: white (`rgb(255, 255, 255) 0px 0px 0px 1px inset`) on white pills, `rgba(234, 229, 255, 0.5)` on translucent pills, `rgba(241, 236, 246, 0.25)` on dark cards and `rgba(23, 20, 27, 0.1)` on light tabs. Depth comes from the purple gradients, glows and star sprinkles, and from product UI panels floating on lilac.

## Shapes

All buttons are **pills** (9999px, the most frequent radius). Cards use **12px** (`rounded.2xl`) with **16px** and **20px** on larger panels; tags and small chips **4px**–**6px**; the segmented tab and the hero's text links have square corners (0px). Bullets are purple four-point sparkles and "Learn more" links end with a black circle containing an arrow.

## Components

- **Primary pill** — white, `#17141b` vinila 15px text, 1px white inset ring, padding 10px 24px, 43px tall ("Get a demo", "View all customer stories").
- **Glass pill** — `#ffffff4d` with white text and a lavender inset ring ("Start for free", "Log in"); hover switches the label to `#17141b`.
- **Segmented tabs** — a pill-shaped track with a black `#17141b` active segment and white Inter 16px weight 500 label, 42px tall.
- **Product tab strip** — rounded bar of labels with a black active tab.
- **Customer card** — white, 12px radius, 8px padding, portrait on purple, small grey tags, 20px title and "Read full case study ⊙".
- **CTA card** — `#430a6d` dark purple card with icon, title, paragraph and pill.
- **Eyebrow** — small black label with a purple sparkle icon ("Galileo AI", "Integrations").

## Design System Architecture

No public design system or brand guideline was found: https://logrocket.com/brand and https://logrocket.com/press both returned 404, and the brief limits research to official pages. The shipped site is a Tailwind build (`--tw-*` root variables) with font variables `--font-vinila`, `--font-inter` and `--font-caveat`, and a Swiper carousel (`--swiper-theme-color: #007aff`, a library default, not a brand color). The de-facto system is the measured set above: purple gradient bands, `#17141b` ink at stepped alphas (100%, 75%, 50%, 40%), vinila + Inter, pill buttons and inset-ring outlines.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Keep content on white with `#17141b` text, and frame the page with purple `#764abc` gradient bands.
- Use white pills on purple and glass pills as secondary actions.
- Set headlines in vinila at 400 with tight negative tracking; body in Inter.
- Use `#17141b` alpha steps for text hierarchy instead of separate greys.
- Decorate purple bands with small white stars and soft glows.

**Don't**
- Don't use square buttons; only tabs and inline links are square.
- Don't add drop shadows; use 1px inset rings.
- Don't use the Swiper default blue as a brand color.
- Don't set long body text in vinila.

## Responsive Behavior

The 390px mobile pass recorded the hero headline at 37.1752px / 36.4317px (tracking stays -1.86px) and centred, one visible header link with a menu toggle labelled "Open main menu", and a 766px hero band with content from x = 16 to x = 374. Mobile font sizes are fluid values such as 16.1814px, 20.1814px, 16.0899px, 14.0449px, 32.9036px and 32.5425px. The stylesheet declares breakpoints at max-width 480px and min-width 481px, 640px, 768px, 880px, 1024px and 1280px. Other reflow behavior was not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with a white canvas, `#17141b` ink and 75% ink for paragraphs.
2. Open with a `#764abc` purple gradient hero with stars, a 62px vinila headline at -1.86px, and white + glass pills.
3. Alternate white sections with lilac `#f1ecf6` panels and one warm `#f9f6f5` band.
4. Use 12px-radius white cards with inset hairlines and small Inter tags.
5. Close with a purple band and `#430a6d` CTA cards.
6. Measure anything not listed here before adding it.
