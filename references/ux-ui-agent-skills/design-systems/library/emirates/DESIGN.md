## Overview

Emirates.com (US English homepage, measured at https://www.emirates.com/us/english/) is a transactional airline site that reads as two layers. The first layer is photographic and emotional: a full-bleed hero photograph (on the day of capture, the honey-coloured leather of the new Premium Economy cabin) carries white display type, and further down a dusk skyline of Dubai carries the next campaign headline. The second layer is utilitarian and quiet: a light-grey `{colors.canvas}` (#f6f6f6) page on which white `{colors.surface}` (#ffffff) cards sit with a faint 3px corner and a soft drop shadow — the flight-search widget, a "Complimentary date change" notice, an icon strip of ancillary products, and a grid of six destination cards.

Colour is almost entirely neutral. Charcoal `{colors.text}` (#333333, the site's own `--text-color` variable) is the UI text and the fill of both the top navigation bar and the footer. The only saturated colour that does real work is Emirates red `{colors.primary}` (#d71921): the filled "Continue" and "Learn more" buttons, the 3px underline of the active search tab, text links such as "Learn more" and "Advanced search", and the red rectangle that holds the Emirates logo, hanging out of the nav bar over the hero.

Type is a three-family system. **Heroic-Medium**, a condensed face, is used for the giant uppercase hero line ("PREMIUM ECONOMY") at 120px with -5px tracking. **Emirates-Bold**, the airline's own display face (loaded as `emirates-bold` / `Emirates-Bold`), sets the hero kicker lines in a spaced italic-looking style, the 50px section titles ("Three extraordinary experiences in Dubai on us") and the 24px city names on the destination cards. Everything else — tabs, labels, inputs, body copy, prices, footer links — is **HelveticaNeue / HelveticaNeue-Light**, running from the hairline 200 weight up to 700.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light-grey `{colors.canvas}` (#f6f6f6) page; white `{colors.surface}` cards with `{rounded.sm}` (3px) corners and `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 2px 4px 2px` shadows.
- Charcoal `{colors.nav}` (#333333) nav bar, 55px tall, with the red logo block overlapping the hero below it.
- One accent: `{colors.primary}` (#d71921) — filled CTA, active-tab inset underline, inline links.
- Hover is expressed as a shadow, not a colour change: every measured button and card gains `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5) 0px 0px 6px 0px` on hover while its background stays put.
- Condensed uppercase Heroic-Medium hero type at 120px / -5px, framed by Emirates-Bold kicker lines at 30px / 2px tracking.
- Very light HelveticaNeue-Light (weight 200) for inactive tabs, field labels and fare small print — a deliberately whispery UI voice next to bold 700 actions.
- Photography is the brand: warm cabin interiors, golden-hour city skylines, saturated destination postcards.

### Principles
- **Weight contrast, not size contrast.** In the search widget the selected tab is HelveticaNeue 700 and its siblings are HelveticaNeue-Light 200 at the same 16px. The Flight / Vacations toggle does the same at 14px (700 vs 200).
- **Spaced caps for labels.** The country eyebrow ("I N D I A") is 10px, 700, uppercase, with 4px tracking; the capture shows the same spaced caps on "LIMITED-TIME OFFER" above the Dubai section.
- **Display faces only on imagery or as titles.** Heroic-Medium and Emirates-Bold appear on photographs, as section titles and as city names; UI chrome never uses them.
- The hero `<h1>` element itself computes to the browser default (Times 20px/700); the visible text lives in child spans. Style the spans, not the heading element.

### Note on Font Substitutes
Emirates-Bold and the other `Emirates*` faces are served from Emirates' own stylesheets and are not publicly distributed; Heroic-Medium is also a served webfont, not a system font. When they are unavailable, use the fallbacks the site itself declares, in order: for Heroic-Medium `"Arial Narrow Bold", "Franklin Gothic Medium", "Franklin Gothic", "Arial Narrow"`; for Emirates-Bold `Helvetica, Arial, "Lucida Grande", sans-serif`. HelveticaNeue-Light falls back to `"Helvetica Neue Light", "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial`.

## Layout

### Grid & Container
- The measured page is 1,280px wide and 4,684px tall.
- Main content modules sit in a 1,100px column ("product-block" 1,100px wide; the search widget 1,100px with an inner 1,082px grid row). The "featured-fares g-site-container" section is 1,200px with `0px 50px 50px` padding, giving the same 1,100px content width.
- Destination cards are a 3-column grid of 333px-wide cards (277px tall), two rows deep.
- The ancillary icon strip ("Hotels", "Car rentals", "Tours & activities" …) is 8 equal white cells separated by 1px dividers.

### Spacing System
- Measured paddings cluster on a 5px rhythm: `{spacing.xxs}` 5px, `{spacing.xs}` 10px (most frequent), `{spacing.sm}` 15px, `{spacing.md}` 20px (second most frequent), `{spacing.lg}` 25px, `{spacing.xl}` 30px, `{spacing.xxl}` 50px (section bottom padding).
- The most frequent flex/grid gap is 15px.
- Odd values also occur in components (11px on the hero button, 17px 19px on inputs, 21px and 29px in the widget) — keep them on the components that own them; do not generalise them into the scale.

### Section Rhythm
From the capture, top to bottom: advisory strip (yellow) → charcoal nav (55px) → hero photograph (~440px at 960px capture width) → search widget overlapping the bottom of the hero → content card → icon strip → black Skywards band → "Featured destinations from" with a city dropdown → 2×3 destination grid → a row with two text links flanking an outlined "See more fares" button → full-bleed Dubai dusk section with a 50px Emirates-Bold title.

Depth is shallow: one short, low-opacity card shadow and one dark hover glow. There are no large blurred "floating" shadows, no glassmorphism and no gradients on UI elements; the drama is all in the photographs.

## Shapes

- **Corner radius:** `{rounded.sm}` 3px is the house radius — buttons, inputs, cards (25 occurrences, the most frequent). Content cards with an image on top use `3px 3px 0px 0px` on the image; bottom pieces use `0px 0px 3px 3px`.
- `{rounded.xs}` 2px on the accessibility skip links.
- `{rounded.md}` 8px appears on 14 rendered elements (secondary rounded surfaces).
- `{rounded.circle}` 50% and `{rounded.round}` 100px for circular controls such as the advisory carousel's previous/next and pause buttons, and avatar-like icons.
- No skewed, angled or clipped buttons. The Skywards tier medals (blue, silver, gold, platinum) are circular artwork in an image, not CSS shapes.
- The logo block is a plain red rectangle that extends below the nav bar into the hero.

## Design System Architecture

**Emirates (the airline) publishes no public design system.** I found no component library, token package, Figma community kit or downloadable brand-guideline document. Searches run (recorded in `SOURCES.json`): "Emirates airline brand guidelines typeface Emirates font official"; "Emirates design system site:emirates.com OR site:github.com/emirates"; "Emirates brand identity logo usage media centre press kit emirates.com"; "npm @emirates design tokens component library"; an npm registry search of the `@emirates` scope (0 packages); and a GitHub repository search for "emirates design system", which returned only unrelated UAE-government and third-party repositories. The one official brand document I opened is the media-centre release [Emirates invites travellers to "Fly Better"](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-invites-travellers-to-fly-better) (25 October 2018). It introduces the "Fly Better" brand promise, which appears as the script mark at the top right of the hero, and says the campaign was created in-house by Emirates' head of brand. It publishes no colour, type or component specifications.

What follows is the **de-facto system measurable on the live site** ([emirates.com/us/english](https://www.emirates.com/us/english/)), not a documented one.

### Token architecture (observed)
- **One flat tier of CSS custom properties** on `:root`, with no primitive → semantic split. The full measured set is: `--white-color: #FFFFFF`, `--red-color: #c60c30`, `--gray-color: #666666`, `--text-color: #333333`, `--font-ek-bold: EmiratesSB-Bold, helvetica, arial, sans-serif`, `--font-helvetica: HelveticaNeue,Helvetica Neue,…`, `--font-helvetica-light: HelveticaNeue-Light,Helvetica Neue Light,…`.
- Most colours are hard-coded in component CSS rather than read from variables. For example, the rendered CTA red #d71921 (77 occurrences in the stylesheets) is not a variable, while the variable `--red-color` holds a different red, #c60c30.

### Naming convention (observed)
- **BEM-style component classes:** `city-card__city`, `city-card__country`, `city-card__date`, `city-card__price`, `featured-fares__destination__heading`, `widget__tab__label`, `market-promo-item__title--full-bleed`, `experience-block__title`, `primary-links__section-heading`, `radio-button__text`, `field__text`.
- **Utility type classes** that name the size: `p16` (16px tab label), `p12` (country eyebrow), `h324` (24px city title).
- **JavaScript and test hooks** are separated from styling by prefix: `js-widget`, `js-hero`, `js-ff-label-first-v2`, and `automation-widget-tab-search-flight__…`.
- **Layout helpers:** `grid`, `grid__item`, `g-site-container`.

### Theming
- No dark mode, high-contrast theme or density modes were observed. Theme-like changes are per section: charcoal chrome, white modules, a black loyalty band, and photographic full-bleed sections with white type.

### Type & spacing systems
- Type uses three families and a fixed px ladder: 10 / 12 / 14 / 15 / 16 / 18 / 20 / 24 / 30 / 50 / 120px. Mobile swaps to 12 / 14 / 16 / 60px for the corresponding roles.
- Spacing values cluster on multiples of 5px (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 50). No base-unit documentation exists.

### Component model (observed inventory)
Advisory carousel, global nav with utility links, hero slide with CTA, tabbed search widget (tabs, segmented trip-type toggle, floating-label airport fields, notice row, primary submit), content card, icon link strip, loyalty band, destination fare card, outlined and filled buttons, text links with chevrons, footer link columns, and accessibility skip links.

### Accessibility (observed, not documented)
- The page begins with visible-on-focus skip links: "Skip to the main content" and "Accessibility information".
- Links that open external sites carry an "Opens an external link in a new tab" text alternative, read from the footer link text.
- Focus-ring declarations exist in the stylesheets (`0 0 2px 1.5px #fff, 0 0 3px 3px #000` and `0 0 2px 1.5px #666, 0 0 3px 3px #666`), giving a white-then-black double ring. No published contrast or touch-target rules were found.

### Delivery
No public packages, framework bindings, Figma kit or versioning are published.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use `{colors.canvas}` #f6f6f6 as the page and `{colors.surface}` #ffffff for every module and card.
- Reserve `{colors.primary}` #d71921 for the one filled action per module, the active-tab underline and inline links.
- Express hover with the `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5) 0px 0px 6px 0px` glow, and keep the background colour (except outlined buttons, which also tint to #f6f6f6).
- Keep the 3px radius everywhere on UI.
- Pair weight 700 for the selected or active item with weight 200 HelveticaNeue-Light for its siblings.
- Put display type (Heroic-Medium, Emirates-Bold) on photography, as white text, and keep UI copy in HelveticaNeue.
- Use full-bleed, warm, high-production photography: cabins, skylines, landmarks at golden hour or dusk.
- Use spaced uppercase 10px/4px eyebrows above titles.

### Don't
- Don't use #c60c30 (`--red-color`) for buttons; the rendered CTA red is #d71921.
- Don't use `{colors.ink}` #af5541 for paragraph text — it belongs to the advisory strip. Paragraphs are #555555 and UI text is #333333.
- Don't round buttons into pills or use radii above 8px on cards.
- Don't add gradients, glass blur or coloured shadows to UI elements.
- Don't set body copy in Emirates-Bold or Heroic.
- Don't use pure black #000000 for text on white; the site's text is #333333.
- Don't change a button's fill colour on hover.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start every screen on `{colors.canvas}` with white `{colors.surface}` modules, 3px corners and the card shadow.
2. Put one red `button-primary` per task flow; make everything else `button-secondary` (white, 1px #333333) or a text link.
3. Check type weights: the active item is 700 and inactive siblings are 200 at the same size.
4. Hero text: kicker line in Emirates-Bold 30px/2px, headline in uppercase Heroic-Medium 120px/-5px, a second kicker line, then the red 40px CTA, all white on a photograph.
5. Fare and destination content: photo, then spaced eyebrow, Emirates-Bold city, light 12px small print, bold 16px price, centred.
6. Hover: add the 6px glow; never recolour.
7. Before shipping, compare values with this file: red #d71921, text #333333, paragraph #555555, muted #666666, canvas #f6f6f6, radius 3px. Anything else needs a measurement.
