# Sony (electronics.sony.com) Design System

## Overview

electronics.sony.com is Sony Electronics' US direct-to-consumer store. It reads as a retail catalogue
rather than a brand manifesto: a white `#ffffff` canvas, a pure black `#000000` header bar and footer,
big product photography that does almost all of the emotional work, and type set in Sony's own
corporate typeface, SST. Colour in the chrome is held back; the strongest hues on screen come from the
product imagery (orange headsets, purple earbuds, red-and-blue TV screens in the capture).

The capture of the homepage at 1280px shows the shape of the page:

- A thin promo strip at the very top ("Free Shipping & Extended Returns  Learn more") on the brand
  blue `#2d74c7`, with carousel chevrons at each end.
- A black header (58px tall on desktop) with the SONY wordmark at left, small 12px category labels
  ("TV & Home Theater", "Imaging", "Audio", "Gaming Gear", "aibo", "More", "Sale") and account/cart
  icons at right.
- On first visit, a centered email-capture modal ("10% OFF YOUR PURCHASE") covers the hero. It is a
  two-panel dialog: product photography on the left, a white form panel on the right with a 4px
  input, a grey `#e4e7eb` SUBMIT bar and a long 12px legal paragraph in `#6b6b7b`.
- A row of six category tiles (Headphones & Earbuds, Cameras, Lenses, TVs, Soundbars, Portable
  Speakers): cut-out product photos on white with a 16px SST-Medium label in `#212738` below.
- Large centered section headlines in SST-Heavy ("New to Gaming", "Benefits of Buying Directly from
  Sony") at 40.0588px, black/near-black.
- Two-up product feature blocks: full-bleed saturated photography, a product name in SST-Heavy 24px,
  a one-line description in `#6b6b7b`, and a square outlined blue button ("Pre-order", "Shop now").
- A four-card "benefits" row: white cards with a 30px radius and a soft offset shadow
  `rgb(223, 223, 223) 5px 5px 15px 0px`, line icon, bold 14px title, 12px grey copy and a small blue
  "Learn more" link.

The overall voice is that of a premium retailer: confident, quiet chrome; editorial-scale headlines;
precise small print. Microcopy is short and promotional ("Shop now", "Pre-order", "Learn more",
"Premium Gaming Headset now with Fnatic's iconic orange.").

Key characteristics:
- Square (0px) buttons everywhere — outlined blue on marketing pages, solid amber `#b65e0c` for
  "ADD TO CART" in commerce flows.
- Black chrome (`#000000`) top and bottom; white content in between.
- One typeface family, SST, delivered as many separately named web fonts (one family name per
  weight/width) rather than one family with weights.
- Soft roundness only on content cards (5px product tiles, 30px benefit cards); controls stay sharp.
- Small type dominates by character count: 12px and 13px are the two most-used sizes on the homepage.

custom properties; the site's variable names are quoted where they exist.

### Brand & Accent
- **Brand blue** (`#2d74c7`, `--indigoHamlet`, aliased by `--primary-cta: var(--indigoHamlet)`):
  the background of the top promo strip; the site's own "primary CTA" token.
- **Outline blue** (`#2d75c7`, `--mariner` / `--lightBlue` / `--curiousBlue`): text and 1px border
  of the outlined marketing buttons ("Pre-order", "Shop now"). Visually identical to the brand blue.

  text/border of the outlined "Email me when available" button. Measured hover: `#8c4808`.

  renders `#b65e0c`, so the live value wins and `#e87722` is documented only as a token.

## Typography

### Font Family
Sony's typeface is **SST**, commissioned by Sony and designed by Monotype (Type Director Akira
Kobayashi with Sony Chief Art Director Hiroshige Fukuhara), covering 93 languages; Monotype's case
study states it is used on the Sony website, packaging and manuals
([Monotype case study](https://www.monotype.com/resources/case-studies/one-typeface-93-languages-for-sony)).

On electronics.sony.com every weight is a separately named `@font-face` family, each registered at
weight 400. Families loaded on the homepage: `SSTPro-Roman`, `SST`, `SSTPro-Medium`, `SSTPro-Bold`,
`SST-Medium`, `SST-Roman`, `SST-Heavy`. The stylesheets also declare `SSTPro-Light`,
`SSTPro-Condensed`, `SSTPro-CondensedBd`, `SSTPro-CondensedMd`, `SSTPro-Heavy`, the `SSTStd-*` set,
`SSTTypewriter*` and `SSTUI-*`. Because the weight lives in the family name, choose the family to
change weight (`SST-Heavy` for display, `SSTPro-Bold` for the PDP title, `SST-Roman` for body).

Families by character count on the homepage: SST (1188), SST-Roman (961), -apple-system (877),
SSTPro-Roman (787), SST-Heavy (460), SSTPro-Bold (147), SST-Medium (76). The `-apple-system` stack
is Bootstrap's `--font-family-sans-serif` and appears in the modal form, small links and listing h1.

### Principles
- The fractional sizes (43.0327px, 40.0588px, 33.0327px, 20.3529px, 14.3791px, 12.3791px) are
  computed at the 1280px viewport, which indicates fluid, viewport-relative sizing for those
  marketing blocks. Reproduce them exactly at 1280px.
- Headlines are heavy (SST-Heavy) and tight (line-height ≈ 1.18–1.2); body is SST-Roman with a
  generous 25px line height at 16px.
- Most text is small: 12px (2022 weighted chars) and 13px (831) outnumber 16px (577).
- Uppercase is reserved for the modal title, the SUBMIT button and the copyright line.

### Note on Font Substitutes
SST is a proprietary Sony corporate typeface (Monotype case study; no retail licence is offered on
the official pages consulted). Monotype describes it as marrying "Frutiger's organic readability and
Helvetica's sharp geometrics"; the site's own fallback is the `-apple-system`/Helvetica Neue/Arial
stack. When SST is unavailable, use that system stack rather than inventing a substitute, and keep
the heavy-headline / roman-body contrast by choosing the heaviest available weight for display.

## Layout

### Spacing System
Measured paddings (homepage, weighted): 12.5px (23), 24px (12), 20px (11), 48px (6), 1px, 6px (5),
96px (4), 8px (4), 12px, 10px. Listing page: 4px (145), 16px (50), 24px (28), 6px, 12.5px, 14px.
Product page: 10px (37), 24px (25), 12.5px, 16px, 4px, 20px, 40px.

The site declares a partial spacing tier in `:root`: `--components-spacing-xxxxs: 2px`,
`--components-spacing-lg: 24px`, `--components-spacing-xxl: 40px`. 24px is the workhorse value — the
horizontal padding of every measured button and the bottom padding under section h2s
(`padding: 0px 0px 24px`).

Gaps: 11px (homepage), 4px (listing grid), 14px/24px/10px/8px/16px (product page).

### Grid & Container
- Desktop measurement viewport: 1280px. Feature blocks are two-up with a narrow gutter; benefit
  cards are four-up at 277px wide; category tiles are six-up.
- Product listing tiles measure 262px wide × 445px tall with 16px inner padding.
- Bootstrap breakpoints are declared as root variables: `--breakpoint-sm: 576px`,
  `--breakpoint-md: 768px`, `--breakpoint-lg: 992px`, `--breakpoint-xl: 1200px`.

### Whitespace Philosophy
Sections are separated by large empty white bands (96px paddings measured), and section headlines
are centered with generous space above. Inside cards the density is higher: small 12px copy, tight
icon-title-copy-link stacks.

| Level | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Flat | none | buttons, nav, footer, feature blocks |
| Tile | `rgb(228, 231, 235) 0px 0px 4px 2px` | product listing tiles (with 1px `#e4e7eb` border) |
| Card soft | `rgb(223, 223, 223) 5px 5px 15px 0px` | homepage benefit cards |
| Card soft (PDP) | `rgb(223, 223, 223) 5px 5px 10px 0px` | review-quote cards on product page |
| Cart | `0 8px 8px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, .1)` | `--cart-shadow` root variable |

The benefit-card shadow is offset down-right (5px, 5px) rather than centered — a soft "lifted paper"
look. The z-index scale is declared as `--z-index-1: 100` through `--z-index-7: 160` plus
`--z-index-max: 999`.

### Decorative Depth
Depth on the homepage comes from photography (studio product shots with shadows, saturated colour
fields) rather than from UI effects. No gradients are used in the chrome; the header's background is
a flat black `linear-gradient(to top, rgb(0, 0, 0) 67px, rgb(0, 0, 0) 0px)`.

## Shapes

### Border Radius Scale

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| none | 0px | all buttons, nav, footer, locale chip, feature images |
| input | 4px | text inputs (email field) |
| tile | 5px | product listing tiles, PDP feature cards |

Other radii exist in the stylesheets (2px, 3px, 3.2px, 4.8px, 8px, 16px, 20px, 25px, 50px, 160px)

component during measurement, so no token is declared for them.

No clip-paths, skews or notched shapes were measured; buttons are plain rectangles.

## Design System Architecture

**No public Sony design system was found.** Searches for a Sony/Sony Electronics design system,
brand guidelines, press-kit colours and npm/GitHub token packages returned only third-party
"DESIGN.md" aggregators (not used) and no official documentation (see `SOURCES.json`). The official
sources that do exist cover the typeface and accessibility policy:

- Typeface: SST, by Monotype for Sony, 93 languages, used on the Sony website, packaging and manuals
  ([Monotype case study](https://www.monotype.com/resources/case-studies/one-typeface-93-languages-for-sony)).
- Accessibility: Sony Group sites adopt the "Sony Group Web Accessibility Policy" and target WCAG
  2.2 AA, as stated on a Sony Group accessibility statement
  ([xyn.sony.net accessibility policy](https://xyn.sony.net/en/accessibility-policy/)). The
  electronics.sony.com statement itself could not be fetched (blocked to the fetcher).

What follows is the de-facto system, read from the live site's `:root` variables and DOM:

- **Token architecture**: ~250 root custom properties in three styles living side by side:
  1. Named colour primitives — `--indigoHamlet: #2d74c7`, `--mariner: #2d75c7`,
     `--laurelGray: #6b6b7b`, `--grayMediumCool: #2f353d`, `--grayishNavy: #3c3c42`.
  2. Semantic aliases pointing at primitives — `--primary-cta: var(--indigoHamlet)`,
     `--primary-text: var(--grayMediumCool)`, `--secondary-text: var(--laurelGray)`.
  3. A long-form component tier — `--color-component-button-primary-bg-default-neutral-inverse-resting:
     #ffffff`, `--color-component-button-primary-bg-default-neutral-inverse-hover: #e5e5e9`,
     `--color-component-button-primary-text-default-neutral-inverse-resting: #0c0c14`,
     `--size-component-button-minWidth-medium: 48px`, `--components-spacing-lg: 24px`.
  Bootstrap 4's default variables are also present (`--primary: #007bff`, `--blue`, `--indigo`,
  `--breakpoint-*`, `--font-family-sans-serif`).
- **Theming**: only a light theme was observed; no dark-mode or density switch was found. The
  "neutral-inverse" component tokens are the only theme-flavoured names.
- **Type & spacing**: one typeface family split into many named web fonts (`--font-pro-roman`,
  `--font-pro-medium`, `--font-pro-bold`, `--font-Std-*`, `--font-UI-*` variables). Spacing tokens
  are sparse (`xxxxs` 2px, `lg` 24px, `xxl` 40px); 24px is the dominant component padding.
- **Component model**: the DOM uses custom elements such as `cx-page-layout`, `cx-page-slot` and
  `ngb-modal-window`/`ngb-modal-backdrop`; no official component inventory is published.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.2 AA target (group policy above); skip links ("Skip to Header", "Skip to
  Main Content", "Skip to Footer") are present at the top of the body; a variable named
  `--ADACompliantGray: #c4c4c4` exists.
- **Delivery**: no npm package, Figma kit or versioned release of a Sony design system was found.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Keep the canvas white `#ffffff` and frame it with black `#000000` header and footer.
- Use SST-Heavy at 40.0588px/500 for centered section headlines and SST-Heavy 24px for product names.
- Use square 0px buttons; outline them in `#2d75c7` with 0px 24px padding at 49px height.
- Use solid `#b65e0c` for Add to Cart, darkening to `#8c4808` on hover.
- Set body copy in SST-Roman 16px/25px `#2f353d`; secondary copy in `#6b6b7b`.
- Let product photography carry the colour; keep chrome neutral.
- Use 30px radius and the offset `5px 5px 15px` shadow only for soft benefit cards.
- Keep the promo strip in `#2d74c7`.

### Don't
- Don't round buttons — no pill or 4px CTAs were measured.
- Don't use the token orange `#e87722` for Add to Cart; the live button is `#b65e0c`.
- Don't use Bootstrap's `#007bff` as the brand blue; it is a framework default in the CSS, not the
  rendered CTA colour.
- Don't add letter-spacing to SST; all measured tracking is `normal`.
- Don't use gradients or glows in the chrome.
- Don't invent dark mode; none ships.
- Don't mix more than one blue in a single component (outline `#2d75c7`, links `#3865e0`).
