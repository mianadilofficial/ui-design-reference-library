## Overview

The measured site is www.thaipaiboon.com, the site of Thai Paiboon Insurance (ไทยไพบูลย์ประกันภัย), a Thai non-life insurer. The capture shows a white page with a full-width photographic banner for "TPBTracking.com" (a woman on the phone, a rider on a scooter, a phone showing a map) followed by a row of four white cards with line-drawn blue pin icons (search for garage, search for motorcycle repair shop, search for hospital, buy insurance online). A saturated royal-blue band holds a "Cyber Insurance" feature block with a ghost-outline "ดูรายละเอียดทั้งหมด" button, and the section "ผลิตภัณฑ์ของเรา" shows eight flat two-colour icons (blue with gold accents) for car insurance, compulsory motor insurance, accident, home and property, business liability, marine and transport, cancer and cyber. The header carries the logo, "TPB Hotline 1525" in gold, a Thai/English switch, an "I-Service" outlined pill and a gold "ซื้อประกันออนไลน์" pill, with a white nav bar below it holding a gold search square. The tone is service-first and plain: hotline numbers, online purchase, tracking and garage finders.

Measured facts about the capture: the page was loaded at a 1280px desktop viewport and its scroll height is 6235px; the document language is "th-TH" and the document title is "ไทยไพบูลย์ประกันภัย". ## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use #084e79 for headings and #de9b3a for the single main action on a screen.
- Do set headings in Prompt 700 at 32px, 24px and 18.72px as measured.
- Do use 24px radius for primary buttons and 28px for outlined ones.
- Do keep icons flat, two-colour (blue with gold accent) and centred over a short Thai label.
- Do not add a second warm accent next to the gold.
- Do not use the browser-default link blues; the link blue is #0077c1.
- Do not use sharp-cornered buttons; every measured button is rounded.
- Do not put cookie banners or the tracking banner red on UI components.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from `canvas` white, `ink` #084e79 and `primary` #de9b3a.
2. Build headings from `display`, `heading`, `subheading` (Prompt 700).
3. Build action buttons from `button-primary` and `button-secondary`.
4. Place content on `card` tiles with the hover shadow described under Elevation.
5. Keep Thai copy short and service-oriented, with the hotline visible in the header.

Before changing any token, re-measure the live site with the repository extractor (`node web/scripts/extract-tokens.mjs --slug=thai-paiboon --force`) and run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=thai-paiboon`; a value that is not in the measurement or in a cited source must not be introduced.
