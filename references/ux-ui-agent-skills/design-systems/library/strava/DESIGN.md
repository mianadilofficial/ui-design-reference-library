## Overview

Strava's public web surface is two different registers stitched together by one orange. The logged-out homepage at `strava.com` (desktop, 1280px) is a compact sign-up screen: a 73px white top bar with the orange STRAVA wordmark, five text navigation items and an outlined "Log In" button; below it a three-column band — full-bleed cycling photograph on the left, a narrow white sign-up column in the middle, and a product photo (athletes, phone and watch running the app) on the right; then a sand-coloured footer (`{colors.canvas-sand}` — `#f9f8f5`). The whole desktop page is only 948px tall. The marketing page `/features` (the "Strava Subscription" page) is the opposite: an 8200px-tall editorial scroll with a black-to-orange gradient hero, huge BoathouseExtended display headlines, a sticky TRAIN / EXPLORE / COMPETE tab bar, full-bleed lifestyle photography and dark product-UI cards.

What holds both together is restraint in colour. Every primary action is the same flat orange `{colors.primary}` — `#fc5200` (the site's own `--color-coreo3`, and the orange Strava's API brand guidelines specify for "View on Strava" links). Everything else is warm neutral: body copy in `{colors.ink}` `#43423f`, UI labels in `{colors.ink-strong}` `#21211f`, headings and navigation in pure `{colors.asphalt}` `#000000`. There are no gradients on controls, no tinted card backgrounds on the homepage, no accent second colour in the chrome. The only blue on the homepage is the legal-link blue `{colors.link-blue}` `#0060d0` in the "By continuing, you are agreeing to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy" line.

Typography is a single family served from Strava's own CSS: **Boathouse** at 400 and 600 for the product UI, with **BoathouseExtended** (600) reserved for big marketing display on `/features`. Sizes on the homepage are small and functional (32px h1, 16px buttons, 15px footer links, 14px body, 12px legal); on `/features` they jump to 72px / 60px / 48px display, a 34px lead and 22px body.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One brand colour, used flat: `{colors.primary}` `#fc5200` on the primary button, the "Log In" text link, the wordmark, the "NEW" tag and the active-tab underline.
- Warm near-black neutrals instead of cool greys: `#43423f`, `#21211f`, `#64635e`, `#e0e0de`, `#f9f8f5`.
- Rectangular 4px-radius buttons (`{rounded.sm}`) on desktop; the mobile homepage CTA switches to a 56px-radius pill (`{rounded.cta-mobile}`).
- Hover is communicated by darkening (orange `#fc5200` → `#dd4800` / `#c94100`) or by underline on text links — never by shadows.
- Photography is real athletes outdoors (road cyclists, runners) and real device screens; illustration is limited to an orange hand-drawn route line on the right-hand photo.
- Desktop homepage density is low: one centred 404px-wide sign-up column carrying the h1, a two-line description, the "Already a Member? Log In" line, three stacked 43px buttons and a legal line.

### Principles
- **Two weights do the work.** On the homepage 491 of 586 measured characters are weight 400 and the rest 600. Bold (700) only appears on `/features` in tags, eyebrows and footer headings.
- **Extended width = marketing voice.** BoathouseExtended is never used in product-like UI (sign-up column, nav, footer); it is the headline face of the subscription marketing page only.
- **Tracking is reserved for uppercase.** Letter-spacing is `normal` everywhere except the uppercase labels: 1.98px on 22px tabs and 1.7px on 17px eyebrows (both exactly 0.09em/0.1em of their size). The mobile `/features` h1 is the one negative case (-2px at 48px).
- **Tight line-heights on headings.** The homepage h1 is set solid (32px/32px); `/features` h1 is 72px/72px.

### Note on Font Substitutes
Boathouse, BoathouseExtended and BoathouseCondensed are served from Strava's own stylesheets. No public foundry page, licence or retail listing for a Strava "Boathouse" family was found (searches listed in `SOURCES.json`; the only "Boathouse" fonts in results are unrelated display fonts). Treat it as unavailable. When it cannot be loaded, use the fallback stack the site itself declares — `"Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", -apple-system, system-ui, Roboto, Arial, sans-serif` — at the same sizes and weights. For BoathouseExtended display lines there is no measured or cited substitute; keep the fallback stack and do not fake the width with `font-stretch` or transforms.

## Layout

### Spacing System
- **Base unit**: the site's `--space-base` is `0.5rem` (8px). The `:root` scale is `--space-4xs` 2px · `--space-3xs` 4px · `--space-2xs` 8px · `--space-xs` 12px · `--space-sm` 16px · `--space-md` 20px · `--space-lg` 24px · `--space-xl` 32px · `--space-2xl` 40px · `--space-3xl` 48px · `--space-4xl` 64px · `--space-5xl` 80px · `--space-6xl` 96px · `--space-7xl` 128px. These map 1:1 to `{spacing.4xs}` … `{spacing.7xl}`.
- **Measured gaps (homepage)**: 8px is by far the most common flex/grid gap (stacked sign-up buttons), then 32px, 20px, 24px, 40px, 64px.
- **Measured paddings (homepage)**: 20px and 26px (nav items `26px 20px`), `10px 50px` (sign-up buttons), `6px 24px` (outlined Log In), 64px and 48px (section blocks).
- **Measured paddings (`/features`)**: 16px dominates (47 elements), then 128px section padding, 24px (CTA and tabs), `83px 0 116px` on the dark footer.

### Grid & Container
- **Desktop homepage**: three columns across the full 1280px between nav and footer — photo, a white sign-up column, photo. In the 960px capture the middle column spans ~293–667px and the content inside it is a 404px-wide stack (h1, description, buttons all measured 404px wide).
- **Nav**: logo left, five nav items immediately to its right (Activities with a chevron, Features, Maps, Challenges, Subscription), outlined Log In button pinned right. Height 73px.
- **Footer**: logo + App Store / Google Play badges + social icons in the left column, then four link columns (Features…, Subscription…, Support…, Privacy…). 360px tall on `{colors.canvas-sand}`.
- **`/features`**: centred hero text over a full-bleed gradient; centred section headlines; full-bleed photo bands with left-aligned overlay text; a three-up row of dark product-UI cards.

### Whitespace Philosophy
The homepage gives almost all of its area to photography and keeps the interactive column narrow and tightly stacked (8px between buttons). `/features` is the inverse — generous 128px vertical section padding and very large display type, with one idea per screen.

## Design System Architecture

**Strava publishes no public design system, token package or component library.** Searches for an official design system site, GitHub/npm token packages, a press brand kit and the typeface origin returned no official source (queries listed in `SOURCES.json` → `_research`). The only official brand document found is the **Strava API Brand Guidelines** ([developers.strava.com/guidelines](https://developers.strava.com/guidelines/)), which covers partner use of Strava logos: "Connect with Strava" buttons in orange and white at 48px height @1x (96px @2x), API logos in orange, white and black (horizontal and stacked), the approved phrases "Powered by Strava" / "Compatible with Strava", and "View on Strava" links treated bold, underlined or in `#FC5200`. It specifies no typefaces and no spacing. The press site ([press.strava.com](https://press.strava.com/)) is a newsroom with no brand assets.

What follows is the **de-facto system measurable on strava.com**:

- **Token architecture**: the page `:root` ships 165 custom properties in flat, kebab-case groups. Colour tokens are tiered by name prefix: `--color-core*` (`--color-coreo3` `#fc5200`, `--color-coreasphalt` `#000000`, `--color-corewhite`, `--color-coren6` `#F2F2F0`, `--color-coren7` `#FAFAFA`), `--color-secondary*` (`--color-secondaryb1` `#00397C`, `--color-secondaryt2` `#129398` …), `--color-extended<hue><step>` (`--color-extendedneutraln1` `#21211f`, `--color-extendedneutraln2` `#43423F`, `--color-extendedneutraln5` `#E0E0DE`, `--color-extendedblueb3` `#0060D0`), `--color-expanded*` (`--color-expandedsand` `#f2f2ed`, `--color-expandednavy` `#0b1527`), `--color-social*` and `--color-achievements*`. Non-colour groups: `--space-*` (t-shirt scale `4xs`…`7xl`, plus `--space-base`), `--border-radius-*` (`none`, `xs`/`base`, `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`), `--border-width-*` (`1`…`8`, `thin`/`medium`/`thick`) and `--divider-size-*` (`xs` 1px … `lg` 4px).
- **Theming**: no dark-mode or alternate-theme switch was observed on the measured pages; both pages render a single light theme (the dark `/features` footer is a section colour, not a theme).
- **Type & spacing systems**: spacing is rem-based on an 8px `--space-base` with 2px and 4px sub-steps; type sizes are absolute px values chosen per page (no type-scale tokens appear in `:root`).
- **Component model**: class names on `/features` are hashed (e.g. the tab link `A0cTS`, the tag `jWR39`), so no public component names are exposed. Observable component families: primary / secondary / outlined buttons, nav with dropdown, text links, tabs, tag, footer.
- **Accessibility**: no official accessibility standard was found. Measured facts: body ink `#43423f` on white; buttons are 40–64px tall on desktop and the mobile CTA is 54px tall; links signal hover with an underline.
- **Delivery**: no npm package, Figma community kit or versioned release of Strava UI was found.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use `{colors.primary}` `#fc5200` for exactly one action per group (the email sign-up, the "Dive into your Subscription" CTA) and for the inline "Log In" link.
- Keep buttons rectangular with `{rounded.sm}` 4px on desktop; use the 56px pill only for the mobile marketing CTA.
- Darken on hover: `#fc5200` → `#dd4800` (small button) or `#c94100` with border `#bf3e00` (large button); neutrals go to `#e0e0de` / `#e6e6e6` with border `#c2c2be`.
- Set body copy in `{colors.ink}` `#43423f`, UI labels in `#21211f`, headings and nav in `#000000`.
- Use Boathouse 400/600 for UI and BoathouseExtended 600 only for marketing display headlines.
- Track uppercase labels (1.98px at 22px, 1.7px at 17px); leave mixed-case text at `normal`.
- Lead with real athlete photography and real device screens.

### Don't
- Don't introduce a second accent colour for buttons; the extended palette is not used in the chrome.
- Don't use cool greys — the neutrals are warm (`#43423f`, `#64635e`, `#e0e0de`, `#f9f8f5`).
- Don't add shadows to buttons or hover states; none are measured.
- Don't use BoathouseExtended in navigation, forms or footers.
- Don't use the legal blue `#0060d0` for general links — on the homepage it only marks Terms/Privacy.
- Don't round buttons beyond 4px on desktop.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from the white canvas, `#43423f` body ink and one orange `#fc5200` action.
2. Reference tokens by name (`{colors.primary}`, `{typography.button-md}`, `{rounded.sm}`) and keep components rectangular with 4px corners.
3. Build button states from the measured pairs only: primary `#fc5200` → `#dd4800`; large primary → `#c94100` / border `#bf3e00`; neutral → `#e0e0de` or `#e6e6e6` with border `#c2c2be`.
4. Use the `:root` spacing scale (`{spacing.2xs}` 8px between stacked buttons, `{spacing.lg}` 24px tab/CTA padding, `{spacing.7xl}` 128px marketing section padding).
5. Pick the register: product-like screens use Boathouse 14–32px; marketing pages use BoathouseExtended 48–72px headlines with 22px body.
6. When Boathouse is unavailable, fall back to the site's own stack (`"Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", -apple-system, system-ui, Roboto, Arial, sans-serif`) and keep sizes/weights unchanged.
7. Do not invent radii, shadows or colours for the dark product cards or the gradient hero — they were not measured.
