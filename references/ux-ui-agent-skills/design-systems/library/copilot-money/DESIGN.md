# Copilot Money

## Overview

Copilot Money (copilot.money) is a personal finance app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web. Its marketing site is a dark, glossy product showcase. It was measured at 1280px (9046px tall) and 390px. A 40px electric-blue announcement bar ("Meet your money assistant ▸") sits on a near-black navy page (#000814). Below it, a minimal header (Copilot wordmark; Features, Pricing, Reviews; Log in; a white "Get started" button) leads to the hero: the 112px headline "Your money, beautifully organized." in 90% white, a short line ("All of your accounts, spending, and investments, automatically tracked."), and a blue "Get started" button. Large glossy pill-shaped category tags float around the headline, each with an emoji-style icon: DANCING, HOME, BABY, WEDDING, GROCERIES, DATE NIGHT. A row of award laurels follows.

The page continues with a 48px statement, "Navigate your finances with confidence. Track spending, budgets, investments, net worth, and get personalized recommendations.", then "Available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Web" with a cluster of device screenshots, and "Track your spending, boost your savings" with a bento grid of dark glass cards (Know where your money goes, Follow the line, Rollovers, Cash flow, Spot subscriptions). After that a light section, "Grow your wealth, track your progress" (#f8f9fa ground with white cards: All your money in one screen, Live performance, Allocation, Real estate), "Honest and thoughtful pricing" ($13/mo, test drive for free), a masonry wall of App Store reviews under "People love Copilot Money", a "Gain peace of mind" closing with a laptop mock-up, a footer, and a final strip of the glossy tags (BABY, GROCERIES, DANCING, DATE NIGHT, HOME, HIKING, WEDDING).

The art direction is premium dark UI: deep navy grounds, translucent blue-tinted panels, a single saturated blue, and playful candy-colored tags for the categories that money is spent on. The copy is calm and reassuring ("Your money, beautifully organized.", "Gain peace of mind").

## Typography

The site uses two commercial families, served as Framer web fonts:

- **Jokker** (Regular 400, Medium 500, Semibold 600) for headlines, nav and links.
- **Matter Variable Thin** at weight **100** for body copy. It is the most-read face (4172 characters), so paragraphs look hairline-thin and airy on the dark ground. A "Matter-TRIAL SemiBold" cut at weight 670 sets the category tag labels.

Neither is a system font. If they're unavailable, the site's own fallback is plain `sans-serif`; a rebuild that cannot license them should keep the contrast between a semibold geometric display face and an ultra-light text face.

Measured roles:

- **Display** — Jokker Semibold 112px / 100.8px, -2.24px tracking (the h1).
- **Heading** — Jokker Medium 48px / 57.6px (the long h2 statement).
- **Subheading** — Jokker Semibold 24px / 28.8px (card titles such as "Know where your money goes").
- **Body** — Matter Variable Thin 16px / 22.4px, weight 100.
- **Nav link** — Jokker Medium 18px / 28.8px, -0.18px tracking.
- **Tag label** — Matter-TRIAL SemiBold 28px, weight 670, +1.12px tracking, uppercase as set.
- **Compact button** — 12px generic sans-serif, measured on the 44px blue "Create" button.

Small UI text in the screenshots and cards uses 15px with +0.15px tracking (675 characters), 13px with +0.13px, and 12px or 11px with +0.24px and +0.22px. Sizes by characters: 16px (4129), 15px (699), 13px (300), 48px (266), 14px, 24px, 28px, 18px, 112px.

## Layout

The hero centers its text column (content from 514px to 1231px in the 1542px-tall hero band) while the tags scatter across the full width. Feature sections center a heading over either a device cluster or a bento grid of cards with mixed widths, at most 3 cards per row. The footer band runs from 80px to 1074px in 4 columns.

Inside cards the spacing is tight: gaps of 8px (102 uses), 12px, 10px and 16px, and paddings of 16px (51), 20px and 8px. Section rhythm comes from large heading-to-content gaps (48px) and the band heights (the dark feature band is 2082px tall, the light band 1380px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from light and gloss rather than drop shadows. The category tags carry a six-layer inset highlight: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16) 0px 24px 64px -12px inset, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.16) 0px -24px 40px -12px inset, …, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24) 0px 16px 28.8px -2px inset` (14 elements). It gives them a candy-like 3D sheen. Blue buttons use a near-invisible soft shadow (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07) 0px 0.362176px 0.651917px -1.5px, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05) 0px 3px 5.4px -3px`). Dark cards separate from the navy page through translucent blue fills (#1744820a and the Framer tokens #2671d914, #3388ff29, #5c8fd633) rather than borders. No border colors were measured.

- **Compact blue button.** "Create": #1c6cff, 16px radius, padding `12px 16px`, 44px tall, 12px text, soft shadow.
- **Announcement bar.** Full-width #1c6cff strip, 40px tall, centered "Meet your money assistant ▸".

## Design System Architecture

We found no public Copilot Money design system or brand kit; https://www.copilot.money/press returned 404. The site is built in Framer: its CSS exposes 62 auto-named color tokens (`--token-<uuid>`) and Framer font plumbing (`--framer-font-family`, `--framer-link-hover-font-family`), and fonts are served from framerusercontent.com with "Placeholder" fallback faces. The token values form a coherent palette:
- Navy surfaces: #000814, #010d1e, #010e21, #020c1b, #09182f, #090f24, #001533, #00204d, #143352.
- Text: #ffffffe6, #fffc, #597caa, #7f8ba4, #7f8ba499, #5e85bab3, #11263b, #7896ba, #78808a.
- Blues: #1c6cff, #0685f2, #437cef, #437df0, and translucent tints #2671d914, #3388ff29, #5c8fd633, #1744820a.
- Status and category: #00cc4b, #f90, #fece4c, #f43.
- Light-section neutrals: #f9fafc, #f9fafb, #f6f7f9, #fff, and #0a0e1414 / #0a0e141a hairline shadows.

The names carry no semantics, so roles here are assigned from where each value was measured.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use #000814 navy as the stage with 90% white text, and switch to a light #f8f9fa section only once for contrast.
- Set headlines in Jokker Semibold with tight negative tracking, and body in an ultra-light (weight 100) text face.
- Use #1c6cff as the single action and accent color, and frame hero CTAs in the 40% blue ring.
- Show the real app UI in dark glass bento cards with 24px corners.
- Use glossy, candy-colored category pills (pink, orange, purple, green, yellow) as the playful accent.
- Keep #00cc4b green as the only green in the UI.

**Don't**
- Don't use borders to separate cards; use translucent blue fills.
- Don't use the candy colors for buttons or text; they are for category tags and charts.
- Don't set long paragraphs in the semibold display face.
- Don't use #0000ee: it is the browser default measured on an unstyled anchor wrapper, not a brand color.

## Responsive Behavior

Measured at 390px:

- The h1 drops from 112px/100.8px (-2.24px) to **49px/49px** (-1.47px) and wraps to five lines (320px tall).
- The h2 statement drops from 48px/57.6px to **32px/38.4px**.
- Body stays **Matter Variable Thin 16px / 22.4px**.
- The 40px #1c6cff announcement bar stays at the top. The compact blue button stays 44px tall with a 16px radius.
- The extractor recorded no visible header links and no menu toggle at either width.
- The light "Grow your wealth" band measured #f1f2f4 on mobile (1918px tall); the rest of the page stays on navy (#000814, #020c1a).
- Most frequent mobile sizes by characters: 16px, 14px, 15px, 20px, 13px and 32px.

Breakpoint values were not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with #000814, a 40px #1c6cff announcement bar and 90% white text.
2. Load Jokker (400/500/600) and Matter Variable Thin. Set 112/100.8 display at -2.24px, 48/57.6 statements, 24/28.8 card titles and 16/22.4 thin body.
3. Build dark bento cards: translucent blue fill, 24px radius, a live product fragment in each.
4. Add the glossy 40px-radius category tags with the six-layer inset highlight, scattered around the hero and repeated at the page foot.
5. Frame primary CTAs in the 2px #0047cc66 ring, and use #597caa for nav hover.
6. Insert one light section (#f8f9fa with white cards and #11263b text) for the wealth story.
7. Pull new colors only from the measured Framer token values listed above.
