## Overview

siamsportprint.com is the corporate and services site of Siam Sport Syndicate Public Company Limited (สยามสปอร์ต ซินดิเคท จำกัด (มหาชน)), titled "SIAM SPORT SYNDICATE PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED – บริการผลิตสื่อสิ่งพิมพ์ครบวงจร" (one-stop printing services). It is the printing arm's site, not the sports news site. The measured home page is 1280px wide and 4,492px tall. A cookie-law bar was present and was hidden by the extractor before measurement.

The page reads as a conventional agency-template corporate site: a slim top strip with a black "Siamsport print" tag, phone number and opening hours, a white header with the round logo, "The Best in Thailand" and "Number" info blocks and a pink Contact us button, then a black navigation bar (Home, About us, Printing services, Services, Portfolio, Awards, Blog, Contact), a full-width slider with a photograph of a press operator and pink-highlighted Thai headline, three color-blocked value cards (pink, black, dark purple-black), an about block, a services carousel on a pink panel and a portfolio carousel. Copy is Thai with English navigation labels.

The microcopy voice is first-person corporate Thai ("เป้าหมายของท่าน คือ งานของเรา", roughly "your goal is our work") with short English labels for services such as Standee, X Stand and Carwrap sticker (inkjet) in uppercase on the carousel cards. The art direction pairs real factory photography with saturated pink overlays, so the pink is both a brand color and a photographic tint. Product photography in the carousels sits on white or light cards with generous margins, and every band is clearly separated by a change of background color rather than by rules or dividers.

The extractor reported the body element color as #fa1e5a, which is the tag-strip text; running paragraph copy is #727272.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is limited. One shadow value was measured: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 0px 15px 0px on 28 elements (the white carousel cards). Depth otherwise comes from stacking colored bands and from overlaying text on photographs. No other shadows were measured.

## Shapes

Measured radii are 3px (56 elements) and 50% (2 elements). The 3px radius is the button and card shape and the 50% is the round control (carousel arrows). The rounded tokens therefore use xs 3px and a pill value for circular controls. Photos and card panels are otherwise sharp rectangles.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system or brand guideline exists on the site: the pages opened (the home page via the extractor) link to none and no root CSS custom properties were measured (rootVars is empty). The de-facto system is a WordPress-style theme: icon fonts FontAwesome, ElegantIcons, revicons (Slider Revolution) and icomoon are loaded, and a cookie-law-info bar is present. Tokens here are therefore the measured computed styles: one font family, a pink primary, black and white surfaces and a single 3px radius.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do use #fa1e5a only for the primary action, key panels and accents, and set text on it in white. Do use Kanit throughout, light weights for large Thai headlines on photography and 700 for the button. Do keep photographs of real print products and production staff. Do give action buttons a 3px radius and 50px height.

Don't introduce a second typeface or a rounded pill button: the only 50% radius measured is on circular carousel controls. Don't render the cookie banner as brand UI. Don't use shadows other than the soft 15px 10% black shadow on cards. Don't confuse this site with siamsport.co.th, which is a separate sports news site.

## Responsive Behavior

Mobile layout, breakpoints, navigation collapse and column counts were not measured.
