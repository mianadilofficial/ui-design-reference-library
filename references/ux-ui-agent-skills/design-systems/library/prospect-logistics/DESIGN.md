## Overview

This is the English home page of **Prospect Logistics and Industrial Freehold and Leasehold REIT** (title: "Prospect Logistics and Industrial Freehold and Leasehold REIT"), at `https://www.prospectreit.com/en/home`. It is an investor-relations site for a REIT that holds warehouses and factories in the Bangkok Free Trade Zone estates (BFTZ 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6) and X44 Bangna KM.18.

The first screen is a full-bleed photograph of a yellow-and-white warehouse with a dark gradient at the top for the header: the PROSPECT logo (an orange and green "P R" mark with "Logistics and Industrial REIT" under the wordmark) at the left, and at the right the language switch (EN highlighted in green, TH in white), a search icon and uppercase links ABOUT US, OUR PORTFOLIO, INVESTORS, NEWSROOM. The h1 is white, uppercase and 900-weight, reading "PROSPECT LOGISTICS AND INDUSTRIAL FREEHOLD AND LEASEHOLD REIT". A vertical green tab at the right edge reads "SCROLL DOWN". Below, the page turns navy: "OUR PORTFOLIO" with a total in orange (752,707 Sq.m.), two donut charts (portfolio by property in greens, by zone in orange and pale orange), then alternating photograph and text rows for each property, each with "INVESTMENT ASSET:", "ASSET TYPE:", large orange figures for leasable and land area, and an outlined "EXPLORE MORE" button. The footer is light grey with navigation columns.

The voice is financial and factual: capitalised labels, defined terms, figures given with units and dates, and a risk alert in the body ("Alert : Investment in REIT contains certain risks").

## Colors

`canvas` #1a3870 (declared as `--dark-navy-00`) is the largest background area (5120 weight) and what the viewer sees across the content. `page-body` #ffffff and `ink` #ffffff are the white text and white surfaces (white text is 2465 characters, the most-read colour). `primary` #f59024 (declared `--orange-00`) colours the large figures and the zone donut chart; it is the accent of the page. `brand-green` #3bb04a (`--green-00`) is the active-language highlight and the "SCROLL DOWN" tab. `surface-light` #f7f7f7 (`--dark-grey-n05`) is the footer background and `ink-dark` #1b1b1d (`--dark-grey-00`) the dark text on it (1541 characters). `ink-on-dark` #ffffffcc is white at 80 percent alpha for secondary lines on navy; `ink-dark-muted` #1b1b1dcc is the same dark ink at 80 percent. `hairline` #ffffffbf is the 1px white outline of the buttons. `ink-bootstrap` #212529 (22 characters) is the Bootstrap default text colour appearing on a few elements.

The portfolio donut uses measured chart colours: `chart-green-1` #98dda1, `chart-green-2` #129476, `chart-green-3` #006a68, `chart-green-4` #47e45b, `chart-green-5` #00730f, and for the zone chart `chart-orange-light` #facc9a beside the primary orange. `text-input-text` #000000 is the search field's text.

Bootstrap's #007bff (declared `--primary`) was measured on an empty anchor and is a framework default, not a brand colour; it is not tokenised. The "WE USE COOKIES" card visible at the lower left of the capture is a consent overlay and is not documented as brand UI.

## Typography

Headings and body use Gothic A1 (weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 900) with Noto Sans Thai (600, 700) as the Thai companion: `"Gothic A1", "Noto Sans Thai", sans-serif`. Gothic A1 carried 3984 of the measured characters; Arial (398 characters) appears in form controls (search input). Icon fonts are Font Awesome 5 Pro and icomoon.

Measured scale: `display` 44.7px / 53.64px at 900 uppercase (h1); `subheading` 34.04px / 40.848px at 900 uppercase ("OUR PORTFOLIO"); `display-mobile` 27.1781px / 32.6138px at 900 at 390px; `body` 16px / 25.6px at 400; `link` 12px / 18px at 500 with 2.4px tracking, uppercase (button labels); `nav` Arial 16px / 25.6px. Header links are 14px; footer text 20.1px / 32.16px at 500; the size and weight of the large orange figures were not separately measured. Weights 500 (1830 characters) and 900 (1731) dominate: a heavy display weight against medium body.

## Design System Architecture

No public design-system, brand-guideline or press-kit page is linked from the page read (https://www.prospectreit.com/en/home). The shipped CSS declares a named palette as root variables: `--dark-navy-00` #1a3870, `--navy-00` #115492, `--navy-10` #003899, `--navy-n06` #f0f8ff, `--orange-00` #f59024, `--green-00` #3bb04a, `--dark-grey-00` #1b1b1d, `--dark-grey-n05` #f7f7f7, `--dark-grey-n10` #e8e8e9, `--grey-00` #dfe0e2, plus Bootstrap 4 variables and breakpoints (576px, 768px, 992px, 1200px and an extra 1640px). The naming scheme (hue, number, with n for lighter tints) is the nearest thing to a system; only some of the declared colours were measured on rendered elements, and only those are tokenised.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the page on navy #1a3870 with white text, orange #f59024 for headline figures and green #3bb04a for small accents.
- Do set headings in Gothic A1 at weight 900, uppercase, and labels at 12px with 2.4px tracking.
- Do give each property its own photograph-and-facts row with a single outlined "EXPLORE MORE" button.
- Don't use Bootstrap #007bff as a brand blue.
- Don't present the cookie card as part of the interface.
- Don't add shadows to buttons; the measured buttons have none.

## Responsive Behavior

Measured at 390px: the h1 is 27.1781px / 32.6138px at weight 900, body copy in the alert line is 13px / 20.8px, and the page shows 3 header links with a visible "Toggle navigation" menu button; the card layout uses a maximum of 2 columns. Other mobile layout details (chart sizing, row stacking) were not measured.
