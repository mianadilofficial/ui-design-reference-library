# Monarch

## Overview

Monarch is a personal finance app for tracking, budgeting and planning. The queued URL monarchmoney.com now redirects to **monarch.com**, whose page title reads "Track, Budget, Plan and Manage Money in One Place | Monarch". The press room carries the copyright line "Monarch Money, Inc.". The site was measured at 1280px (9722px tall) and 390px; a consent dialog was hidden before measuring.

The page is warm and editorial. A thin orange promo bar ("Get 30% OFF Core for one year with code WELCOME*") sits above a header with the Monarch logo, Features, For Couples, For Professionals, Pricing and Download, then "Sign in" (outlined) and "Sign up" (orange). The hero is a full-bleed lifestyle photograph of a smiling couple at home. On its left: "The modern way to manage your money" in a light sans, the line "Budget, track your progress, and plan for the future —all in one app.", and an orange "Unlock better money habits" pill. Press badges follow (Wall Street Journal, Forbes, CNBC and app-store ratings). Then "Your home base for money clarity" over a dashboard screenshot, and "Everything you need, all in one app" on a sand panel with feature cards (Spend, Budget, Plan, Collaborate). After that come alternating feature rows (Net worth "All your accounts, in one place", Transactions "Every transaction in one list", Recurring "Take control of your subscriptions", Reports "Visualize the flow of money", Goals "Your goals, on track", Apps "On every device, always in sync"), a yellow "Best-in-class data connectivity" band with institution logos, "What our members say" (a grid of review cards), a press-quote block, "Community resources", "Try Monarch free for 7 days", a red-orange closing band ("Track, budget, and feel good about your money with Monarch in your corner."), and a large white footer.

The tone is friendly and grown-up: serif section headings, warm cream grounds, real people in the photography, and small monospace uppercase eyebrows (NET WORTH, TRANSACTIONS, RECURRING) in orange that give a ledger feel.

## Colors

- **Page body — #fffaec.** A warm cream: the `body` and the main band below the hero (`--gray-2`).
- **Canvas — #ffffff.** The largest painted area (2890): review cards, the outline button, the footer.
- **Surface sand — #f3ecd7.** The "Everything you need" panel and other tinted sections (area 1578). It is also the nav-item hover fill. The CSS names it `--gray-3` and `--theme-gray-background`.
- **Card background — #fbfaf8.** Feature cards (`--gray-1`).
- **Ink — #2a2926.** Warm near-black for all headings and body (7100 characters; `--gray-12`).
- **Ink muted — #65625d.** Secondary copy (`--gray-11`).
- **Primary — #f86713.** Every CTA pill (`--orange-9`), with #fefcfb text (`--orange-1`). Hover changes to **#f35b16** (`--orange-10`), which is also the color of the mono eyebrows.
- **Surface orange — #e64818.** The closing CTA band (area 453), declared as `--theme-orange-background`.
- **Surface yellow — #efb921.** The data-connectivity band (area 219).
- **Hairline — #dcd9d6.** The outline-button border (`--gray-6`); on hover it darkens to **#bebbb5** (`--gray-8`).

## Typography

Monarch uses three commercial families. The CSS declares `--font-primary: "ABC Oracle", sans-serif` and `--font-secondary: 'ABC Otto', 'Copernicus', serif`; a mono face, Beltram Mono, is used for eyebrows.

- **ABC Oracle** (350 and 500) — body, buttons, nav, links. Weight 350 is the default body weight (7431 characters).
- **ABC Oracle Light** (300) — the hero headline only.
- **ABC Otto** (serif, 350; Copernicus fallback) — section headings, quotes and big statements.
- **Beltram Mono** (500, 14px, uppercase, 0.42px tracking) — eyebrows above feature rows and inside feature cards.

Measured roles:

- **Display (h1)** — ABC Oracle Light 59.68px / 68.632px, weight 300, -1.22344px. The words "your money" measured 61.9478px.
- **Heading (h2)** — ABC Otto 40px / 48px, weight 350, -0.8px ("Everything you need, all in one app"). A 48px variant with -0.96px tracking sets "Your home base for money clarity".
- **Subheading (h3)** — ABC Otto 32px / 38.4px, weight 350, -0.64px (feature-row titles and the press quote; 1320 characters).
- **Body** — ABC Oracle 18px / 25.2px, weight 350.
- **Button** — ABC Oracle 19px / 21.85px, weight 350, -0.19px. Small buttons are 14px / 20px.
- **Link** — ABC Oracle 20px / 28px, weight 500 (press links such as "Wall Street Journal").
- **Nav** — ABC Oracle 16px / 24px, weight 400.

Serif headings use tracking of -2% of their size (-0.64px at 32px, -0.8px at 40px, -0.96px at 48px).

## Layout

The hero is a full-bleed photographic band, 800px tall, with the copy column on the left (content from 32px). Below it, the cream band runs from 16px to 1264px. Sections alternate a centered serif heading over a screenshot or grid with two-column feature rows (copy left, product fragment right, or vice versa). Feature and review grids go up to 4 columns, and review cards are 326px wide. The footer is white, with several link columns and app-store badges.

Spacing is calm and even. The dominant gap is 16px (73 uses), then 8px, 4px and 32px, with 64–80px between sections. The most common padding is 24px (34 uses, the card padding), then 16px and 12px, with 40px, 64px, 80px and 160px for section padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Shadows are soft and warm-tinted (the base color is rgb(34, 32, 29)):
- `rgba(34, 32, 29, 0.05) 0px 1px 2px 0px` — buttons and small surfaces (11 elements).
- `rgba(34, 32, 29, 0.1) 0px 10px 15px -3px, rgba(34, 32, 29, 0.1) 0px 4px 6px -4px` — floating product fragments (2).
- A 0.075-alpha variant on individual cards.

Each value is written with two transparent ring slots in front. The focus ring is a box shadow too: `rgb(251, 250, 248) 0px 0px 0px 2px, rgb(42, 41, 38) 0px 0px 0px 4px` (a cream gap, then a 2px ink ring). Depth otherwise comes from the cream-to-white and cream-to-sand color steps and from the photography.

## Shapes

Only three radii were measured: **9999px** pills (16 elements — every button and nav hover area), **12px** feature cards and panels (13) and **8px** review cards (10). Product screenshots sit in soft frames, and the hero photograph runs full bleed with square edges. The system is simple: pills for anything you press, 8–12px for containers.

## Design System Architecture

Monarch's press room (https://www.monarch.com/press) offers a press kit download (https://www.monarch.com/press/Monarch-Money-Press-Kit.zip) with "our logo, product images, and company info". The page itself lists no colors, typefaces or usage rules, and we did not find a public component system.

The site's CSS (129 non-vendor custom properties, plus a large set of `--osano-*` variables that belong to the third-party consent widget) shows the de-facto system:
- **12-step color scales**: `--gray-1` … `--gray-12` (warm neutrals from #fbfaf8 to #2a2926, with #fffaec and #f3ecd7 at steps 2 and 3), `--orange-1` … `--orange-12` (#fefcfb → #f86713 at 9, #f35b16 at 10, #d64700 at 11, #592c1d at 12), plus `--red-*`, `--pink-*`, `--purple-*`, `--indigo-*`, `--blue-*`, `--green-*` and `--yellow-*`.
- **Theme pairs**: `--theme-{gray|orange|yellow|green|blue|purple|none}-background` / `-foreground`, e.g. `--theme-orange-background: #e64818` with `--theme-orange-foreground: #fff`, and `--theme-gray-background: #f3ecd7`. These drive the colored section bands.
- **Fonts**: `--font-primary` and `--font-secondary` as above.
- Also `--announcement-*` (promo bar) and `--tracking-*` variables.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use cream #fffaec as the page ground, with white and #f3ecd7 sand panels.
- Pair ABC Otto serif headings (350, -2% tracking) with ABC Oracle 350 body.
- Keep a single light sans display line for the hero.
- Use #f86713 orange pills for every action, hover to #f35b16, and focus with the cream-plus-ink double ring.
- Label sections with small orange uppercase Beltram Mono eyebrows.
- Use warm lifestyle photography of real people, and show the actual product screens.
- Use the theme pairs (orange #e64818, yellow, sand) for full-width accent bands.

**Don't**
- Don't use pure black or cool greys; the measured inks and borders are warm.
- Don't square off buttons; every measured button is a 9999px pill.
- Don't use heavy shadows; the strongest measured shadow is 10% alpha.
- Don't mistake the `--osano-*` cookie-banner variables for brand tokens.

## Responsive Behavior

Measured at 390px:

- The h1 drops from 59.68px/68.632px to **42px/48.3px** (-0.861px). It sits below the hero photograph (292.5px top padding) and "your money" measured 43.596px.
- The h2 drops from 40px/48px to **32px/38.4px**, and 32px subheadings become **28px**.
- Body stays **18px / 25.2px**.
- The primary pill text drops from 19px to **18px / 20.7px** (-0.18px).
- The header (with promo bar) is 84px tall, with 2 visible header links and no menu toggle detected (7 links on desktop, 108px tall).
- Content is inset 16px (16px to 374px); the hero copy band is inset 32px. Card grids measured up to 4 columns.

Breakpoint values were not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Set the page to #fffaec with #2a2926 text and load ABC Oracle (300/350/500), ABC Otto and Beltram Mono.
2. Build the type: 59.68px light sans hero, 40px/32px serif headings at -2% tracking, 18/25.2 body at 350, 14px mono eyebrows.
3. Make the orange pill family (54px and 36px, #f35b16 hover) and the white outline pill with the #dcd9d6 border.
4. Compose alternating feature rows with an eyebrow, a serif title, a short paragraph and a product fragment; use #fbfaf8 12px cards and white 8px review cards.
5. Add full-width theme bands (#f3ecd7 sand, #efb921 yellow, #e64818 orange) for emphasis.
6. Take new values from the `--gray-*`, `--orange-*` and `--theme-*` scales, never from the `--osano-*` set.
