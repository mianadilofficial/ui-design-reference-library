# Todoist

## Overview

The measured page is `https://www.todoist.com/` (redirected from `https://todoist.com`), titled "Todoist | A To-Do List to Organize Your Work & Life", language `en`. It is the marketing home for Doist's task manager, opening with the two-word headline "Clarity, finally." and the line "Join 50+ million professionals who simplify work and life with the world's #1 to-do list app."

The look is warm, soft and product-centred. The page is a near-white with a cream tint (`#fefdfc`); the text is a warm brown-black (`#25221e`) used at 66% opacity for body copy, which makes paragraphs feel gentle rather than stark. The one strong colour is Todoist red (`#e34432`), used for "Start for free" buttons, small eyebrow labels ("Clear your mind", "Plan with confidence", "In it for the long haul") and inline links. Panels behind product screenshots use a pale peach (`#fff6f0`), and template cards carry light mint and pistachio header tints.

Imagery is the product: the Todoist app window with Today, Upcoming and project lists; a calendar week view; phone and watch screenshots showing "9:41" and "Add task". Press quotes in italic serif ("Simple, straightforward, and super powerful" — The Verge) and a handwritten note ("Take a peek! Watch Todoist in action.") add warmth. A friendly cartoon mascot appears in the "What's Todoist?" video card. Copy is calm and reassuring: "Capture tasks at the speed of thought", "A task manager you can trust for life", "We've been building Todoist for 19 years and 261 days. Rest assured that we'll never sell out to the highest bidder."

## Typography

The site loads four families, served under hashed names: **Inter** (variable 100–900; 2,610 characters), **Graphik** (400, 600; 456), **Caecilia** (400 italic; 108 — the press quotes) and **Shantell Sans** (variable 300–800; 36 — the handwritten annotation). Each has a metric-matched Arial or Times New Roman fallback face. The stylesheet maps them as `--font-family-sans` (Graphik) and `--font-family-body` (Inter). Graphik and Caecilia are commercial; substitute Inter for Graphik and any slab-serif italic for Caecilia.

- **display** — Graphik 55px / 600 / 63.25px, -0.55px. "Clarity, finally."
- **heading** — Graphik 38px / 600 / 48.64px, -0.19px. Section headings ("Kickstart your next project with Todoist Templates"); feature headings measured 38px / 700.
- **body** — Inter 20.5008px / 500 / 32.8013px. Lead paragraphs; the most-read size.
- **button** — Inter 18.5px / 600 / 25.9px, +0.185px.
- **nav** — Graphik 16px / 400. **link** — Inter 16px / 400 / 25.6px.
- **input** — Inter 14.5px / 500 / 19.575px (the language select).

The final band's heading "Gain calmness and clarity with the world's most beloved productivity app" measured 44px / 600. Sizes are fluid: root variables define `clamp()` scales such as `--major-lg-font-size: clamp(1.75rem, 1.2813rem + 1.5625vw, 2.375rem)` and `--body-lg-font-size: clamp(1.125rem, 1.0078rem + .3906vw, 1.2813rem)`, which is why measured sizes land on fractional values like 20.5008px and 15.5008px. Body weight is 500 (most-read), not 400; weights 475 and 625 also appear from the variable Inter.

## Elevation & Depth

Shadows are warm (tinted with `#25221e`, not black) and used sparingly:

- `rgba(37, 34, 30, 0.04) 0px 1px 0px 0px` — template cards at rest (`--shadow-card-idle`).
- `rgba(37, 34, 30, 0.07) 0px 14px 19px -9px, rgba(37, 34, 30, 0.18) 0px 10px 48px 0px` — the floating "Play" buttons (`--shadow-overlay`).

The stylesheet also defines `--shadow-productui-app-window: 0 1px 7px -3px #25221e12, 0 0 20px -4px #25221e1f` for app screenshots. Nav items hover to a `rgba(37, 34, 30, 0.07)` tint. Focus on the CTA shows a two-ring box-shadow: `oklch(0.9451 0.0168 215.46) 0px 0px 0px 4px, oklch(0.6772 0.1122 245.18) 0px 0px 0px 1px` — a pale blue halo around a blue hairline.

## Design System Architecture

No public Todoist design system or brand guideline was found. The press page (https://www.todoist.com/press) offers only logo and screenshot ZIP downloads and press contacts; the About page (https://www.todoist.com/about-us) lists Design and Brand Design teams but links no design resources.

The shipped CSS exposes a large token layer (400 root variables). It is organised into colour ramps in OKLCH (`--grey-150`, `--purple-400`, `--sea-salt-300`, `--brand-700`), opaque and transparent warm-grey scales (`--greyopaque-900` `#25221e`, `--greytransparent-700` `#25221ea8`), semantic composition roles (`--composition-background-neutral`, `--display-onlight-colored-red`, `--box-highlight-background`), a separate `--productui-*` set for the in-app screenshots, fluid type scales (`--major-*`, `--minor-*`, `--body-*` with `clamp()` sizes and per-step letter-spacing like `--major-lg-letter-spacing: -.005em`), shadows (`--shadow-card-idle`, `--shadow-overlay`) and sizes (`--tube-height-lg`, `--icon-xl`). Legacy names (`--color-td-primary: #e44232`, `--color-td-cta-hover: #ee5244`) remain alongside. Variables prefixed `--osano-` belong to a third-party consent tool and are not part of the brand.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use the warm neutrals: `#fefdfc` page, `#fff6f0` panels, `#25221e` ink at 66% for body.
- Keep red `#e34432` for primary buttons, eyebrows and a few inline links; darken to `#cf3520` on hover.
- Set headings in Graphik 600 with slight negative tracking; body in Inter 500.
- Show the real app in soft peach panels with warm, low-opacity shadows.
- Add small human touches: italic serif quotes, a handwritten note, the mascot.

**Don't**
- Don't use pure black text or neutral grey shadows; everything is tinted `#25221e`.
- Don't use pill buttons; measured button corners are 15px and 8px.
- Don't spread red across backgrounds; it is an accent.
- Don't set body copy at 400 when matching the site; it reads at 500.
- Don't document or reuse the `--osano-*` consent colours as brand colours.

## Responsive Behavior

The mobile pass at 390px recorded: two visible header links and a menu toggle labelled "Open menu"; a 72px nav with `0px 24px` padding; h1 at Graphik 37px / 600 / 42.55px with -0.37px tracking; h2 at 28px / 600 / 35.84px; body at 18px / 500 / 28.8px in `#25221ea8`; the CTA at 18px/600, `12px 16px`, 64px, 15px radius. Content ran from x=24 to x=351 (24px gutters). The long middle band measured `#fafaf4` (5,969px) rather than `#fefdfc`. Breakpoints and the exact fluid-type endpoints in use were not measured beyond the declared `clamp()` values.

## Iteration Guide

1. Set the canvas to `#fefdfc`, text to `#25221e` (headings) and `#25221ea8` (body).
2. Load Graphik (or Inter) for headings at 600 and Inter for body at 500; use fluid `clamp()` sizes.
3. Make the CTA red `#e34432`, 64px tall, 15px radius, hover `#cf3520`; header variant 39px with 8px radius.
4. Put screenshots in `#fff6f0` panels; use warm shadows from `#25221e` at 4–18% alpha.
5. Lead each feature with a short coloured eyebrow and a 38px heading.
6. Build template cards: white, 10px radius, tinted image header, idle shadow.
7. Check that no colour is pure black and no button is a pill.
