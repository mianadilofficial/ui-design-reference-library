## Overview

The measured site is www.thaibma.or.th (English home), the site of The Thai Bond Market Association (ThaiBMA), shown in the footer as "The Thai Bond Market Association, 548 One City Centre, Lumpini, Pathumwan, Bangkok". The capture is a dense data portal on a warm off-white page: an orange top bar with the ThaiBMA logo and the menu Products & Services, Market Data, Bond Info, Rules / Regulations, About Us and News; a left rail "Quick Link" of peach tiles (Non-resident Flows, Issuer Search, Bond Calculation, Financial Ratio Statistics, Dealer Member, ThaiBMA Service Access Management System (TSAM) and more) and promotional banners; a centre column with a search field with an orange round button, a rotating banner "Bond Regulatory Update 2026", a "ThaiBMA in Focus" card holding net-flow figures in green and red, a half-circle gauge reading 33,836, an "Issue/Issuer Highlight" carousel and a "Bonds News" striped table; and a right column of cards (Gov. Yield Curve, Daily Trading, Government Bond Index, Indicative Intraday Yield Movement) with orange headings and tabular numbers. The footer is dark brown-black with orange column headings and social icons. A cookie banner and a chatbot panel were hidden for measurement and a round mascot button remains at the right; neither is documented as brand UI. The extractor read the gauge value "33,836" as its h1; it is a data readout, not a page title.

Measured facts about the capture: the page was loaded at a 1280px desktop viewport and its scroll height is 2224px; the document language is "en" and the document title is "home". The extractor counted 804 visible elements. Consent or overlay layers were hidden before measuring and are not documented as brand UI.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system, brand guideline or token package was found; the page was opened and links to none. The measurement shows no root custom properties, so the system is carried by the stylesheet: a font stack of SF UI Text, Inter and Noto Sans Thai, the orange navigation, and card widgets reused across the market-data panels. The page also loads FontAwesome and social-share-kit icon fonts.

The page declares no CSS custom properties on the root in the measurement (rootVars is empty). 

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use orange #f37021 for the navigation bar and peach #ffe9d9 for quick-link tiles with dark text.
- Do show numbers in tabular rows, green for gains and red for losses as in the in-focus card.
- Do keep card titles in orange and links in #ff5200.
- Do use 16px radius and a 0 2px 12px 6% shadow on data cards.
- Do not document the cookie banner, chatbot panel or mascot button as brand UI.
- Do not use the gauge readout as an h1.
- Do not fill navigation with peach.
- Do not use light text on the peach tiles.
