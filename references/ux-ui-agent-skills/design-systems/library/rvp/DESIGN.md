## Overview

The measured site is www.rvp.co.th, titled "บริษัท กลางคุ้มครองผู้ประสบภัยจากรถ จำกัด" (Road Accident Victims Protection Company Limited), whose pages offer services around compulsory motor insurance (พ.ร.บ.). The capture shows a floating white rounded header with the RVP logo (a purple and red flag mark), dropdown nav links (บริการลูกค้า, เกี่ยวกับเรา, ข่าวสารและกิจกรรม, บริการคู่ค้า, ติดต่อเรา), a round search button and a TH/EN switch in a purple pill. The hero is a promotional illustration in lilac and blue: a smiling rider pointing at a phone showing a vehicle tax label, with the bold navy-and-yellow headline about พ.ร.บ. and the tax label and three numbered white cards explaining policy period, choice of duration and daily premium. Below it a light grey band shows six outlined white icon tiles with purple pictograms and bold purple labels (buy motorcycle พ.ร.บ., claim, check with the department, find service points, pay vehicle tax, service standards). A large photograph of the company building sits beside a pale lilac illustration. The lower part of the capture is blank white where content had not rendered at capture time. A round chatbot mascot button ("แชทกับเรา") floats at the right and a cookie layer was hidden; neither is brand UI.

Measured facts about the capture: the page was loaded at a 1280px desktop viewport and its scroll height is 5071px; the document language is "th" and the document title is "บริษัท กลางคุ้มครองผู้ประสบภัยจากรถ จำกัด | บริษัท กลางคุ้มครองผู้ประสบภัยจากรถ จำกัด". ## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use #774cb8 for primary buttons and the TH/EN pill, with white text.
- Do use deep purple #3d1982 for strong labels and the #f7f4fb tint for soft panels.
- Do round buttons to 12px and cards or tiles to 24px.
- Do pair a purple pictogram with a short bold Thai label inside an outlined tile.
- Do not treat the chatbot mascot button or cookie layer as brand UI.
- Do not use pure blue or green for actions.
- Do not use sharp corners.
- Do not put body text in purple; body text is black.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from `canvas` white, `ink` #000000 and `primary` #774cb8.
2. Take extra purples only from the root scale listed above (for example 925 and 25).
3. Build action buttons from `button-primary` at 12px radius.
4. Give cards 24px radius, 24px padding and the 0 4px 8px shadow.
5. Write copy about พ.ร.บ., claims and service points in short bold labels.

Before changing any token, re-measure the live site with the repository extractor (`node web/scripts/extract-tokens.mjs --slug=rvp --force`) and run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=rvp`; a value that is not in the measurement or in a cited source must not be introduced.
