## Design System Architecture

No public design system or brand guideline was found: the home page has no About or Press link, only social links, Contact Us and Terms of use; no search was run. The de-facto system is a bespoke stylesheet: no colour variables (only --vh), a viewport-scaled type system (sizes are fractional multiples of 1.7778px, e.g. 14.2222px, 17.7778px, 21.3333px), and translucent cream overlays as the standard control surface.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep photography monochrome and let #ffcc00 be the one accent.
- Do set display type at weight 100 and body at 500 with slightly negative tracking.
- Do use 10.6667px uppercase Aeonik Fono labels for controls.
- Do build controls from translucent cream glass with 4.44px to 5px radii.
- Don't add shadows or gradients to controls.
- Don't use tan as a text colour.
- Don't treat the cookie widget as brand UI.
