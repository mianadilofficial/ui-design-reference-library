# Rippling

## Overview

Rippling (https://www.rippling.com/) sells one platform for HR, IT and Finance; the page title is "Rippling: #1 Workforce Management System | HR, IT, Finance". The home page combines a dark, saturated plum brand world with a calm, warm-neutral product world and a single bright amber for every action.

The capture opens with a lilac #eab8f2 announcement bar ("Reintroducing Automated Compliance, with AI built into every stage of GRC: before, during, and after your audit." / "Learn more", with a close icon). The header sits on the plum #4a0039 hero: the RIPPLING wordmark with its stacked-R mark, dropdown links (Products, Platform, Solutions, Global, Resources) and Pricing in translucent white, then EN, Login and an amber "See a demo" button. The hero shows a "4.8 stars 16k+ reviews" badge, the headline "AI that knows your company, and acts like it", a short paragraph, a white email field joined to an amber "Create free account" button, a "Take a product tour" link and three review-site ratings (Software Advice, PC Magazine, Capterra). On the right, a hand holds a tablet showing the Rippling AI assistant and charts, lit in magenta.

The capture also contains a third-party chat widget (an amber "Hey there!" panel and a round chat launcher). That widget is not part of the brand UI and is not documented here.

Below the hero the page turns to #f2eeeb sand: "Start with the apps you need, or run them all together" over a six-tile product grid (HR, IT, Finance, Payroll, Custom applications, AI), each with a glossy plum 3D icon, a short description and a "Get a demo" link underlined in amber. A white "Manage your entire global workforce" banner follows. Next, on white, "The workforce OS with intelligence built in" with alternating text-and-UI rows and sand case-study cards (Barry's "5×", Clay "80%", Cook Out "2–4"), a black "Go from prompt to productivity in seconds" video block, a plum section with workflow features, a black testimonial band, a "What's new at Rippling" carousel, award badges, a plum "Get a demo—live or on demand" band and a black footer.

The voice is direct and outcome-focused ("Automate busy work with all-in-one HR"), heavy with proof points and numbers.

The palette pairs two worlds: plum/black for brand moments and sand/white for product explanation. Amber is the only colour that appears in both.

## Typography

All measured text (8,249 characters) is set in **Basel**, loaded through Next.js font optimisation as `__basel_e55247` (weights 400 and 500) with a generated fallback. The root variables also declare `--font-styrene-a` (Styrene A), which did not render on the home page. Basel is a licensed commercial typeface; a neutral grotesque at 400/500 is the closest substitute.

Font sizes are fluid, so the measured values at 1,280px are fractional:

- **display** — 45.1434px / 49.6189px, weight 500, letter-spacing -0.78624px. The hero headline in white.
- **display-mobile** — 36px / 42px, -1px.
- **heading** — 39.1434px / 45.1434px, weight 500, -1.14336px. Section headlines.
- **subheading** — 28.5715px / 34.5715px, weight 500, 0.04736px.
- **body** — 23.0474px / 29.0474px, weight 500, 0.23712px. Testimonial quotes, case-study numbers and footer column titles.
- **button** — 14.7622px / 14.7622px, weight 500, 0.25px. Also the most-read size on the page (3,590 characters).
- **nav** — 16px, weight 400.
- **input** — 15.2378px / 22.4771px, weight 400.
- **link** — 12px / 16px, weight 400 (privacy note, small links).

Paragraphs are 17.5229px (1,643 characters) with 0.25px tracking. The system uses only two weights: 500 for headings, buttons and emphasis, 400 for running text. Large sizes tighten tracking; small sizes open it slightly to 0.25px.

## Layout

The desktop capture is 1,280px wide and 10,561px tall. The header is 80px tall over the plum hero (the root variables declare `--header-height: 70px` and `--search-bar-height: 80px`). Hero content starts at x 24; the hero is a two-part composition with text left and imagery right.

The product grid is three columns by two rows, each tile separated by a hairline rule above it. Case-study rows alternate text and UI mockup in two columns. The extraction reports a maximum of three card columns.

Spacing tokens are 2px, 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 20px, 24px, 32px, 48px and 64px. The dominant gaps are 8px (100 uses), 24px (87) and 16px (86), then 12px, 32px and 48px. Paddings are mostly 32px (41 uses), 16px and 24px, with 120px section padding.

## Elevation & Depth

One box-shadow was measured: `rgba(40, 22, 29, 0.08) 2px 8px 12px 0px`, a soft, warm-tinted shadow on a single element. Otherwise depth comes from colour blocks (sand cards on white, white banners on sand) and from the product imagery, where UI cards are layered over each other. The 3D product icons are glossy rendered illustrations with their own lighting. Keep interface surfaces flat; the shadow tint is warm plum-brown, never neutral grey.

## Shapes

Radii are small and practical: 4px (23 elements) for buttons, 5px, 8px (the video button and image panels; split as 8px 8px 0px 0px and 0px 8px 8px 0px where two panels meet), 12px (31 elements) for cards, one 9999px pill (the rating badge), and one 0px 0px 48px 48px rounded bottom on a large section. Amber buttons are nearly square-cornered, which keeps them firm next to the soft 12px cards.

## Design System Architecture

Rippling does not publish a public web design system. Its newsroom (https://www.rippling.com/press) offers media resources as Google Drive folders — a brand-logo suite (PNG and SVG, light and dark variants, described as including usage guidelines), product screenshots and executive bios — and a press contact; it gives no colour or font specifications. https://www.rippling.com/brand returns 404.

The de-facto system is visible in the shipped CSS: a Tailwind build (`--tw-*` variables, `--tw-ring-color: #2780ce80`) extended with named shadow colours (`--twc-shadow-color-primary/secondary/tertiary`), font variables (`--font-basel`, `--font-styrene-a`) and layout constants (`--header-height`, `--search-bar-height`). The SVG logo carries a `sava-site-logo-primary` class and a `fill-content-label-l0-active` utility, so the site uses semantic content-colour utilities named by level (l0…) and state. The CSS is organised around a 1024px split (1,127 `min-width: 1024px` rules and 1,070 `max-width: 1024px` rules) and a 767px boundary (1,069 rules).

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use amber #ffa81d with black text for every primary action, with a 4px radius.
- Contrast brand moments (plum #4a0039, black) with product explanation on white and sand #f2eeeb.
- Set everything in Basel 400/500; never bolder than 500.
- Use 12px-rounded sand cards with one big number for proof.
- Underline text links in amber.
- Tint shadows warm (plum-brown) and keep them faint.

**Don't**
- Don't use the plum as a button colour; actions are amber.
- Don't round buttons into pills.
- Don't use cool greys; neutrals are warm sand and taupe (#cec1b8).
- Don't document or imitate the chat widget as part of the design.
- Don't use weights above 500.

## Responsive Behavior

At 390px the mobile pass measured the hero headline at 36px / 42px (-1px tracking), the section heading at 30px / 36px with normal tracking, the testimonial body at 20px / 26px, and the amber "Create free account" button at 14px with 16px 20px padding, 54px tall and 348px wide (full content width). The lilac announcement bar measured 142px tall because its text wraps. A "Toggle main menu" button was visible with one header link, and the maximum card column count dropped from three to two.

The mobile capture shows the product tiles and case-study rows in a single column and the chat widget overlapping the hero.

The stylesheet's breakpoints cluster at 1024px (both `min-width` and `max-width`), 767px / 768px, 1440px and 428px. Behaviour at intermediate widths was not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Set up the two worlds: plum #4a0039 / black for hero and brand bands, white / #f2eeeb for product sections.
2. Load Basel 400/500 (or a neutral grotesque) and apply display 45.1434px / 49.6189px, heading 39.1434px, subheading 28.5715px at 1,280px; 36px and 30px at 390px.
3. Make every action an amber #ffa81d 4px-radius button with black text; hover #f59701.
4. Build product tiles with hairline tops, plum 3D icons and amber-underlined links.
5. Use 12px sand cards with a single large metric for case studies.
6. Space with 8px / 16px / 24px gaps, 32px padding and 120px section padding.
7. Exclude chat widgets and popups when comparing against the captures; dark mode and intermediate widths were not measured.
