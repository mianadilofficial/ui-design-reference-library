# Appwrite

## Overview

Appwrite is an open-source backend cloud (Auth, Databases, Storage, Functions, Messaging, Realtime, Sites) and its home page at appwrite.io is a bright, airy product tour on pure white. The hero centres a light-weight Aeonik Pro headline, "The open-source cloud for agents & devs_", at 76px with a trailing underscore that recurs on every section title ("All the services you need in one platform_", "Designed for the AI agents in your workflow_", "Loved by teams building in production_"), a typed-cursor motif that signals a developer audience. Above the headline sits a small pill announcement ("Breaking — We're thrilled to announce…"); below it, an intro paragraph and two buttons: the pink "Start project" and a quieter outlined "View docs".

The hero then drops into a large product screenshot of the Appwrite console — a usage chart drawn in pink, project sidebar and stats — on a soft pink-tinted glow. The page continues with a framework logo strip, a bento grid of product cards (each card is a miniature UI: a sign-in form, a database table, a storage upload, function logs), an AI-agent section with a chat mock and a benchmark table ("AI Arena"), customer logos and a case-study quote, an "Embedded security" grid of compliance cards, "The Appwrite Network" dotted globe, a big-number stats band, a pink line chart, pricing cards ($0, $25, Custom) and a dense link footer.

The voice is direct and developer-centric: "Start project", "View docs", "Built into every Appwrite project". Imagery is entirely product UI plus a few photos (the kittens in the storage demo) and pale gradient washes.

## Colors

The official assets page (https://appwrite.io/assets) lists three brand colors: **Appwrite Pink #FD366E**, **Light Grey #EDEDF0** and **Dark Grey #19191D**.

- **Primary #fd366e** — Appwrite Pink, measured on every filled button ("Start project", "Sign up", "Start building"); also the color of the usage chart in the console screenshot. Hover lowers opacity to 0.9 (measured).
- **On-primary #ffffff** — button labels.
- **Canvas #ffffff** — the page background and by far the largest painted area.
- **Ink #71717b** — the most-read text color: paragraphs, nav items, footer links.
- **Ink-strong #09090b** — headings and card titles; **ink-soft #0b0b0ecc** and **ink-plum #0d090bd9** are translucent near-blacks for secondary headings; **ink-slate #45556c** for small labels.
- **Surface-muted #f4f4f5** — muted fills (tabs, chips, table headers).
- **Hairline #e7e7ea** — the dominant border color on cards, inputs and the footer.
- **Card-bg #ffffffcc** — 80% white frosted cards over the soft background glows.
- **Brand-light-grey #ededf0** and **brand-dark-grey #19191d** — the official neutrals from the assets page, for logo backgrounds and brand material.

A green (#009966) appears in small status text (uptime, success states); it is a status color, not a brand color.

## Typography

Two families carry the page:

- **Aeonik Pro** (loaded at 400) — every heading. Display 76px / 79.04px, weight 400, -1.672px tracking; section titles measure 48px and 44px (with -1.2px and -1.1px tracking); heading 18px / 27px with -0.45px; card titles (subheading) 17px / 25.5px with -0.17px. The brand never bolds Aeonik on this page — hierarchy comes from size and tight tracking.
- **System UI stack** (-apple-system, system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, …) — all body and interface text. Body 15px / 24px weight 400; button 14px / 21px weight 500; small button 13px / 19.5px weight 500; nav 16px / 24px. By character count 13px is the most common size, reflecting the many small UI labels inside the product mocks.
- **ui-monospace** — code snippets and command text.

Uppercase micro-labels use positive tracking (11px with 0.55px or 2.2px, 12px with 0.6px). Aeonik Pro is a licensed typeface served by the site; for prototypes without a licence use a geometric grotesk such as Inter or Manrope at weight 400 with the same negative tracking.

## Layout

At 1280px the hero content spans from 140px to 1256px and is centred; later sections run as wide as 24px to 1256px for the bento grid and stats. Card grids reach three columns. The header shows ten visible items (products menu, Docs, Pricing, Enterprise, Customers, Blog, Changelog, the GitHub star count, Sign in, Sign up).

Spacing is tight and component-driven: 6px is the most frequent gap, followed by 8px and 4px; paddings cluster at 8px, 12px, 6px and 4px. Section-scale paddings of 28px, 40px and 80px separate blocks. The spacing tokens step 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 and 20px. The page is long (about 9670px at desktop) and dense with miniature UI.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is subtle. Cards use a 1px #e7e7ea border plus a soft 10% black 0px 1px 3px / 0px 1px 2px -1px shadow (the card token). Many elements use a 1px ring rendered as a box-shadow, and inset 1px hairlines (6% near-black top highlight, -1px left border line) to fake bevelled edges on panels. Translucent white fills — #ffffffcc, #ffffff73 and lower alphas — layer over faint pink and lilac background glows (#fff6f9 and #faf6fd bands were measured), giving a glassy depth without dark shadows.

## Shapes

8px is the dominant radius, used on buttons, cards and inputs. Pills (9999px) shape the announcement chip, badges and toggles. 6px, 14px, 3px, 10px and 4px appear on smaller controls; 16px and a 28px top-rounded (28px 28px 0px 0px) shape frame the large console screenshot. Icons are outline-style with rounded joins.

## Design System Architecture

Appwrite's official design system is **Pink** (https://github.com/appwrite/pink): "Appwrite's official framework agnostic design system", shipped as the CSS library `@appwrite.io/pink` and the `@appwrite.io/pink-icons` icon set, with documentation at pink.appwrite.io. The repository was archived (read-only) on Sep 7, 2026, and its README publishes no token values. Brand colors and logo rules are published at https://appwrite.io/assets. The marketing site measured here ships a Tailwind v4 theme with shadcn-style semantic variables (`--card`, `--muted`, `--border`, `--primary-foreground`, `--sidebar-primary-foreground`) and `--radius-*` steps derived from a 0.625rem base, rather than Pink's classes.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use Appwrite Pink #fd366e for filled actions and data highlights only; keep everything else white and zinc.
- Set headings in Aeonik Pro at weight 400 with negative tracking, and end section titles with the underscore cursor.
- Illustrate products with real, miniature console UI inside frosted 8px cards.
- Keep body copy in the system UI stack at 15px / 24px in #71717b.
- Write the name "Appwrite" with a lowercase w and no space, as the assets page requires.

**Don't**
- Don't alter the logo or place it on busy backgrounds; the assets page prefers neutral backgrounds.
- Don't use bold display weights; the measured headings are all 400.
- Don't introduce dark sections on the marketing page — the measured page is white from top to footer.
- Don't treat the green status text as a second brand color.

## Responsive Behavior

The extractor's mobile pass at 390px measured: the display headline at 48px / 49.92px with -1.056px tracking; the h2 at 16px / 24px with -0.4px; body text at 14px / 24px; the primary button unchanged (14px, 40px tall, 0px 24px padding, 8px radius). Three header items remain visible and a menu toggle labelled "Open website navigation" appears. The maximum card columns recorded on mobile is 3 (small grids such as the stats and logo rows); content spans 16px to 378px. The stylesheet declares min-width queries at 419px, 420px, 640px (most used), 768px, 1000px, 1024px, 1200px, 1280px and 1536px, plus max-width 600px and 1024px. Other mobile layout changes were not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start on #ffffff with #71717b text and #09090b headings; add #e7e7ea hairlines.
2. Use Aeonik Pro 400 for headings (76px display, 44–48px section titles) with the trailing underscore.
3. Make every filled button #fd366e with an 8px radius and 40px height; secondary actions are outlined.
4. Build the bento grid from frosted #ffffffcc cards containing live-looking product UI.
5. Add faint pink and lilac glows behind key sections instead of dark bands.
6. Keep spacing on the 4/6/8/12px ladder inside components.
7. Check the 48px mobile display and the 390px layout before shipping.
