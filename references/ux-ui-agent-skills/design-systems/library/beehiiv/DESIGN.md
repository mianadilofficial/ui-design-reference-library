# beehiiv

## Overview

beehiiv is a newsletter, website and podcast platform for creators, and beehiiv.com presents it as a loud, poster-like page on an inky near-black #060419 canvas that runs from the header almost to the bottom. The hero stacks "NEWSLETTERS. PODCASTS. COMMUNITY. ONE PLATFORM." in Clash Grotesk at 60px, weight 700, uppercase, with a 60px line height, beside a collage of dashboard screenshots. Under it: a one-line pitch, a star rating with a creator count, and two sign-up buttons — cobalt #2f39ba "Sign up with Google" and dark #131125 "Sign up with email" — plus a small "No credit card required" note. The header holds Product, Solutions, Resources, Enterprise, Ad Network, Pricing, Login, an outlined "Get a demo" and a white "Sign up for free" button.

A press/logo strip ("Trusted by the world's top publishers, creators, and brands": TIME, Morning Brew, ramp, Newsweek, status, Pinterest…) leads into product sections, each with a small pink eyebrow, an uppercase 48px Clash heading, a short paragraph, a right-aligned cobalt button and a large framed product screenshot: "STAND OUT IN THE INBOX" (newsletter builder), "NO CODE. NO LIMITS." (website builder), and "MONETIZE YOUR CONTENT" (ad network, on a hot-pink #f092dd panel). Pricing follows ("BUILD YOUR CONTENT YOUR WAY": Free $0, Lite $49, Pro $95 cards), then a scrolling feature ticker, "MORE THAN JUST NEWSLETTERS" (Create, Publish, Grow, Monetize cards), a creator testimonial, a persona picker ("THE ONE PLATFORM FOR EVERY CREATOR"), an integrations grid, an FAQ ("GOT QUESTIONS? WE'VE GOT ANSWERS."), a "READY TO BUILD?" card and a dark footer that ends on a white strip.

The voice is energetic and creator-first, with all-caps statements ending in periods.

- **Ink #ffffff** — headlines and primary text; the most-read color.

- **Card-border #15194f** and **hairline #0b0d2a** — navy outlines for pricing cards and dividers; **footer-border #ffffff0f** — 6% white rule in the footer.

A pale periwinkle #b3b8f0 appears in small text; #131125 fills the secondary "Sign up with email" button.

## Typography

Two faces are self-hosted under aliased names exposed as CSS variables (`--clash-grotesk-font-family`, `--font-satoshi`):

- **ClashGroteskFont** (700) — all display and section headings, always uppercase. Display 60px / 60px; section heading 48px / 48px. Tracking stays at normal; the heavy weight and caps do the work.
- **SatoshiFont** (400, 500, 700; 600 also rendered) — everything else. Body 24px / 32px weight 500 for testimonials and leads; running copy is mostly 20px (the most common size by characters) at weight 500; subheading 16px / 24px weight 600 ("What's included…"); buttons 18px / 28px weight 700; header button 14px / 20px weight 600; nav 16px / 24px weight 400. Uppercase eyebrows use 14px with 1.12px tracking.

Weight 500 dominates running text, which gives the page a slightly heavier, friendlier read than a 400 body. Clash Grotesk and Satoshi are served by the site under aliases; for prototypes use a condensed-feeling bold grotesk for caps headlines and a geometric sans at 500 for body.

## Layout

At 1280px the header pads 20px vertically and 128px horizontally; 128px horizontal padding recurs on section containers (12 measured instances), giving a content width of about 1024px inside a 1280px page. Product sections are a heading row (title left, button right) above a full-width framed screenshot. Pricing runs three columns; feature cards two by two; integrations as a dense icon grid. Seven header items are visible on desktop.

Spacing: 16px is the most common padding, then 24px and 8px; gaps are 8px and 16px, with 40px between larger groups; 64px and 96px paddings separate sections. The spacing tokens step 4, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 and 64px.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is mostly flat dark. Pricing and feature cards carry a soft Tailwind-style shadow (10% black 0px 4px 6px -1px plus 0px 2px 4px -2px), and one element uses 10% black 0px 10px 15px -3px. On the dark canvas these shadows are barely visible; separation comes from navy #15194f borders, framed screenshots with light bezels, and saturated pink panels.

## Shapes

6px is by far the most common radius (header buttons, inputs, chips, FAQ rows); 12px is used for screenshot frames and larger cards (`--radius-xl` is 12px); 8px for pricing cards; 9999px pills for tabs ("Media", "Creator", "Journalist", "Business") and toggles; 24px, 4px and 2px appear once each. The main cobalt buttons report a 0px radius on the measured element.

## Design System Architecture

beehiiv publishes no public design system or brand-guideline page: /brand, /press and /media-kit return 404, and neither the home page nor https://www.beehiiv.com/about links to brand assets. The shipped CSS exposes only the font variables (`--clash-grotesk-font-family`, `--font-satoshi`), a `--radius-xl` of 12px, and third-party variables from a phone-input and a tooltip library (`--PhoneInput-*`, `--rt-color-*`), which are not brand tokens. Colors and spacing are compiled into Tailwind-style utility classes, so the measured values above are the de-facto system.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Keep the canvas #060419 from top to bottom.
- Set every headline in uppercase Clash Grotesk 700 and end statements with a period.
- Use cobalt #2f39ba for sign-up and section buttons; use hot pink #ff5ec4 for links and eyebrows.
- Show large framed product screenshots, and drop the most important one onto a pink #f092dd panel.
- Run body copy in Satoshi at weight 500, 20–24px.

**Don't**
- Don't introduce light sections in the middle of the page; only the footer's last strip is white.
- Don't use sentence-case display headlines.
- Don't mix more than the blue and pink accents.
- Don't treat the phone-input or tooltip library colors as brand colors.

## Responsive Behavior

Two header items remain visible with a menu toggle (labelled "mobile navigation menu toggle button"); content spans 16px to 374px; the dark canvas still runs to a short white strip at the bottom. The stylesheet declares queries at min-width 480px, 640px, 768px, 800px, 890px, 1024px, 1086px, 1280px, 1284px, 1512px, 1535px and 1536px and max-width 600px, 639px, 640px, 650px, 768px, 1024px, 1283px and 1512px. Other behaviour was not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Paint the page #060419 with white headlines and #f7f5ff body copy.
2. Set headings in Clash Grotesk 700 uppercase: 60px display, 48px sections.
3. Use Satoshi 500 at 20–24px for copy; 700 at 18px for buttons.
4. Make primary buttons cobalt #2f39ba, 44–50px tall; make the header CTA a white 6px-radius button that inverts on hover.
5. Pair each section heading with a right-aligned button and a framed screenshot; place one on a #f092dd panel.
6. Build pricing as three navy-bordered 8px cards.
7. Check the 42px mobile display and the full-width mobile button.
