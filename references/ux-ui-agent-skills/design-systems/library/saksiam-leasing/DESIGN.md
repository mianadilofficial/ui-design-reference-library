## Design System Architecture

No public design system, brand guideline or token package was found; the page was opened and links to none. The shipped page does declare a colour scale on the root, which is the closest thing to a system: --main-blue-*, --main-yellow-* and --light-blue-* from 50 to 950, semantic aliases such as --color-primary-bg (var(--main-blue-50)), --color-text-secondary (var(--gray-600)), --color-disabled-bg and --color-warning-hover (var(--yellow-500)), plus a --gray-* scale. Third-party notification (--toastify-*), dialog (--swal2-*) and slider (--swiper-theme-color:#007aff) variables also sit on the root and are not brand tokens.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use navy #243865 for primary pill buttons with white 18px/600 text.
- Do use the main-blue scale (50 to 950) for tints and shades and keep ink at #192543.
- Do set display text in sukhumvitTadmai at 48px/800.
- Do use 28px cards on a soft sky-blue background with full-width pill buttons.
- Do not use the cookie dialog, toast or dialog library colours as brand colours.
- Do not use Swiper blue #007aff.
- Do not use square corners on buttons; the radius is 32px.
- Do not overlay body text on busy photos without a navy or white panel.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from `canvas` white, `ink` #192543 and `primary` #243865.
2. Take tints and shades from the main-blue root scale listed under Colors.
3. Build service grids from `card` with an image, a bold title and a navy pill.
4. Use `accent-yellow` only for highlight buttons and promotional tags.
5. Write loan names plainly in Thai with a short benefit line beneath.
