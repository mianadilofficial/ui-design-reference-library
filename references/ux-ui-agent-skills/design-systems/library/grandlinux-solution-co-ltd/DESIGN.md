# Grandlinux Solution Co., Ltd

## Overview

The measured page is grandlinux.com/en/, the English home page of Grand Linux Solution Co., Ltd. (Bangkok), titled "Saeree ERP - Complete ERP Solution". The company sells Saeree ERP, a budget-centred ERP for Thai public organizations, and, in a second panel, Claude AI licensing, training and integration for Thai organizations. The site's own fetched content lists the navigation as Home, About Us, ERP Modules, Claude AI, Solutions, Resources and Contact Us, and describes certifications from the Revenue Department, TICTA, ISO/IEC 29110 and the Ministry of Finance. The measured page height is 11082px at a 1280px viewport.

The first screen is a split hero. The left half is a deep-navy gradient headed "ERP Built Around The Budget" with a solid orange "Request a demo" button; the right half is a warm dark-brown panel headed "Putting Claude AI To Work Inside Your Organization" with a muted-orange "Claude services" button. A dark stats band follows (25+ years in operation, 2,500+ people using the system, ISO/IEC 29110 certified, Level 1 consultant) with orange numerals. Below, sections alternate white and a very light blue-grey (`page-largest`, #f7f9fc): client logos, four certification cards in solid blue, red, green and orange, and a grid of module cards with small blue code badges (BG, PO, IM, FA).

Copy is plain and candid. Short declarative sentences, regulatory vocabulary (purchase orders, withholding tax, P.P.36) and honest trade-off statements appear in place of marketing slogans. Section kickers are small orange uppercase labels with a short rule ("OUR CLIENTS", "STANDARDS AND CERTIFICATION", "ALL MODULES") above Title Case headlines.

The extractor hid a mourning-overlay scrim before measuring and the capture shows no overlay; the floating LINE chat button and the small toggle widget at the left edge of the capture are third-party or utility controls, not brand components, and are not tokenized.

## Design System Architecture

No public design-system or brand-guideline page exists on the site; the fetched home page links only to the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions plus social and contact channels. The de-facto system is a Bootstrap 5 build with a customized theme: the extracted root contains 51 custom properties dominated by the `--bs-*` defaults, with Bai Jamjuree as the page font and `boxicons` as the icon font. Brand colors are applied through component CSS rather than through variables, so the hex values listed here come from measured element styles.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:
- Use orange #fe6b35 for the one primary call to action on a section and for stat numerals and kickers.
- Keep body text in `ink` on white or `page-largest`, and use white text on the navy and brown bands.
- Use Title Case headlines in Bai Jamjuree 700 with -0.3px tracking.
- Show credentials (certifications, years, user counts) prominently.

Don't:
- Use Bootstrap default blue #0d6efd or other `--bs-*` defaults as brand colors.
- Document the LINE chat button or utility toggles as components.
- Mix more than one orange family (#fe6b35 for actions, terracotta for the Claude panel only).

## Responsive Behavior

Measured at the mobile viewport: the h1 becomes 27px with line height 36.18px (34px on desktop) and body text is 16.5px with the same size as on desktop. Mobile column counts and navigation behavior were not measured.
