## Overview

Patagonia's site is a catalogue that behaves like a field journal. The chrome is almost colourless — `{colors.ink}` (`#000000`) type on a `{colors.canvas}` (`#ffffff`) page, a black utility bar on top and a black footer at the bottom — so the photographs carry the page: a tent pitched in snow under purple skis, a van camp in a rainforest, snowboarders mid-spray, a climber on a sea cliff, a surfer inside a barrel. The pictures look like documentary shots of people outdoors, not studio product shots.

The homepage capture (1280px desktop) reads top to bottom as:

1. A 48px black top bar (`--pat-secondary-nav-height: 48px`) with small "Activism" / "Stories" links on the left, a centred campaign message ("Earth Is Now Our Only Shareholder" with a chevron) and "Find a Store" on the right, all in Ridgeway Sans 12px/300.
2. A 112px white primary header (`--pat-primary-nav-height: 112px`) with the lowercase serif *patagonia* wordmark at left, a row of 16px/300 category links underneath (Featured, Men's, Women's, Kids', Packs & Gear, Sports, Food & Beer) and four line icons (search, wishlist, account, bag) at right.
3. A split hero: full-height photograph on the left, a flat `{colors.hero-navy}` (`#1b2b79`) panel on the right holding an uppercase 16px/300 eyebrow ("MADE FOR THE MOMENT. BUILT FOR A LIFETIME."), the 64px/500 h1 "New Arrivals" in white, a 16px/300 paragraph and four white pill buttons (Explore, Women's, Men's, Kids' & Baby).
4. A carousel of square `{colors.surface}` image tiles with 8px corners, each with a 24px/500 title ("Black Hole® Bags", "Kids' & Baby Clothing", "Patagonia Provisions®", "Books") and 14px/500 sub-links underneath, and a white circular arrow button with a shadow.
5. A full-width warm off-white band (`{colors.surface-warm}`, `#f3f3ef`) with a large hand-drawn black-ink illustration (a crowd of hikers, voters and dogs drawn into the numerals "11.2"), an orange italic bold headline "11.2 Million Vote Project" (`{colors.accent-orange}`, `#fa4616`), one line of 16px copy and a small black "Join Us" pill.
6. A three-up row of tall adventure photographs (climbing, alpine running, surfing) leading into "Latest Stories".
7. A black footer (`#000000`, padding `0px 64px 120px`) with a newsletter sign-up, help links and a `#cccccc` copyright line.

The brand's activism is part of the page structure, not a side note. The top-bar message, the "Activism" link placed before "Stories", and the full-width illustrated voting band sit in the same hierarchy as the product sections.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-monochrome chrome: black `#000000` ink, white `#ffffff` canvas, `#f5f5f5` tile surfaces; the photography supplies the colour.
- Two custom faces: **Ridgeway Sans** for the whole interface (300 body, 500 headings, 700 italic only for the activism headline) and **Copernicus** serif for Stories content and story-card titles.
- Headings tracked at -0.02em everywhere (64px → -1.28px, 48px → -0.96px, 32px → -0.64px, 24px → -0.48px) at line-height 1.1.
- Pill buttons, 30px radius, `9px 28px` padding, 39px tall, Ridgeway 16px/500; white on dark, black on light.
- A single saturated navy used as a flat hero colour field (`#1b2b79`), and a single orange (`#fa4616`) used for activism.
- Square image tiles with 8px corners on a `#f5f5f5` placeholder ground.
- Black top bar, black footer, white header with a soft three-layer shadow.

## Colors

### Brand & Accent
- **Primary / Ink Black** (`{colors.primary}` — `#000000`): Bootstrap's `--bs-primary`, `--bs-body-color`, `--bs-link-color` and `--bs-dark` all resolve to black. Black is the action colour (`.btn-dark` is `#000` with `#fff` text by default), the body text colour, the top bar and the footer.
- **On Primary** (`{colors.on-primary}` — `#ffffff`): text on black and on the navy hero; also the fill of the inverted pill buttons in dark contexts.
- **Hero Navy** (`{colors.hero-navy}` — `#1b2b79`): the solid panel beside the hero photograph; the only saturated chrome colour above the fold.
- **Accent Orange** (`{colors.accent-orange}` — `#fa4616`): the "11.2 Million Vote Project" headline, set in Ridgeway Sans 48px/700 italic. The shipped CSS also uses it for a `0 2px #fa4616` underline shadow. Keep it for activism and campaign moments only.

- **Canvas** (`{colors.canvas}` — `#ffffff`): the page background and the background of the header, consent card and pill buttons. It covers the largest share of pixels in the capture.
- **Surface** (`{colors.surface}` — `#f5f5f5`): the placeholder ground of the square category and product tiles (`--bs-gray-100`, `--bs-tertiary-bg`). It ranks first by measured area because the tiles are numerous; on screen the photographs cover it.

- **Story Cream** (`{colors.story-cream}` — `#fbf0df`): the article panel on Stories pages, centred over a black page and overlapping the bottom of the hero video still.

By measured characters, 16px is the most common size on the homepage (1,619), then 14px (1,332), 24px (401), 12px (258), 32px (203), 48px (25) and 64px (12). By weight, 300 is most common (1,971), then 500 (1,219).

### Principles
- **Two weights do almost all the work.** 300 for anything you read and 500 for anything you scan. Use 700 only in the italic activism headline.
- **Every heading is tracked at -0.02em with line-height 1.1.** The shipped CSS sets `letter-spacing:-.02em; font-weight:500; line-height:1.1` on every class from `.h1` to `.h10`, `.title-1` to `.title-5` and `.marketing-1` to `.marketing-6`. The measured values follow from that: 64 → -1.28px / 70.4px, 48 → -0.96px / 52.8px, 24 → -0.48px / 26.4px.
- **The serif marks editorial content.** Copernicus appears only where Patagonia is telling a story: story cards and article pages. Story-card titles are tracked tighter (-1.2px at 24px) than the sans titles (-0.48px).
- **No uppercase styling on headings.** The hero eyebrow is typed in capitals in the copy; buttons and headings are sentence or title case with `text-transform: none`.

### Note on Font Substitutes
No official Patagonia source found in research documents who designed Ridgeway Sans or Copernicus, or how they are licensed. The claims on third-party aggregator sites were not used. When the real files are not available, use the fallbacks the site itself declares. For Ridgeway Sans that is `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial`. Keep weights 300 and 500 and the -0.02em heading tracking so the light, tight feel survives. For Copernicus it is `"Palatino Linotype", Palatino, Palladio, "URW Palladio L", "Book Antiqua"`.

## Layout

### Spacing System
Measured padding values cluster on an 8px rhythm with 4px half-steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40, 64, 80 and 120px. The one measured flex `gap` is 8px.
- `{spacing.xxs}` 4px · `{spacing.xs}` 8px · `{spacing.sm}` 12px · `{spacing.md}` 16px · `{spacing.lg}` 20px · `{spacing.xl}` 24px
- `{spacing.btn-x}` 28px: horizontal button padding (`--bs-btn-padding-x: 2.8rem`)
- `{spacing.xxl}` 32px · `{spacing.section}` 40px
- `{spacing.footer-x}` 64px: footer side padding · `{spacing.band}` 80px · `{spacing.footer-bottom}` 120px: footer bottom padding

### Grid & Container
- Header container side padding is `--nav-container-padding-x`: 16px by default, 32px from 768px, 48px from 1200px. The Bootstrap gutter is `--bs-gutter-x: 1.6rem` (16px).
- The hero is a two-column split, image on the left and colour panel on the right, each about half the 1280px viewport in the capture.
- Category tiles are 269×269px squares in a horizontally scrolling carousel. About four are visible at 1280px, and the first is cut off at the left edge to show the row scrolls.
- The story/adventure row is three tall photographs, inset from the page edges.
- Stories articles centre a cream text panel over a full-bleed black page. The measure is narrow, about 390px of text inside a panel of about 640px in the capture.

### Whitespace Philosophy
The space is generous but not grand. Sections are separated by large blocks of plain colour (white, then the warm band, then white) rather than by rules or borders. Inside a module, spacing is tight: tile title to sub-links is short, and sub-links sit in compact two-column pairs. The density is that of a store, framed by large quiet photographs.

## Elevation & Depth

The site is essentially flat. Only three kinds of shadow appear:

| Level | Value | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| Flat | `none` | Everything by default: tiles, buttons, bands |
| Raised | `0 4px 5px #00000024, 0 1px 10px #0000001f, 0 2px 4px -1px #00000033` | Primary header (`.primary-navigation`), carousel arrow buttons |
| Overlay | `0 0 18px #00000033` | Cookie consent card |

The raised value is computed from `color(srgb 0 0 0 / 0.14) 0px 4px 5px`, `/ 0.12 0px 1px 10px` and `/ 0.2 0px 2px 4px -1px`. In the shipped CSS the colour is mixed from `var(--pat-theme-cta-fore, #000)`, so the shadow takes on the foreground colour of the current theme.

### Decorative Depth
Depth comes from the photography: deep snowfields, layered mountain ridges, forest canopies. The hand-drawn activism illustration is flat black line work on a flat warm ground. No gradients appear in the chrome.

## Design System Architecture

Patagonia publishes **no public design system**, token package, Figma kit or brand portal that research could find. Seven searches were run, including for an official design system, brand guidelines, a press or media kit, GitHub/npm token packages, and documentation of the Ridgeway Sans and Copernicus typefaces. All are recorded in `SOURCES.json`. Only third-party aggregator pages came back, and they are not used. What follows is the system as it can be read from the site's own shipped code: [global-modern.css](https://www.patagonia.com/on/demandware.static/Sites-patagonia-us-Site/-/en_US/v1791396716386/css/global-modern.css), the [homepage](https://www.patagonia.com/home/) and a [Stories article](https://www.patagonia.com/stories/planet/activism/a-letter-from-yvon-chouinard/story-127258.html).

### Token architecture
- **Two prefixes.** Bootstrap-style `--bs-*` custom properties hold the semantic layer. Patagonia's own prefixes are `--pata-*` for font stacks and `--pat-*` for layout and theme.
- **Semantic tokens (verbatim):** `--bs-primary: #000`, `--bs-body-color: #000`, `--bs-body-bg: #fff`, `--bs-link-color: #000`, `--bs-secondary-color: #000000bf`, `--bs-tertiary-color: #00000080`, `--bs-tertiary-bg: #f5f5f5`, `--bs-border-color: #dee2e6`, `--bs-focus-ring-color: #91abe9`, `--bs-focus-ring-width: .2rem`, `--bs-danger: #e10000`, `--bs-success: #32b67a`, `--bs-warning: #feb904`, `--bs-border-radius: 8px`, `--bs-border-radius-pill: 50rem`, `--bs-body-font-weight: 300`.
- **Brand tokens (verbatim):** `--pata-font-sans` ("Ridgeway Sans" stack), `--pata-font-serif` ("Copernicus" stack), `--pata-font-special` ("BT Belwe W01"), `--pata-font-sans-light-ja` / `-regular-ja` / `-medium-ja` / `-demi-ja` / `-bold-ja` (Hiragino stacks for Japanese), `--pata-font-monospace`.
- **Layout tokens:** `--pat-primary-nav-height: 112px`, `--pat-secondary-nav-height: 48px`, `--pat-nav-height: calc(var(--pat-primary-nav-height) + var(--pat-secondary-nav-height))`, `--nav-container-padding-x`.
- **Component tokens:** button variables such as `--bs-btn-padding-x` (2.8rem default, 2rem small) and `--bs-btn-padding-y` (.9rem default, .5rem small). The shadow colour comes from `--pat-theme-cta-fore`.
- Inline SVG icons are stored as custom properties: `--chevron-down`, `--plus`, `--minus`, `--x`, `--check`, `--play`, `--pause`, `--lock-locked`, `--chat-bubble-white`. An icon font named `patagonia-icons` is also loaded.

### Theming
- No dark mode or high-contrast mode was found in the shipped variables.
- Dark-surface theming happens at the component level. The stylesheet defines `.btn-dark` twice: `color:#fff; background-color:#000` by default, and `color:#000; background-color:#fff` in dark contexts. That is why the same `btn-dark` class renders white on the navy hero and the black footer, and black in the light activism band. The `--pat-theme-cta-fore` variable feeds the raised shadow colour through `color-mix`.
- Japanese typography is switched through the `--pata-*-ja` font-stack variables.

### Type & spacing systems
- The root size is 10px (`html { font-size: 10px }`), so 1rem is 10px across the stylesheet.
- The heading scale has three parallel class families that share values: `.h1`–`.h10`, `.title-1`–`.title-5` and `.marketing-1`–`.marketing-6` / `.display-1`–`.display-6` / `.jumbo-1`–`.jumbo-2`. Every one is `letter-spacing: -.02em; font-weight: 500; line-height: 1.1`.
- Responsive type steps up at `min-width: 992px`. For example, `.h1` goes from `4rem` (40px) to `6.4rem` (64px), `.h2` from 3.2rem to 4.8rem, `.h3` from 2.8rem to 4rem, `.h4` from 2.4rem to 3.2rem, `.h5` from 2rem to 2.8rem and `.h6` from 1.6rem to 2.4rem. `.marketing-1` goes from 8rem to 12.8rem.
- Breakpoints in the stylesheet: 576, 768, 992, 1200, 1400, 1600 and 1800px. `--bs-breakpoint-xxxl: 1600px` and `--bs-breakpoint-xxxxl: 1800px` extend Bootstrap's standard set.
- The 16px gutter (`--bs-gutter-x: 1.6rem`) widens through `--nav-container-padding-x` to 32px at 768px and 48px at 1200px.

### Component model
The inventory is based on Bootstrap classes, observed in markup: `.btn` with `.btn-dark`, `.btn-light`, `.btn-outlined`, `.btn-sm`, `.btn-lg` (min-width 15.6rem, max-width 20rem) and `.btn-progress`; `.form-control`; utilities such as `d-block` and `mt-6`. Patagonia-specific blocks use BEM-like names: `hero-main__cta-primary`, `slider-cards__header-title`, `slider-cards__header-count`, `primary-navigation__links__level-one__link`, `copy__title`, `footer__bottom-copyright`. Carousels use Swiper (`swiper-button-prev` / `swiper-button-next`, `--swiper-theme-color`). Module stylesheets are split per feature: `hero/hero-main-v2.css`, `sliders/slider-product.css`, `sliders/slider-page-designer.css`, `homePage.css`, `util-modern.css`.

### Accessibility
- A "Skip to main content" link (black, white 14px/300 text, `0 16px` padding, 30px tall) sits off-screen until it receives focus.
- Focus is shown as a `#91abe9` border on buttons (`.btn-dark:focus-visible`, `.btn-light:focus-visible`, including the outlined variants), with a `.focus-visible-unsupported` fallback that applies it on `:focus`.
- The primary nav buttons have a 48px hit height.
- No published contrast or touch-target policy was found.

### Delivery
Stylesheets are served versioned from `/on/demandware.static/Sites-patagonia-us-Site/-/en_US/v1791396716386/css/`. No npm package, public repository or Figma library was found.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Let a full-bleed or half-bleed photograph lead every section, with black type around it.
- Set body copy in Ridgeway Sans 300 (16px/24px or 14px/24.5px) and headlines in 500 with -0.02em tracking and 1.1 line height.
- Use 30px-radius pills at `9px 28px` / 39px for actions: white with black text on dark or navy surfaces, black with white text on light ones.
- Put content tiles on `#f5f5f5` squares with 8px corners, titled underneath at 24px/500.
- Switch to Copernicus for editorial and story content: 64px/500 headlines and 20px/300/30px reading text on `#fbf0df`.
- Use navy as a flat colour panel beside a photograph, not as a button colour.
- Reserve `#fa4616` orange, in 48px/700 italic, for activism and civic campaigns.
- Underline inline links and remove the underline on hover.

### Don't
- Don't add a chromatic button colour. Actions are black or white.
- Don't use bold (700) for ordinary headings. It appears only in the italic activism line.
- Don't round tiles more than 8px, or make buttons anything other than full pills.
- Don't stack heavy shadows. Only the header and carousel arrows are raised, and only the consent overlay floats.
- Don't apply uppercase transforms to headings or buttons.
- Don't set Copernicus in the product UI (nav, buttons, tile titles). It belongs to Stories.
- Don't use tints of the navy or orange. Neither has a measured lighter or darker step.

### Touch Targets
- Category nav buttons are 48px tall (`12px 4px` padding around 24px line height).
- Top-bar links are 48px tall.
- Pill buttons are 39px tall at default size and about 26px at `.btn-sm`. Leave space around small pills on touch screens.
- Carousel arrows are 60px circles.

### Collapsing Strategy
- At 390px the primary header measures 56px tall with `8px 0` padding (112px on desktop).
- The hero h1 drops from 64px/70.4px to 40px/44px.
- Stories articles keep the same ramp at smaller sizes: h1 40px/44px, h2 32px/35.2px, body 16px/24px.

### Image Behavior
- Hero and adventure photographs fill their containers edge to edge in the capture. The tile row is a Swiper carousel (prev/next arrows) rather than a wrapping grid.
- Tiles measure as exact squares (269×269px) on desktop.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with a white page, black text and a black 48px top bar above a 112px white header with the raised three-layer shadow.
2. Set everything in Ridgeway Sans (or the declared system-ui fallback): 300 for reading, 500 for headings and buttons, with -0.02em tracking and 1.1 line height on headings.
3. Build the hero as a photograph next to a flat `#1b2b79` panel: a 64px/500 white h1, 16px/300 copy, and white 30px pills at `9px 28px`.
4. Add a carousel of 269px `#f5f5f5` squares with 8px corners, titles at 24px/500/-0.48px and 14px/500 sub-links.
5. Break the rhythm with a full-width `#f3f3ef` band for a cause: line illustration, a `#fa4616` 48px/700 italic headline and a small black pill.
6. For editorial pages, switch to Copernicus on a `#fbf0df` panel: 64px/500/-1.92px headline, 20px/300/30px body, 40px/300 pull quotes, and underlined links that lose the underline on hover.
7. Close with a black footer (`0 64px 120px` padding): 24px/500 white column headings, 14px/300 links, a bottom-border-only email input, and a `#cccccc` 12px legal line.
8. Check: no chromatic buttons, no heavy shadows, radius only 2/8/30/32px or 50%, and photography doing the emotional work.
