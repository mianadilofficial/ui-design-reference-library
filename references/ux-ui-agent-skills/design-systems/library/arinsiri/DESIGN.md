## Do's and Don'ts

- Do pair deep navy #003b70 surfaces with gold #ad8700 numerals, eyebrows and nav highlight.
- Do use large gold numerals over white labels in the stats band.
- Do frame photographs with thick white borders and soft 15% corners.
- Do keep headings in Rethink Sans and Thai text in Kanit.
- Don't use the WordPress preset colors (vivid purple, pale pink) or the popup's yellow.
- Don't treat the lead popup, side action bar or reCAPTCHA badge as components.
- Don't mix more than the two brand hues; navy and gold carry the identity.
