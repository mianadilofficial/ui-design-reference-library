## Overview

`https://www.ch7.com/en/` is CH7ON, the online and streaming home of BBTV Channel 7 (Channel 7HD); its page title is "CH7ON | ดูทีวีออนไลน์ ดูสดช่อง 7HD ละคร ข่าว กีฬา วาไรตี้ | CH7ON". This file is named after the measured site, CH7ON, which is the brand's own online portal. The first capture of `ch7.com` was a tribute splash (a framed portrait, a Thai date, a gold headline and an "เข้าสู่เว็บไซต์" enter button on a pale-blue ornamental background); the page was re-measured once from the batch file and the re-capture shows the real portal. A black tribute strip with gold ornamented text ("พระพันปีหลวง ในดวงใจ") still runs across the top of that capture; it is a tribute overlay and is not documented as brand UI.

The portal is a dense, white, content-rail layout: a nav bar (101px tall at 1280px wide) with the red CH7 logo, six text links (ละคร, หนัง / ซีรีส์, ข่าว, กีฬา, PPV, อื่น ๆ with a chevron), a partner wordmark, search and language icons, a white "Login" button and three small red icon buttons labelled APP, SHOP and PREMIUM; then a "Live" player on the left beside a rounded "Welcome CH7ON" panel holding a "Program Schedule" ("Live Now" and "Next Program" cards) and a "Recommended for You" rail; then rails titled "Short Video" and "Chula XL Hub" with "View All" links; promotional banners; a poster carousel; and a dark-red "Top 10 Today" band with numbered tiles. Imagery is TV key art and stills; the page is 2,400px tall in the measurement, with 3,194 visible elements.

Copy is bilingual Thai and English with short labels ("Live", "Short Video", "Recommended for You", "Program Schedule", "View All"). The footer copyright reads "© CH7ON Official Site." Promotional ad banners in the capture are advertising and are not brand UI.

## Design System Architecture

No public design-system or brand-guideline page was found: a fetch of `https://www.ch7.com/en/` returned only the page title, and the measured page links no brand guidelines, press kit or logo assets. The de-facto system is a token layer in the shipped CSS: 400 custom properties on `:root`, grouped by prefix as `--colors-*` (86), `--utility-*` (45), `--numerical-*` (43), `--bg-*` (37), `--text-*` (30), `--button-*` (29), `--cc-*` (27, cookie-consent variables such as `--cc-cookie-category-block-hover-bg`, not brand), `--fg-*` (21) and `--typography-*` (15). The semantic names use a roles-based convention (for example `--fg-brand-rose-primary_alt`, `--bg-brand-rose-primary_subtle`, `--button-secondary-rose-fg`, `--stroke-brand-rose`, `--focus-brand-rose-ring`) over a declared `--colors-brand-rose-*` ramp that runs from 25 to 950.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use `{colors.primary}` for links, "View All" and the Download pill, and keep body text in `{colors.ink}`.
- Do round cards at 12px with a soft rose border and the two-layer neutral shadow.
- Do organise content as titled horizontal rails with "View All" links and a round next-arrow.
- Do use Sukhumvit Set at 400, 600 and 700 for Thai and Latin text alike.
- Don't document the tribute strip, advertising banners, consent-widget variables (`--cc-*`) or other overlays as brand UI.
- Don't use the declared purple, blue or green ramps for the portal chrome; they were not measured on the page.
- Don't set body copy larger than 16px: the dominant size is 14px.

## Responsive Behavior

Measured at 390px: the nav bar is 390px by 61px with `8px 0px` padding (101px tall with `18px 0px` padding on desktop); the "Download" pill appears as the call to action (81px by 28px, 12px / 600); section headings step down to 20px / 700 / 30px ("Lifestyle", 374px wide) and 18px / 700 / 28px ("Recommended for You", 356px wide); the copyright line stays 16px / 400 / 24px (358px by 69px). By characters, mobile text sizes are 12px (41,116), 14px (9,430), 10px (1,550), 16px (1,199), 18px (401), 20px (9) and 13px (4), against 14px (42,687) and 16px (9,929) as the dominant desktop sizes, so the dominant body size is 12px on mobile. Breakpoint widths, rail card widths and how the nav links are presented are not measured.

## Iteration Guide

5. Close with a dark-red "Top 10" band using `brand-rose-900` `#b50202` and white numerals.
