# Docusign

## Overview

Docusign sells electronic signature and what it now calls Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM). The measured home page (docusign.com, 1280px wide, 10552px tall, `lang="en-us"`) opens with a thin deep-purple utility bar in `#26065d` carrying an analyst-report announcement and small 12px links (Sales phone number, Support, Access Documents, Log In), a white main navigation row with uppercase "CONTACT SALES", "BUY NOW" and a violet "TRY FOR FREE" button, and then a full-width hero whose background runs from violet at the top to near-black purple at the bottom. In the hero sits one light-weight 80px headline in white, "Everything you need to agree", a single line of supporting copy and an email field joined to a dark "Get Started" button.

Below the hero the page turns white. A 58px light-weight section title ("AI-powered agreement management") introduces a row of filter tabs (View solutions, Featured, Sales, Customer Experience, Procurement, Human Resources, Legal) and then a two-column grid of tall lavender `#ede5ff` cards, each with a 24px medium title, a short paragraph, a violet text link ("Explore Agreement Manager") and a product UI vignette — search panels, phone screens, a code editor, agent chat bubbles. Further down come a second violet-to-indigo gradient band about custom workflows and integrations, a dark "Trusted the world over" statistics band, a customer-logo strip on white, a "Recommended for you" row of event and article cards, a large violet closing call to action ("Docusign IAM is the agreement platform your business needs") with a photo of a smiling person, and a sand-coloured footer.

The microcopy is short and functional: "TRY FOR FREE", "View Plans and Pricing", "Explore Agreement Manager", "Get Started". The visual language is violet-on-white with almost no other hue, and a single typeface across every role.

## Typography

Every measured character except a widget is set in **DSIndigo**, Docusign's own typeface, loaded from Docusign's CDN in light, light italic, regular, medium and semibold files (the page loaded weights 300, 400, 500 and 600). The declared stack is `DSIndigo, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

- **display** — 80px / 300 / 86.4px, -1.5px tracking. The hero headline, white on the gradient. Using the light weight at display size is the signature move.
- **heading** — 58px / 300 / 62.64px, -1.25px tracking. Section titles such as "AI-powered agreement management".
- Section titles in the dark bands ("Build custom contract workflows", "Trusted the world over", "Companies do better with Docusign") measured 40px with -0.5px tracking.
- **subheading** — 24px / 500 / 29.76px, -0.25px. Card titles.
- **lead** — 24px / 400 / 36px, -0.25px. Large introductory paragraphs on white. A 30px size with -0.25px tracking also appears (716 characters).
- **input** — 18px / 400 / 27px; 18px is also a frequent paragraph size (1181 characters).
- **button** — 14px / 600 / 21px, +0.2px tracking, uppercase. The header "TRY FOR FREE".
- **button-large** — 16px / 500 / 24px, +0.2px tracking, sentence case. In-page buttons.
- **nav** and **link** — 12px / 500 and 12px / 400, both 18px line height and +0.2px tracking, for the utility bar.
- **footer-label** — 12px / 600 / 18px, +0.8px tracking, uppercase: footer column titles such as "APPLICATIONS".

Weights in rotation: 400 (3983 characters), 500 (2268), 600 (195) and 300 (59, the display lines). Tracking rule: negative on everything 24px and up, +0.2px on 12–16px UI text, +0.8px on uppercase labels.

**Substitute:** DSIndigo is proprietary. Use the site's own fallbacks, Helvetica then Arial, keeping the 300 weight for display sizes and the tracking values above.

## Layout

The main content column measured from x=100 to x=1180 — 1080px inside the 1280px viewport. The utility bar and header span wider (content from x=80 to x=1240). The hero headline is centred. Product cards are laid out two per row at 524px wide and 674px tall. The footer measured a five-column link area.

Spacing is built on a 4px/8px rhythm: the most common gaps are 8px (96 uses), 4px (42), 16px (25), 48px (21) and 24px (20); paddings cluster at 16px (65), 8px (31), 48px (25), 24px (24), with 80px and 96px used for section padding. Bands alternate white, gradient, white, dark, white and sand, so the page reads as a series of full-bleed chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Docusign uses very little shadow. The most frequent measured shadow is a 1px inset ring `rgba(19, 0, 50, 0.15) 0px 0px 0px 1px inset` (6 uses) that outlines form controls and chips in the brand's purple-black at 15%. Floating panels use `rgba(19, 0, 50, 0.15) 0px 16px 32px 0px` and a smaller `rgba(19, 0, 50, 0.15) 0px 4px 8px 0px` — note the shadows are tinted with the brand's `#130032`, not neutral black. Depth on the page comes mainly from the gradient hero and from product screenshots layered over the lavender cards.

## Shapes

- **4px (sm)** — the most frequent radius (119 uses): links, tabs and small UI.
- **8px (md)** — buttons, inputs and cards (46 uses). The hero email field and "Get Started" button form one control: the input rounds `8px 0px 0px 8px`, the button `0px 8px 8px 0px`.
- **16px (xl)** — larger panels (14 uses), including top-only `16px 16px 0px 0px`.
- **12px (lg)** and **2px (xs)** — single uses; 2px on the country selector in the footer.
- **round 100.16px** and `50%` — circular avatars and icon buttons.

Corners are modest; nothing in the measured UI is a full pill apart from a third-party chat launcher, which is not part of the brand UI.

## Design System Architecture

No public UI design system, token package or brand-guideline document from Docusign was found in the sources opened. Docusign's news center (https://www.docusign.com/company/news-center) links a press kit on Brandfolder (https://brandfolder.com/docusign-1/press-kit), which offers a public "Docusign Public Brand Assets" collection of logo files and states that a separate brand portal for employees requires sign-in; it lists no colours or typefaces. Docusign's GitHub organisation (https://github.com/docusign) holds SDKs and demos, not a design system.

The de-facto system is therefore read from the shipped page: one custom typeface (DSIndigo, served as per-weight `.woff2` files from `docucdn-a.akamaihd.net`), a violet-and-purple-black palette with alpha variants of `#130032` for secondary text and borders, an 8px control radius with 4px for small UI, a 4/8px spacing rhythm, and brand-tinted shadows. The site sets no `:root` custom properties, so values are compiled into component styles rather than exposed as tokens.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Set every text role in DSIndigo (or Helvetica) and use weight 300 for display and section headings.
- Use `#4c00ff` for the main action and violet text links; darken to `#4000cc` on hover.
- Use `#130032` at full strength for headings and at 75% (`#130032bf`) for body copy.
- Put lavender `#ede5ff` behind product cards and keep the cards at 8px radius.
- Use the violet-to-midnight gradient (`#3903ae` to `#26065d`) for hero and feature bands with white text.
- Tint shadows and borders with `#130032`.

**Don't**
- Don't use bold display type; the measured headlines are 300.
- Don't introduce a second accent hue; every measured accent is in the violet family.
- Don't make buttons pill-shaped.
- Don't use pure grey for borders — use `#13003226`.
- Don't treat the geo-location "switch region" banner or the chat launcher as brand components.

## Responsive Behavior

Only the extractor's 390px mobile pass is reported here. At 390px the hero headline measured 58px / 300 / 62.64px with -1.25px tracking (down from 80px), the paragraph text measured 18px / 27px, and the "TRY FOR FREE" button kept its desktop size (128px wide, 45px tall). The measured content area ran from x=24 to x=366 (24px side margins). The extractor recorded no visible header links at 390px and did not detect a menu toggle; the navigation pattern at mobile was not measured. Breakpoint widths were not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Begin with white `#ffffff`, body text `#130032bf` at 18px or 24px/36px, headings `#130032`.
2. Build the hero as a vertical gradient from `#3903ae` to `#26065d` with an 80px/300 white headline at -1.5px tracking.
3. Primary actions: `#4c00ff`, 8px radius; uppercase 14px/600 in the header, 16px/500 sentence case in content.
4. Product sections: 58px/300 title, a filter-tab row, then two-column `#ede5ff` cards with UI vignettes.
5. Close with a violet band and a sand `#f8f3f0` footer with uppercase 12px/600 labels.
6. Verify any new value against a measurement, then run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=docusign`.
