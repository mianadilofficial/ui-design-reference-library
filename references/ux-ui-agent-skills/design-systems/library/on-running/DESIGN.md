## Overview

On's site at on.com is a near-silent, monochrome storefront: a pure `#ffffff` canvas, `#000000` text and
`#000000` pill buttons, with all colour supplied by photography. The homepage capture opens on a full-bleed
editorial photograph (Zendaya campaign) with the white nav floating over it, a 48.448px bold headline
"Zendaya Together with On", a 30.08px regular subheadline, and a single white "Shop now" pill. Below the hero the
page drops onto white: three photographic category tiles (Shoes / Apparel / Accessories) with 4px corners and a
soft two-layer shadow, then a typographic sport index — "Soccer →, Running →, Marathon →" — set as 48.448px bold
links stacked in a left column beside a large image.

There is no brand accent colour in the UI chrome. Interactive elements are black (on light) or white (on dark),
and hover states move through greys (`#333333`, `#666666`, `#e5e5e5`, `#cccccc`). The only recurring chromatic
UI colours are the amber `#995c00` On Mono product labels ("Bestseller", "New color"), the warm grey-pink
`#f0eceb` product image backdrop, and a deep night `#151522` full-bleed colour block lower on the homepage.

Typography carries the identity. Every glyph is the served family `On` — files named `OnDiatypeStandard-*`,
which Dinamo documents as **On Diatype**, a 2023 customisation of Diatype with perfectly circular round letters
([abcdinamo.com/custom/on](https://abcdinamo.com/custom/on)). Display sizes are bold (700) with negative
tracking of exactly -1% of the font size (48.448px → -0.48448px, 38.304px → -0.38304px), while uppercase
`On Mono` with wide tracking marks utility text: breadcrumbs, result counts, product labels.

Microcopy voice is short and declarative: "Don't miss a step", "Shop now", "Show filters (2)", "101 Results",
"Stay in the loop, with exclusive offers and product previews". Sentence case everywhere except the mono labels.

Key characteristics:
- Monochrome chrome: `#000000` on `#ffffff`, greys only for state.
- Pill buttons with a 40px radius and fixed 48px height.
- Fluid type via CSS `clamp()`; measured values at 1280px are non-integers (15.104px, 23.552px, 48.448px).
- Photography is full-bleed and edge-to-edge; UI never decorates it with gradients or illustration.
- Uppercase On Mono for metadata, never for headlines.

### Principles
- **Fluid sizes.** The stylesheet declares sizes as `clamp()`; e.g. `clamp(.875rem,.84rem + .13vw,1rem)` resolves
  to 15.104px at 1280px, `clamp(1rem,.94rem + .26vw,1.25rem)` to 18.368px, `clamp(1.25rem,1.16rem + .39vw,1.625rem)` to
  23.552px, `clamp(1.625rem,1.52rem + .45vw,2.0625rem)` to 30.08px and `clamp(2.0625rem,1.93rem + .58vw,2.625rem)` to
  38.304px. Reproduce the clamp, not only the 1280px snapshot.
- **-1% tracking on bold display.** 61.696px uses -1.23392px (-2%); 48.448px, 38.304px and 30.08px use exactly -1%.
- **Bold, not light.** Headlines are 700; 400 is reserved for body and subheadlines; 500 for chips and category links.
- **Mono = metadata.** Uppercase On Mono with 0.12em tracking (12px → 1.44px, 14px → 1.68px) for labels and crumbs.

### Note on Font Substitutes
On Diatype is proprietary to On (Dinamo custom, per [abcdinamo.com/custom/on](https://abcdinamo.com/custom/on)).
The documented base is Dinamo's commercial ABC Diatype — the licensed substitute closest to it; for the mono cuts,
ABC Diatype Mono / Semi Mono. Without a licence, the served fallback stack (`system-ui`, `roboto`) is what visitors
see when the webfont fails; keep weights 400/500/700 and the -1% display tracking.

## Layout

### Spacing System
- Measured gaps: 8px (most frequent), 24px, 4px, 12px, 16px, 32px, 64px, 40px.
- Measured paddings: 4px, 12px, 8px, 24px, 42.624px, 16px, 40px, 32px, 64px.
- Tokens: `xxs` 4px, `xs` 8px, `sm` 12px, `md` 16px, `lg` 24px, `xl` 32px, `xxl` 40px, `section` 64px.

- Header left inset `nav-inset` 26.624px; header height 90px.

### Grid & Container
- Full-bleed: hero billboard (1280×900 in the probe) and colour blocks span the viewport.
- Category tiles: three equal columns inside the gutter with 4px corners.
- Product grid (PLP): square `#f0eceb` image containers, name / descriptor / price stacked under each.
- Sport index: a left text column of 48.448px links next to a large right-hand image.

### Whitespace Philosophy
White space is generous between sections (64px paddings in the footer, large vertical gaps between homepage
modules) and tight inside components (4px–8px gaps in product cards). Imagery does the visual work; the UI
stays out of the way.

## Design System Architecture

On publishes no public design system, token package or brand-guideline portal that I could find. Searches
recorded in SOURCES.json ("github on-running design system tokens npm", "On running \"design system\" engineering
blog frontend components", "on.com press kit brand guidelines logo On AG" and others) returned nothing official,
and `https://www.on.com/en-us/press` returned no content. What follows is only what the shipped site and the type
foundry document.

- **Typeface (official source):** Dinamo's case study ([abcdinamo.com/custom/on](https://abcdinamo.com/custom/on))
  documents On Diatype (2023, Renan Rosatti and Fabian Harb): Regular, Mono, Semi Mono and an added Black;
  geometric-circle rounds, simplified punctuation, a `[logo]` contextual alternate that inserts the On logo, and
  pairing guidance for Japanese, Chinese and Hangul. The site's fallback stack begins with "Noto Sans JP" and
  "Noto Sans KR".
- **Token architecture (de facto):** there are no global design-token custom properties on `:root` (rootVars hold
  only `--ticker-height`, `--sb-width`, `--adjust-banner-height` and a Swiper variable). Tokens live per
  component as scoped custom properties, e.g. the field component in the homepage's inline CSS:
  `--field-background-color`, `--field-hover-background-color`, `--field-label-color`, `--field-border-color`,
  `--field-focus-color`; and `--footer-text-color` / `--footer-text-hover-color`, `--editorial-card-padding`,
  `--headline-ctas-justification`. Colours are otherwise hard-coded hex in component CSS.
- **Component model (de facto):** CSS modules with hashed class names (`._button_sfcvn_29`, `._input_1j5dp_18`,
  `._label_1d3gn_102`) served from `/_nuxt/` bundles. Variants are modifier classes on the same module — button
  `_light`, `_dark`, `_outlined`, `_outlinedDark`, `_gray`, `_link`; sizes default and `_extraSmall`; shape
  `_notRounded`, `_iconOnly`; layout `_fullWidth`, `_hasIcon`, `_rightIcon`; states `_disabled`, `_loading`
  ([entry.Dhw1mVcX.css](https://www.on.com/_nuxt/entry.Dhw1mVcX.css)).
- **Theming:** no site-wide dark mode was observed. Surface-aware variants are chosen per component: buttons
  `_light`/`_dark`, fields `_lightHighContrast`/`_dark`/`_darkHighContrast`, nav switching its `--navigation-list-color`
  between `#fff` and `#000`.
- **Type & spacing:** fluid `clamp()` font sizes in rem (listed under Typography); spacing in rem multiples of
  .25rem (4px) in component CSS (e.g. `padding:0 1.5rem`, `height:3rem`, `margin-left:1.0625rem` is an exception).
- **Breakpoints:** `min-width:768px`, `min-width:1024px` (most frequent, 122 rules), `min-width:1440px`,
  `min-width:2500px`, plus `hover:hover` guards on hover styles.
- **Accessibility (observed, not a published mandate):** `:focus-visible` rings `0 0 0 .25rem #2f7efe` on buttons and
  `0 0 0 .0625rem` on fields with a transparent outline fallback; `.srOnly` text ("Press Escape to close navigation",
  "Skip to main content"); 48px default button and chip height; high-contrast field themes.
- **Delivery:** none public — no npm package, Figma kit or versioned docs were found.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use `#000000` pills (40px radius, 48px tall, 0px 24px padding, 16px/700) for every primary action.
- Flip to the white `button-secondary` on photography; hover `#e5e5e5`.
- Set all text in `On` (On Diatype) and keep display type bold with -1% tracking.
- Use uppercase `On Mono` with 0.12em tracking for crumbs, counts and product labels.
- Let full-bleed photography supply colour; keep the chrome black, white and grey.
- Put product shots on the `#f0eceb` square backdrop.
- Show focus with the 4px `#2f7efe` ring on buttons.

### Don't
- Don't introduce a brand accent colour for buttons or links — On's action colour is black.
- Don't round inputs; fields are square (0px) with a translucent `#0000000d` fill.
- Don't use light (300) weights or positive tracking on headlines.
- Don't use the amber `#995c00` outside product labels.
- Don't add gradients, glows or drop shadows to buttons.
- Don't set headlines in On Mono or in uppercase.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from white `#ffffff` and black `#000000`; add no accent colour.
2. Load On Diatype (or licensed ABC Diatype) as family `On` at 400/500/700, plus a mono at 400/500.
3. Build type with the `clamp()` formulas; check the 1280px results match 15.104px, 18.368px, 23.552px, 30.08px,
   38.304px.
4. Build one button module with variants (black, white, outlined, gray, link) and sizes 48px / 40px, radius 40px.
5. Inputs: 64px, square, `#0000000d` fill, floating label, 1px black focus ring, `#ed0000` invalid.
6. Product cards: `#f0eceb` square, 15.104px bold name, 15.104px regular descriptor, 13.184px price, amber mono label.
7. Compose pages as full-bleed photography + white sections; check a capture — the eye should land on images and
   black type only.
8. Verify focus rings (`#2f7efe`, 4px) and hover greys (`#333333` on black, `#e5e5e5` on white).
