## Overview

Floyd is a Detroit furniture company ("DESIGNED IN DETROIT, MADE FOR KEEPING." reads the homepage eyebrow; "Since 2013, we've developed modular, adaptable furniture systems…" the headline beside it). Its site looks like a well-set product catalogue: a warm cream page (`{colors.canvas}` — `#f8f6ed`, which the site itself calls `--color-vintage`, `--color-reverse`, `--color-light` and `--color-floyd-background`), brand-black text and buttons (`{colors.ink}` — `#231e1e`, `--color-brand-black` / `--color-primary` / `--color-ink`), and natural-light photography of oak furniture in lived-in rooms.

Type is the system's personality. Large statements are set in a sans served under the font-family name **Regular** at 32–40px with tight negative tracking (-1.28px to -1.6px, i.e. -0.04em). Small labels are monospaced and uppercase ("HOW IT WORKS: THE BED SYSTEM", "01 - CHOOSE", "MEMBER PROGRAM") in faces served as **Mono** and **GT America Mono**. The custom **Floyd Gothic**, drawn by Order from Youngstown factory signage ([order.design](https://order.design/project/floyd)), sets the navigation, product-page feature headings and the (visually hidden) page h1.

Buttons are dark and almost square: the Add To Cart is `#231e1e` with cream `#f8f6ed` GT America Mono 16px/700 text, 51px tall and a 5px radius; the newsletter "Continue" button has a 1px radius and lightens to `{colors.primary-hover}` `#75736c` on hover. Links are underlined text with a "→".

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm cream canvas `#f8f6ed`; brand-black `#231e1e` for ink and buttons; cream text on dark buttons (never pure white).
- Big, tightly tracked sans statements (40px / 36px / 32px at -0.04em) — calm, editorial, left-aligned.
- Monospaced uppercase eyebrows and numbered steps in grey `#7e7b71`.
- Near-square geometry: 1px, 3px and 5px radii; 24px only on review-widget filter chips.
- Underlined text links with arrows instead of secondary buttons.
- Flat — the only shadow measured is a 1px 3px 8%-black lift on the collection grid.
- Three-up "Choose / Add / Change" step modules with photography above numbered mono labels.

### Imagery & Art Direction
- Photography is daylight interiors: oak platform beds, white linen, plaster walls, plants, rugs; people appear mid-task (making a bed, assembling a frame on the floor).
- Images are full-bleed in the hero and edge-to-edge in three equal columns in the step module, with small gutters.
- The hero carries a monospaced caption in cream along its bottom edge: "THE BED FRAME, ORIGINAL IN 'OAK/BLACK' WITH ADJUSTABLE HEADBOARD ADD-ON".
- Colour comes from wood, textiles and plants rather than from the UI.

### Voice of Microcopy
- Calm and practical: "Start with the frame that works for you.", "Add what you need.", "Change it over time."
- Proof points stated plainly: "Named Wirecutter's 'Best Modular Bed Frame' five years running."
- Labels are numbered and monospaced: "01 - CHOOSE", "02 - ADD", "03 - CHANGE".
- Links are verb + object + arrow: "Shop The Bed System →", "View Tour", "Learn More".

### Principles
- **Negative tracking scales with size**: about -0.04em on 32–40px statements, -0.015em on 18–20px body.
- **Mono for metadata, sans for meaning**: anything that numbers, labels or captions is monospaced; sentences are sans.
- **Weights stay light**: 300 and 400 dominate; 500 for link emphasis and mono labels; 700 only in buttons.

### Note on Font Substitutes
Floyd Gothic is proprietary (Order NY for Floyd). GT America and GT America Mono are Grilli Type retail families. The site's own declared fallbacks are Arial / Helvetica for Floyd Gothic and GT America Mono, and Roboto / monospace for "Mono"; use those as served. No other substitute is endorsed by Floyd.

## Design System Architecture

Floyd publishes **no public design system** or token package. Searches for "Floyd furniture Detroit brand identity 'Floyd Gothic' typeface", "Floyd design system", "Floyd brand guidelines" and "floydhome press kit" found the identity work by Order ([order.design/project/floyd](https://order.design/project/floyd)), which states Order provided "robust brand guidelines to Floyd's inhouse design team" — those guidelines are not public — and Print magazine's piece on the 2021 Floyd Gothic Inktrap ([printmag.com](https://www.printmag.com/?p=354)). Floyd's own blog post on the fonts (`floydhome.com/blogs/livedin/graphic-standards-floyd-font`) returned 404. What follows is the de-facto system measured on the site.

- **Token architecture**: 51 `:root` custom properties in a flat `--color-*` / `--*-font` / `--z*` / `--duration-*` naming scheme. Two tiers are visible: brand primitives (`--color-brand-black: #231E1E`, `--color-vintage: #F8F6ED`, `--color-brand-gray: #7E7B71`, `--color-floyd-red: #EF4123`, `--color-floyd-green: #386641`, `--color-membership-green: #D5FA44`) and semantic aliases pointing at the same values (`--color-primary`, `--color-ink`, `--color-reverse`, `--color-light`, `--color-text-secondary`, `--color-subdued`, `--color-error`, `--color-success`, `--color-focus`, `--color-tertiary`). Fonts are tokens too (`--floyd-font`, `--floyd-inktrap`, `--america-mono-font`, `--akademie-font`, `--inter-font`), as are z-index layers (`--zbehind: -1`, `--zbase: 1`, `--zflyout: 5`, `--zmodal: 101`, `--zhighest: 9999`) and durations.
- **Theming**: no dark or high-contrast mode observed. `--color-reverse` equals the cream, used as text on dark buttons.
- **Type & spacing**: a sans for statements, two monos for metadata, the custom gothic for nav and product features; negative tracking proportional to size. Spacing clusters on 5 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 20 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 80px.
- **Component model**: dark near-square buttons, underlined arrow links, mono option tags, step modules, member band, newsletter modal; product reviews come from a widget whose variables are prefixed `--kl-reviews-*`.
- **Accessibility**: a "Skip to content" link is first in the DOM; `--color-focus: #1351ee` exists as a focus colour. No published standard found.
- **Delivery**: no npm package, Figma kit or public repo found.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use the cream `#f8f6ed` page with `#231e1e` text; put cream (not white) text on dark buttons.
- Set statements large and tight (32–40px, -0.04em) and left-aligned.
- Label with monospaced uppercase eyebrows and numbered steps in `#7e7b71`.
- Keep corners near-square: 1px, 3px, 5px.
- Use underlined text links with "→" for secondary actions.
- Let daylight interior photography carry colour.

### Don't
- Don't use pure white as the page colour on marketing pages (white is for inputs and the PDP body).
- Don't round buttons into pills.
- Don't add shadows outside the collection grid.
- Don't set long body copy in mono.
- Don't use the red/green accent tokens as button colours — buttons are brand-black.
- Don't track statements positively.

- Body copy drops from 18px/22.5px to 16px/20px (-0.16px).
- The hidden h1 drops from 64px to 48px.
