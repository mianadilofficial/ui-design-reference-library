## Overview

Global House (globalhouse.co.th, page title "Global House — วัสดุก่อสร้าง ของตกแต่งบ้าน | โกลบอลเฮ้าส์") is a Thai building-materials and home-decor retailer, and its home page is a dense, promotion-heavy online store. The capture shows a full-width promotional banner strip at the very top, a dark green utility bar (hotline 1160, branch selector, help center, TH / EN switch, dark-mode toggle), a rounded hero carousel with a "GLOBAL HOUSE APP" panel and a members' coupon box, then a quick-link row of campaign icons, a red "Flash Sale ประจำวัน" band with a countdown and scrolling product cards, a "หมวดหมู่สินค้า" (product categories) grid of photo tiles with two-line Thai labels, a "ช้อปตามงาน" (shop by job) mosaic of dark-overlaid photographs, a brand logo strip, and a "สินค้าแนะนำ" (recommended products) grid of white product cards with yellow add-to-cart buttons.

The capture also shows a fraud-warning modal in the center of the screen with a dark scrim, and a cookie banner at the bottom of the first screen. Those are overlays, not brand UI, and are not documented here as tokens. The extractor's h1 guess is the number "100" from a coupon badge, which is overridden in SOURCES.json; the display token is the "หมวดหมู่สินค้า" section heading instead.

The voice is practical and promotional: short Thai nouns for categories, discount percentages, "ซื้อเลย" (buy now) buttons, countdown timers and sold-count lines such as "ขายแล้ว 47 ชิ้น เหลือ 53" (sold 47, 53 left).

The de-facto system is utility-first with semantic variables.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use yellow `#ffcb4b` with `#1a1a1a` text for every primary action.
- Do use green `#2c5640` for structural bands and red `#e20720` for sale prices and urgency.
- Do keep cards white with an 18px radius and a hairline ring.
- Do write Thai labels in IBM Plex Sans Thai and use Sarabun for numeric badges.
- Don't use white text on yellow; use `#1a1a1a`.
- Don't document the fraud-warning modal, scrims or the cookie banner as brand UI.
- Don't treat Tailwind's default palette variables as brand colors.
