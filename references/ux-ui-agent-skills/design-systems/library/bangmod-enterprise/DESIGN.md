## Overview

`https://bangmod.cloud/` is the Thai-language storefront of Bangmod.Cloud, which its footer credits as "© 2026 Bangmod.Cloud by Bangmod Enterprise Co., Ltd." (บริษัท บางมด เอ็นเตอร์ไพร์ส จำกัด). The page title is "Bangmod.Cloud – ผู้ให้บริการ Cloud & Hosting ชั้นนำของไทย". This file is named after the queued brand, Bangmod Enterprise, with the measured site, Bangmod.Cloud, in brackets.

The home page is a long single scroll (8,768px at 1280px wide): a two-row header (a thin dark top bar with a phone number and utility links, then the main bar with logo, English-labelled mega-menu entries Services, Products, Solutions, Help & Support and About Us, a "เข้าสู่ระบบ" log-in link and a blue "สมัครสมาชิก" button); an electric-blue hero with the headline "Cloud คุณภาพสูง ดูแลโดยทีมงานมืออาชีพ", two buttons and an illustration; a white domain-search card overlapping the hero edge; a "บริการของ Bangmod.Cloud" services grid; a navy ISO/IEC 27001:2022 band; five annual WordPress hosting price cards; feature, partner and article sections; a call-to-action band; and a navy footer.

Colour is split three ways: white content areas (the most common colour in the capture, 33% of pixels), a deep navy `#000147` (16%, and the largest background by DOM area, which is why it is the `canvas` token), and a saturated blue `#182eb8` hero. Amber `#ffb000` is reserved for the main action. The copy is promotional and technical: "NVMe SSD WordPress Hosting", uptime and support promises, and price in baht. Small "Hot" and "Soon" tags mark grid cells. The floating chat prompt ("ต้องการแชท?") and the cookie-settings control are widget chrome and are not documented as brand UI.

## Design System Architecture

No public design-system or brand-guideline page was found: the home page links no brand guidelines, design system or GitHub organisation (only a header logo image and an About page at `https://bangmod.cloud/about/`). The de-facto system is a WordPress theme with Elementor: the stylesheet references `--e-global-typography-*` font-family variables and the `eicons` font, and `:root` also carries `--direction-multiplier` and `--page-title-display`. Brand values are therefore set in the page's own CSS rather than as a published token set: the amber and blue buttons, the navy bands and the six-layer card shadow repeat across sections. Class and variable naming for brand tokens was not measured beyond the `--bm-*` properties noted under Colors.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep amber `{colors.primary}` for the single main action and navy `{colors.on-primary}` text on it.
- Do use white content sections between blue and navy bands; headings are navy, body copy is the grey-blue `{colors.ink}` or `{colors.ink-body}`.
- Do keep corners at 3px on buttons and 10px on cards.
- Do tint shadows blue (`rgba(24, 46, 184, ...)`) rather than black.
- Don't document the cookie-settings button, chat prompt or consent controls as brand components.
- Don't use `--bm-primary` (`#0066CC`), `--bm-accent` (`#00c48c`) or `--bm-danger` (`#e74c3c`) as page colours; they were not measured on the visible page.
- Don't add a second accent beside amber and blue.
- Don't mix in a second brand typeface: the measured page uses Bangmod DB alone.

By characters the most used mobile sizes are 21px (2,634), 18px (1,525), 26.88px (787), 17.9999px (236) and 46.2px (224). Breakpoint values, grid column counts, menu behaviour and image treatment are not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Set `canvas` `#000147`, `page-body` `#ffffff`, `primary` `#ffb000`, `secondary` `#182eb8` and load Bangmod DB (400, 500, 700) with Arial as fallback.
2. Build a hero on `{colors.secondary}` with a 69.9999px / 700 white headline, one amber and one outline button.
3. Overlap a white search card (51px input beside a blue button) on the hero edge.
4. Lay services as a three-column bordered grid with `hairline` dividers and 10px-radius cards.
5. Use the six-layer 4%-alpha shadow on cards and the `rgba(24, 46, 184, 0.3) 0px 2px 10px 0px` glow on buttons.
6. Close with a navy footer; check mobile against the 390px values above and mark anything else as not measured.
