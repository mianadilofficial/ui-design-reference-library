# ClickUp

## Overview

The measured page is `https://clickup.com/`, titled "ClickUp™ | Maximize productivity • Software, AI, and humans converge". It is the marketing home for ClickUp's all-in-one work platform, opening with "One Workspace built to think and work" and a black "Get started. It's FREE!" button above a large product screenshot of the ClickUp app (task list, chat and docs panes).

The page is long (12,553px) and moves between three moods. First, a light, neutral, product-first section on white and `#f8f9fa`: a grid of app icons ("All apps, AI Agents, and humans in ClickUp"), diagrams about context ("60% of work is lost in context – and AI is lost without it"). Second, a vivid AI moment: "A new era of humans, with Super Agents" with a glossy gradient mask object and a human silhouette over a warm orange-to-pink gradient. Third, a full black `#000000` "Brain²" section ("The best AI is your AI", "Nothing comes close to Brain²", "The only AI that actually knows your work") with dark cards and purple glows. The page returns to white for use-case tabs ("AI solutions for every team"), statistics ("It's like adding 15 full-time employees" — 384%, $3.9M, 92,400, +4mo), video testimonials, G2 badges ("#1 most reviewed company on G2 reports"), an enterprise strip and a closing gradient band ("All your work, all your people, all powered by AI").

ClickUp's brand page (https://clickup.com/brand) defines the identity: a four-colour gradient (pink `#FF02F0`, orange `#FC6D2D`, purple `#6647F0`, blue `#0091FF`) and three typefaces — Plus Jakarta Sans for headlines, Inter for body, Sometype Mono for captions and illustrations. On the marketing page the gradient is reserved for the logo, the AI imagery and the closing band; the UI itself is monochrome.

## Typography

Loaded web fonts: **Inter** (variable 100–900, roman and italic), **Plus Jakarta Sans** (variable 200–800, roman and italic) and **Sometype Mono** (variable 400–700). "SF Pro" and `-apple-system` appear inside product mock-ups. By character count: Inter 3,776, Plus Jakarta Sans 1,279, SF Pro 832, Sometype Mono 689. All three brand faces are free Google Fonts.

- **display** — Plus Jakarta Sans 60px / 700 / 66px, -2.1px. The h1.
- **heading** — Plus Jakarta Sans 48px / 650 / 60px, -1.68px. Section h2s.
- **subheading** — Plus Jakarta Sans 26px / 650 / 32.5px, -0.91px. Product tiles ("Projects").
- **text** — Inter 14px / 500. The most-read size and weight for marketing copy.
- **body** — SF Pro 12px / 500 / 18px. Text inside product screenshots (the extractor's body-text sample is a mock "Growth & Acquisition Update").
- **button** — Inter 18px / 650 / 24px, -0.36px. **button-small** — Inter 14px / 600 / 16px, -0.15px.
- **label-mono** — Sometype Mono 14px / 500 / 18px, uppercase. Small labels such as "IOS" and section kickers.

The Brain section's header uses Plus Jakarta Sans 80px / 700 / 96px, -3.2px. Weights in use: 500, 400, 650, 600, 700, 590 — the variable fonts allow the in-between 650 that ClickUp uses for most headings. Tracking is consistently negative on headings (about -3.5% of size).

## Design System Architecture

ClickUp publishes brand guidelines at https://clickup.com/brand ("Brand Assets & Guidelines"): the four gradient colours with RGB values, the three typefaces with their roles, logo rules ("Our logo can appear in black or white when necessary"; don't change the colour, outline, stretch, rotate, use on coloured backgrounds or use without the symbol), clear-space guidance and a downloadable brand kit (`/assets/brand/v4/clickup-brand-assets.zip`). No public component library or token documentation was found.

The shipped CSS has 400 root variables in several families: core tokens (`--Core-Text-Secondary`, `--color-core-text-main`, `--Core-Utilities-Red: #f0382d`), text styles split into properties (`--text-display-md--font-weight: 650`, `--text-display-lg--letter-spacing: -1.6px`, `--text-sm--letter-spacing: -.15px`), radii (`--radius-radius-12`, `--radius-media` 14px, `--radius-masonry-media` 9px, `--border-radius-md` 20px), easing (`--easing-natural`, `--easing-bounce`), breakpoints, and a large `--ProductScreen-*` set used to render the in-page app mock-ups (status greens, calendar accents, whiteboard nodes). Several colour variables are RGB triplets for alpha composition (`--color-purple: 123,104,238`).

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Keep the UI monochrome: white/`#f8f9fa` surfaces, `#202020` buttons, `#646464` copy.
- Use Plus Jakarta Sans 650–700 with tight negative tracking for headings; Inter for UI; Sometype Mono uppercase for small labels.
- Reserve the pink-orange-purple-blue gradient for the logo, AI imagery and one closing band.
- Use a full black section to stage the AI product.
- Round buttons 8–15px; round chips fully.

**Don't**
- Don't put the logo on coloured backgrounds, recolour, outline, stretch or rotate it (brand page rules).
- Don't fill primary buttons with a gradient or brand colour; the measured CTA is `#202020`.
- Don't use pure black for body text on white; it is `#646464`.
- Don't use heavy shadows; product cards use a hairline plus a 2px blur.
- Don't swap Sometype Mono into body copy; it is for captions and illustrations.

## Responsive Behavior

The mobile pass at 390px recorded: one visible header link and a menu toggle labelled "Open mobile menu"; the h1 text changed to "Software to replace all software" at Plus Jakarta Sans 38px / 650 / 44px in `#333333`; h2 at 34px / 650 / 40.8px; the CTA "Get started. It's FREE" at 18px/650, `12px 24px`, 48px tall, 12px radius; at most three card columns. Bands ran from x=20 to x=372 (white, a 4,481px media/dark section, white, media, white). The most-read mobile size became 12px. Breakpoints in CSS are declared (`--breakpoint-desktop: 72.5rem`, `--breakpoint-xxl: 106.25rem`); behaviour at those widths was not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with white, a translucent white header (`#ffffffe6`), and a 60px/700 Plus Jakarta Sans headline in `#202020`.
2. Add the black CTA: 52px, 15px radius, Inter 18px/650; hover to 80% opacity.
3. Set supporting copy in Inter 14px/500 `#646464`, with mono uppercase kickers in `#838383`.
4. Show the product in white cards with the hairline shadow.
5. Stage AI features in a full black section with charcoal cards and white buttons.
6. Use the brand gradient (`#ff02f0` → `#fc6d2d` → `#6647f0` → `#0091ff`) only for hero objects and the final band.
7. Follow the brand page for any logo placement.
