# Palo Alto Networks

## Overview

paloaltonetworks.com ("Leader in Cybersecurity Protection & Software for the Modern Enterprises") is a very long,
dark, cinematic page — 16,742px at desktop — built from scroll-driven storytelling sections. A black strip at the top
announces "Introducing Cortex XCOR: Observability, reimagined. Discover now." The header carries the orange
"paloalto NETWORKS" logo, Products, Solutions, Services, Industries, Partners, Resources, search and language icons,
an outlined "Accounts & Support" pill and an orange "Demos and Trials" pill.

The hero is a photographic scene — a person in a dark hoodie against glowing data waves — with an uppercase eyebrow
("CORTEX XCOR"), the headline "Ask. Remediate. Optimize." in TT Hoves 500 at 44px, a subline, an orange "Explore Cortex
XCOR →" pill and an underlined "Read the blog" link. Under it a #190000 red-black "RECOMMENDED" strip carries a
carousel of article cards, followed by an in-page nav (Why Palo Alto Networks?, Platforms, Solutions, Proven
Success, Engage with Us, Demos and trials).

The body is a chain of dark scroll scenes with orange line art and particle fields: "A new AI world is here",
statistics ("78%", "~1.5X growth", "56%" increases in exploits and ransomware) with orange bars, "Platformization
empowers you…", platform sections for Strata, Prisma, Cortex (each with its own product color), "So you can defend at
speed and scale", Unit 42 ("Intelligence-driven. Response-ready."), customer logos, event cards ("Ignite on Tour",
"Executive Briefing Center", "Under Attack? We're Here."), a newsletter form and a white legal footer. Tone: big,
declarative, AI-forward enterprise security.

## Colors

ramp (0 #FFBF9C, 250 #FF724D, 750 #B23808, 1000 #190000) and GTM product colors: Strata Security Yellow #FFCB06,
Prisma Cloud Blue #00C0E8, Cortex Intelligence Green #00CC66, Unit 42 Fire Red #C84727 and Idira Identity Blue
#265BFF.

## Design System Architecture

Palo Alto Networks publishes a brand page at https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/brand with logo downloads (parent brand,
Cortex, Prisma, Strata, Idira, Unit 42) and the Brand Guidelines PDF
(https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/content/dam/creative-assets/brand-assets/guidelines/Palo_Alto_Networks_Brand_Guidelines_July2026.pdf).
The guidelines define Cyber Orange and its ramp, the GTM product colors (and say not to mix them), Figtree as the
primary font with Source Serif for long texts and Noto Sans for other scripts, and CTA button rules: font size equals
1/3 of the button height, padding 1/5 of the width per side, a 2pt stroke for the outline style, and uppercase button
text. The live home page uses sentence-case button labels and TT Hoves, so it does not follow the July 2026 type
rules.

The site's CSS declares Bootstrap-style variables (`--breakpoint-sm 576px`, `--breakpoint-md 768px`, `--breakpoint-lg 992px`,
`--breakpoint-xl 1200px`, `--font-family-sans-serif`) alongside the brand font variables.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use orange pills (#f76a43, 36px radius, black text) for primary actions; hover to #f98768.
- Use Cyber Orange #fa582d for eyebrows, highlights and the logo.
- Keep storytelling on #141414 / #000000 with white TT Hoves 500 headlines and orange light art.
- Give each platform its GTM color (Strata yellow, Prisma blue, Cortex green, Unit 42 red) and never mix them, per the
  guidelines.
- Use small (12.8px) type for legal and supporting copy and 19.5556px for sublines.

**Don't**
- Don't use the Bootstrap blue #007bff that appears on an unstyled nav link; it is not a brand color.
- Don't square off buttons.
- Don't add heavy shadows; depth is light and imagery.
- Don't mix product colors in one lockup.

   orange pill with an arrow and an underlined text link.
3. Add a #190000 recommended strip with thumbnail cards and a sticky section nav.
4. Tell the story in dark scroll scenes with orange line art and large statistics.
5. Color-code platform sections with the GTM colors; keep cards flat with 20% white hairlines and 8px radii.
6. End with event cards, a CelestePro newsletter field and a white legal footer; at mobile, 28px display and 35px pills.
