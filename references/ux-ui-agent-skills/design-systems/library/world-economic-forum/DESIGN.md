# World Economic Forum

## Overview

The World Economic Forum home page (https://www.weforum.org/, title "The World Economic Forum") is an editorial and programme portal for the international organisation. The measured page opens on a saturated blue field (#0065f2 shading toward #003d91) with a white AkkuratLLWeb headline, "Connecting leaders to make sense of global challenges and move the world forward together", a translucent pill button "More about the Forum" with an arrow, and a rounded video tile marked "Watch full video" showing speakers at microphones. A thin white bar carries a stacked "WORLD ECONOMIC FORUM" wordmark at the centre, a menu icon at the left and two pill buttons, "Join us" (solid blue) and "Sign in" (white outline), at the right. Below the hero sit a tabbed "How we drive impact" carousel (Centres, Meetings, Stakeholders) with a white intro card "We tackle global challenges through our 11 Centres" beside photographic cards such as "Centre for AI Excellence" and "Centre for Cybersecurity"; a "Spotlight" section of story cards with uppercase category labels (ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, ECONOMIC GROWTH, GLOBAL COOPERATION, PODCASTS, EQUITY, DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION); and a dark photographic band "Latest analysis on war in the Middle East". A floating "日本語" language tab sits at the left edge. Scroll height at 1280px is 5426px.

The extraction was flagged "blocked: true" in the queue, but the measured capture has real content (5773 characters of text), so the measurements are of the actual site.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do set headlines in AkkuratLLWeb 900 and body copy in 700 at 16px.
- Do use the #0065f2 blue for the hero and the single filled call to action.
- Do use pill buttons for secondary actions and 12px radii for cards.
- Do label every story with a small uppercase tracked category.
- Don't use the browser link colour #0000ee.
- Don't use heavy shadows; the measured shadow is a soft two-layer 8 percent and 4 percent black.
- Don't put dense text over photographs without a dark scrim; the measured dark bands carry white text.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with a #0065f2 hero, white AkkuratLLWeb 900 headline at the `display` token, and a translucent pill secondary button.
2. Add the white nav bar with centred wordmark, a solid "Join us" button and an outlined "Sign in".
3. Build story cards with 12px radius, an uppercase 14px tracked category and a 900-weight headline.
4. Use dark photographic bands with white headlines for topical features.
5. Run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=world-economic-forum` after edits.
6. Section order measured from the capture, for composing a similar page: blue hero with video tile, white sticky bar, "How we drive impact" tab carousel with an intro card and image cards carrying white titles, "Spotlight" with a "More Stories" outlined pill, a two-up lead row, a three-up row with play and podcast badges on thumbnails, then a dark topical band with horizontally scrolling story tiles marked with minutes-to-read labels.
7. Copy voice is plain and declarative: sentence-case headlines, the verb first in calls to action ("Watch full video", "More about our Centres", "Join us", "Sign in"), and a short descriptive sentence under each section title.
8. The language switch for Japanese is a small white tab docked at the left viewport edge, separate from the main header controls.
