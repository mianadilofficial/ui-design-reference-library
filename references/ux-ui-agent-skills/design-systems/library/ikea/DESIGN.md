## Overview

IKEA's US storefront is a large, densely merchandised retail site that still reads as calm: a pure white
`#ffffff` canvas, near-black `#111111` type, and almost all colour delegated to photography and a small set of
commercial signals. Every glyph is set in **Noto IKEA**, served in two weights only (400 and 700). The page is
assembled from Skapa components (IKEA's internal design system — the `--skapa-focus-ring-internal` variable and
`hnf-btn`, `pipf-btn`, `hri-*` class prefixes are visible in the DOM), and that discipline shows: there is one
pill radius (64px), one grey (`#f5f5f5`) for every soft surface, and a blue (`#0058a3`) reserved for the one
action that matters on a product page — **Add to bag**.

The capture shows the rhythm from top to bottom:

- A 48px black `#111111` utility bar ("US | English", "Shop new arrivals", "Enter ZIP code", "Select store") in white 14px.
- A white header: the blue-and-yellow IKEA logo at left, five 14px `#484848` text links (Products, Rooms, Deals,
  Design & ideas, Services), a wide `#f5f5f5` pill search field ("What are you looking for?") with a camera icon,
  and three icon buttons (account, favourites, bag).
- A horizontally scrolling row of `#f5f5f5` category pills, each with a small cut-out product thumbnail and a
  bold 14px label ("Storage & organization", "Sofas & armchairs", "Lighting"…).
- A **bento grid** of square-cornered photo tiles separated by 12px gutters: one large lifestyle photo with a white
  24px bold headline and a right arrow, smaller tiles, and a solid `#007cc1` IKEA Family tile ("Sign in to IKEA
  Family or IKEA Business Network to save") with a white circular arrow button.
- A tiny 14px `#484848` legal line under the grid ("See full Terms and Conditions." underlined).
- A horizontal product-category carousel: a `#f5f5f5` text card ("Holiday hosting? This year, your kitchen will
  shine.") with a circled arrow, followed by portrait photos with 14px bold captions and arrows, and a thin
  progress track below.
- Another bento row of photos carrying small blue "IKEA Family price" badges and white headlines.

The voice is practical, direct and friendly ("Hej!" greets signed-out users in the header). Headlines are short
sentences in sentence case, with prices and dates inline ("20% off select KUSTFYR décor now thru 10/12"). There
is no decorative typography, no gradients, and no drop shadows on content: hierarchy comes from weight (700
against 400), from negative letter-spacing on bold sizes, and from photography.

## Typography

### Font Family

The served stack is `"Omani Rial", "Emirati Dirham", "Saudi Riyal", "Noto IKEA", "Noto Sans JP", … "Noto Sans",
Roboto, "Open Sans", system-ui, sans-serif` (exposed as `--ikea-font`). The first three names are currency-symbol
fonts placed ahead of the text face; the only text face actually loaded is **Noto IKEA** in 400 and 700
(`document.fonts` reports exactly "Noto IKEA 400 normal" and "Noto IKEA 700 normal"). Some product-page
components declare the shorter `"Noto IKEA", "Noto Sans", Roboto, …` stack.

### Principles

- Two weights only. Bold (700) is for headings, labels, prices and buttons; regular (400) for everything else.
- Bold sizes from 16px up carry **negative tracking that grows with size** (-0.107143px at 16px to -1.39286px at
  40px); 12px labels get slight positive tracking (0.107143px). The odd decimals come from the system's
  em-based tracking values and should be copied exactly.
- 14px is the workhorse: it accounts for most characters on the homepage. Text is never uppercase.

### Note on Font Substitutes

Noto IKEA is IKEA's own build of the Noto family (Google + Monotype); IKEA moved from Verdana to Noto, per
[It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/ikea-changes-typeface-to-noto-monotype-google-graphic-design-210819).
Noto IKEA itself is not distributed publicly. The site's own fallback is **Noto Sans** (open-licensed), which is
the closest available substitute; keep weights at 400/700 and copy the tracking values.

## Layout

### Spacing System

Measured gaps and paddings cluster on a 4px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64. 8px is the most common
padding and gap; 12px is the bento gutter (`.hri-bento-box` gap 12px); 16px pads content cards; 32px pads the
large button horizontally; 48px is the page gutter; 64px separates the footer from content.

### Grid & Container

- Page content sits in a container with 48px side padding (`.hnf-footer__container` padding `0px 48px`,
  max-width 1792px); at 1280px viewport the content column is 1184px wide.
- The hero is a **bento box**: one large tile (about half the width) plus a column of smaller tiles, all
  separated by 12px, with photos cropped to fill (portrait/landscape aspect-ratio boxes) and 0px corners.
- Carousels scroll horizontally with a thin progress track (`#dfdfdf` with a `#111111` thumb) underneath.

### Whitespace Philosophy

The page is dense but orderly: around 64px of empty white between bento sections, tight 12px gutters inside
them. White space is a separator, never decoration — every band contains merchandise.

## Elevation & Depth

The storefront is flat. Content tiles, pills and cards have no shadow. Measured shadows are functional only:

- `rgb(17, 17, 17) 0px 0px 0px 1px inset` — the 1px outline of secondary buttons (drawn with an inset shadow
  instead of a border).
- `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 4px 16px 0px` — floating elements such as dropdown panels (CSS `0 4px 16px #0000001a`).
- Focus: `0 0 0 4px var(--skapa-focus-ring-internal, …)` — a 4px ring is the system-wide focus treatment.
- The `#0000008a` translucent layer is the only measured overlay colour.

## Design System Architecture

IKEA builds its digital products on **Skapa**, its internal design system. Skapa documentation is not public:
[android.skapa.ikea.net](https://android.skapa.ikea.net/) redirects to a Cloudflare Access login, and no Skapa
token or component package is published on the public npm registry (an `@ingka/button` lookup returns 404).
Everything below is therefore what is measurable on the live site, not documented intent.

- **Token architecture (observed)**: two tiers are visible in `:root`. Primitive-ish palette tokens —
  `--colour-neutral-1` … `--colour-neutral-7`, `--colour-static-ikea-brand-blue`, `--colour-extended-palette-01` …
  `-21` — and semantic/role tokens — `--colour-text-and-icon-1` … `-5`, `--colour-elevation-1`,
  `--colour-semantic-positive`, `--colour-commercial-message-new`, `--colour-campaign-sustainability`, and
  interaction tokens with explicit states: `--colour-interactive-emphasised-bg-default` / `-hover` / `-pressed`,
  `--colour-interactive-subtle-border-selected`, `--colour-interactive-disabled-1`. Values are RGB triplets so
  alpha can be applied at use.
- **Theming (observed)**: there are paired `--colour-interactive-*` and `--colour-interactive-static-*` sets, and
  focus rings resolve through `--skapa-focus-ring-internal`. No dark-mode switch was observed on the storefront.
- **Type & spacing**: one face (Noto IKEA, 400/700); component classes expose a named type scale —
  `*-typography-heading-xl`, `heading-l`, `heading-m`, `body-m`, `label-l`, `label-m`, `label-s` — prefixed per
  app (`hnf-` header & footer, `hri-` homepage, `pipf-`/`pipcom-` product page, `plann-` planner). Spacing values
  measured sit on a 4px grid.
- **Component model (observed)**: BEM-style classes with modifiers — `pipf-btn pipf-btn--emphasised
  pipf-btn--fluid`, `pipf-btn--primary`, `pipf-btn--secondary`, `pipf-btn--small`; buttons always pill-shaped, in
  56px (default) and 40px (small) heights.
- **Accessibility (observed)**: skip links ("Skip to main content", "Skip images", "Skip product categories list"),
  an "Accessibility" link first in the tab order, a 4px focus ring, and 48px minimum heights on pills and search.
- **Delivery**: each storefront area ships its own prefixed bundle of the same components (hnf, hri, pipf,
  plann), which is why identical buttons appear under different class prefixes.
- **Brand foundations (official)**: per IKEA's
  [logo history page](https://www.ikea.com/ph/en/this-is-ikea/about-us/the-ikea-logo-history-and-design-pub55d85f50)
  the blue and yellow logo is "the symbol for instant recognition of the IKEA Brand", lettered in an extra bold
  Futura with tiny serifs; the [IKEA Museum](https://www.ikeamuseum.com/en/explore/the-story-of-ikea/history-of-the-logotype/)
  dates the blue-and-yellow logo to the 1970s and the first visual profile manual to 1984.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Use `#ffffff` canvas, `#111111` for headings and `#484848` for running text.
- Reserve `#0058a3` for the single most important commerce action (Add to bag).
- Use 64px pill radius for every button, pill and search field; 0px for photos and tiles.
- Use `#f5f5f5` for every soft surface (pills, search, footer, text cards).
- Set bold headings with the measured negative tracking (e.g. 24px at -0.535714px).
- Let photography carry colour; put white bold headlines and an arrow directly on the photo.
- Use 12px gutters in bento grids and 48px page gutters.

### Don't

- Don't introduce a third font weight or a second typeface.
- Don't round photo tiles or add drop shadows to cards.
- Don't use IKEA yellow `#ffdb00` as a button or large surface colour.
- Don't uppercase labels or add letter-spacing to body copy.
- Don't use blue for secondary actions — those are black `#111111` or outlined.
- Don't draw borders on buttons; outlines are 1px inset shadows.

## Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints

Measured at 1280px and 390px. At 390px the header measures 181px tall (138px on desktop), the
page title drops from 40px to 24px/30px (-0.535714px), promo headings from 28px to 20px/25px, product
description lead from 20px/32px to 16px/26px, and category pills tighten from 12px to 8px padding while keeping
48px height.

### Touch Targets

Pills, search and the utility bar links are 48px tall; the Add to bag button is 56px; small buttons are 40px.

### Collapsing Strategy

The category pill row and product carousels are horizontal scrollers. How the desktop text links and the bento
tiles re-arrange at 390px was not measured, so do not infer it from this file.

### Image Behavior

Images keep their aspect-ratio boxes (portrait / landscape) and crop to fill; product images stay on 0px corners.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with white canvas, Noto Sans (as Noto IKEA substitute) 14px/21px `#484848` text and `#111111` headings.
2. Build the 48px black utility bar and white header with a `#f5f5f5` 48px pill search field.
3. Add the thumbnail category pills (`#f5f5f5`, 64px radius, 14px/700 label).
4. Lay out the bento hero with 12px gutters and 0px photo corners; white 24px/700 headlines on photos.
5. Use `#007cc1` only for IKEA Family content; `#0058a3` only for Add to bag.
6. Check every bold heading carries its measured negative letter-spacing.
7. Finish with the `#f5f5f5` footer (64px top padding, 48px side padding).
