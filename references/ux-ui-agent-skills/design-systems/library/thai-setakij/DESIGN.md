## Overview

The measured site is www.tsi.co.th (title "Home TSI Insurance"), the site of Thai Setakij Insurance, branded TSI Insurance, which shows its Thai company name บริษัท ไทยเศรษฐกิจประกันภัย จำกัด (มหาชน) and the Chinese name 泰国商业保险(大众)有限公司 in the capture. The first screen is a full-bleed photograph of glass towers under a blue sky with a large Thai headline "เดินหน้าธุรกิจของคุณ" in white and "ได้อย่างมั่นใจ" in yellow, a "1352 24 ชั่วโมง" call pill at the top right, and two translucent rounded tiles for car insurance and accident insurance. A yellow navigation bar carries the white TSI logo block on the left and five Thai links plus a TH language dropdown on the right. The next slide celebrates "84 YEARS OF EXCELLENCE" in gold over a golden-bokeh sky with the staff photographed from behind. Below, a news section "ข่าวสารและกิจกรรม" shows three photo cards with outlined "อ่านเพิ่มเติม" buttons, then a navy "ราคาตลาดหลักทรัพย์" stock-price panel with a line-chart photo, and a navy footer with the hotline 1352, Facebook and LINE icons. A royal tribute pop-up covered the first load; it was suppressed before the current measurement and is not brand UI.

Measured facts about the capture: the page was loaded at a 1280px desktop viewport and its scroll height is 2420px; the document language is "th" and the document title is "Home TSI Insurance". The extractor counted 145 visible elements. Consent or overlay layers were hidden before measuring and are not documented as brand UI.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system, brand guideline or token package was found; the homepage was opened and links to none. The de-facto system is the shipped Bootstrap 4/5 style stack: the page declares both --bs-* variables and legacy names such as --breakpoint-xl:1200px, --font-family-sans-serif and --yellow:#ffc107, plus CKEditor (--ck-*) variables and Font Awesome 6 Free for icons. Those framework variables are not the brand palette: the brand colours #ffcd00, #0a2544 and #007eff come from the measured element backgrounds and borders.

The page declares 99 CSS custom properties on the root, for example `--bs-yellow: #ffc107`, `--light: #f8f9fa`, `--bs-info: #0dcaf0`, `--yellow: #ffc107`, `--bs-gray: #6c757d`, `--ck-color-table-caption-text: hsl(0, 0%, 20%)`, `--bs-orange: #fd7e14`, `--ck-image-style-spacing: 1.5em`, `--bs-success: #198754`, `--pink: #e83e8c`, `--gray-dark: #343a40`, `--ck-highlight-marker-pink: hsl(345, 96%, 73%)`, `--ck-color-image-caption-text: hsl(0, 0%, 20%)`, `--bs-body-font-weight: 400`. 

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do pair the #ffcd00 navigation bar with navy #0a2544 text and footer.
- Do set the hero headline in Sukhumvit 700 at 74.88px with 1px letter-spacing on desktop.
- Do use outlined #007eff buttons with a 10px radius for secondary actions.
- Do keep the hotline 1352 visible at the top and bottom.
- Do not use the Bootstrap default blue #0d6efd as a brand colour.
- Do not document the tribute pop-up or cookie consent as part of the system.
- Do not fill buttons: no filled primary button was measured.
- Do not add heavy shadows to cards; the measured shadow is 0 1px 3px.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from `canvas` #f8fafc, `ink` #212529 and `primary` #007eff.
2. Use `surface-3` yellow only for the navigation bar and `surface-2` navy for stock and footer panels.
3. Build news items from `card` with a `button-secondary` link.
4. Keep Sukhumvit as the only family.
5. Keep copy formal and corporate: Thai company name first, hotline visible.

Before changing any token, re-measure the live site with the repository extractor (`node web/scripts/extract-tokens.mjs --slug=thai-setakij --force`) and run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=thai-setakij`; a value that is not in the measurement or in a cited source must not be introduced.
