## Overview

The measured site is bhi.bdms.co.th, the site of Bangkok Health Insurance (BHI), a health and accident insurer whose own page text states it is backed by the Bangkok Dusit Medical Services (BDMS) hospital network; the document title is empty in the measurement. The capture opens with a white header (BHI logo at left; Thai/English switch, phone 02 769 7788 and info@bhi.co.th at right), then an indigo navigation bar with the links Home, About BHI, Products, Customer Care, Health & Wellness and Contact Us and a white search field. The hero is a split block: a pale grey-blue panel with "BHI HEALTH INSURANCE" in grey and blue italic and "Easy to access", with a blue ">> FIND OUTMORE" button. A dark bar with the quoted line "Get your insurance easily with BHI online 24 hours" carries a green "Get Online Insurance" button. Next come "Customer Care ศูนย์ดูแลลูกค้า" in blue with three round-photo columns (Group Care, Individual Care, Network Hospital), a full-bleed handshake photograph with a frosted white "Our history" card, a "VISION / MISSION" pair of slate-blue cards, and a slate footer with social icons, the Thai company name and a white panel of partner hospital logos (Bangkok Hospital, Samitivej, BNH, Phyathai, Paolo, Royal Bangkok). Copy is bilingual and informational.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use #3a6fba for the main call to action with a 4px radius and white 12px bold text.
- Do use #333333 for body text on white.
- Do keep Sarabun as the family and the Thai/English pairing in headings.
- Do show partner hospital logos on a white rounded panel.
- Do not describe the green online-insurance button with a hex: its value was not measured.
- Do not use the chat or floating widgets as brand UI.
- Do not use sharp 0px corners on cards; the measured card radius is 12px.
- Do not exceed the soft 0 4px 15px shadow.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from `canvas` white, `ink` #333333 and `primary` #3a6fba.
2. Use `nav-bar` indigo only for the navigation band.
3. Build content blocks as `card` panels over photography or slate `surface-3` panels.
4. Keep headings in Sarabun with weights 600 to 800 as in the tokens.
5. Keep text bilingual (English headline with Thai explanation) as on the page.

Before changing any token, re-measure the live site with the repository extractor (`node web/scripts/extract-tokens.mjs --slug=bangkok-health-insurance --force`) and run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=bangkok-health-insurance`; a value that is not in the measurement or in a cited source must not be introduced.
