## Overview

Skooldio (สคูลดิโอ) is a Thai online-learning and corporate upskilling company, measured at skooldio.com (page title "Skooldio (สคูลดิโอ) | คอร์สเรียนออนไลน์ และเวิร์กชอปพัฒนาทักษะ"). The measured h1 is "Scale ทักษะยุค AI ทั้งองค์กร / สร้าง Impact แบบเห็นผลจริง". The page sells to organizations first (hero copy, client logos, "สำหรับองค์กร" in the navigation) and to individuals second (online courses, live workshops, bootcamps).

In the capture, a black hero carries the centred 60px headline, a short sub-line, a search field with an amber search button and a large translucent rounded panel with a glowing amber-to-brown gradient ("ผู้ช่วยพัฒนาทักษะยุคใหม่ คู่องค์กรชั้นนำระดับประเทศ"). Under it come three big counters (550+ Companies, 120,000+ Students, 400+ Workshops), a logo wall of Thai banks and listed companies under "องค์กรชั้นนำที่ไว้วางใจ Skooldio", a carousel of popular company-chosen courses, a grid of colourful gradient learning-path cards (AI Foundations, Data Analyst, Front-End Web Developer, UX/UI Designer, Product Manager, Back-End Developer, Supply Chain & Logistics), a section on learning formats (คอร์สออนไลน์, เวิร์กชอป, Bootcamp) in three tinted cards, product listings with ratings and prices, and a four-column footer.

The voice mixes Thai with English product terms (Scale, Impact, Workshops, Bootcamp), is promotional and outcome-oriented, and shows many figures. A seasonal promotion modal and a cookie notice appeared in an earlier capture; they were excluded from the final measurement and are not brand UI.

## Design System Architecture

No public design-system or brand-guideline page was found, and none is linked from the home page; research was limited to the measured page. The shipped CSS has no root variables (rootVars is empty) and carries legacy Bootstrap-style colors in its corpus. The de-facto system is a set of reusable pill buttons, 16px cards, tinted section bands, and Thai-friendly type from IBM Plex Sans Thai and Rubik. Tokens here are computed-style measurements.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use amber #faa91a with white labels on 100px pills for actions.
- Do alternate a black, glowing hero with white content sections.
- Do use 16px radii for cards and 8px for tags.
- Do show social proof with counters and logo walls.
- Don't use the framework link blue #007bff.
- Don't treat the seasonal promo modal, cookie notice or scrims as brand UI.
- Don't use heavy shadows; keep them navy-tinted and low-opacity.

## Responsive Behavior

Mobile character-weighted sizes: 14px (2320), 12px (2275), 16px (1959), 20px (827), 24px (381), 18px (359), 34px (100). Breakpoints, column counts and navigation collapse were not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with a black hero, a 60px weight-500 headline at -0.35px tracking and an amber pill CTA.
2. Add proof: three large counters, then a logo wall.
3. Use gradient 16px cards for learning paths, tinted cards for formats.
4. Keep body copy 16px weight 300 in #707070 on white.
5. Use layered, low-opacity navy shadows for cards.
6. Gate: node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=skooldio.

## Additional Measurement Notes

The re-measured page reports a 1280px viewport and a 7170px scroll height. The spacing scale in the frontmatter runs from 1px to 24px and reflects the dense inner spacing of cards, tags and buttons, while the larger 32px, 48px and 64px paddings belong to section blocks. When extending the system, keep the amber accent restricted to actions and highlights, reuse the pill radius for every button, and reserve the multicolour gradients for learning-path cards.
