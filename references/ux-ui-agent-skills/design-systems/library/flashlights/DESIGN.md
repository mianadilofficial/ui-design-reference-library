## Overview
The measured page is "Our Roots • Flashlights", served from history.jailhouselawyers.org. It is a long-form interactive history of jailhouse lawyers, told in four periods. The queued brand name is Flashlights; the site itself names the project Flashlights and the section Our Roots. The capture (the first screen of a page measured at 90,657px tall) shows a cream page with a tiny breadcrumb "OUR ROOTS • FLASHLIGHTS" at the top left, TIMELINE and a menu icon at the right, a monumental two-line headline "THE HISTORY OF JAILHOUSE LAWYERS" in black ultra-condensed capitals, and a short description to its right ("A brief historical overview of the emergence and impact of jailhouse lawyers in the legal system"). Under it, four equal columns labelled CH.1 to CH.4 form a chapter index in pink-red, each with a period title (1700s–1900s, 1920s–1960s, 1970s–1990s, 2000s–present) set in the same heavy face and a small archival photograph centred in the panel. Further down, a full-width pink-red "PERIOD 1" panel carries the centred title "ESTABLISHMENT OF THE PRISON SYSTEM". The page is solemn in subject and loud in form: huge type, flat colour, archival black-and-white and sepia images.

## Typography
The system is three display and text faces from the FK type family plus Inter. Fkscreamertrial at weight 900 is the ultra-condensed headline face: the main title is 124.444px with a 112px line height, the period heading 160px with a 144px line height, the subheading style 88.888px with a 79.9992px line height, and on a 390px viewport the title measured 83.1987px with a 74.8788px line height; all are uppercase except the large date range. Fkgrotesktrial (400 and 700) is the text and button face: buttons are 14.2208px uppercase with a 15.6429px line height, and a secondary label style is 12.4416px with 0.2432px letter spacing. Fkgroteskmonotrial is a monospaced companion (3,539 measured characters); Georgia (1,641 characters) carries some body copy; Inter at 16px with a 19.9111px line height sets the chapter-link labels. The dominant measured text size is 31.112px (7,257 characters), then 16.888px and 12.448px. Loaded file names carry the word "trial", so the family files in use are the trial versions.

Font substitutes: the FK family is a commercial foundry release and the loaded files are trial cuts. Where they are unavailable, an ultra-condensed heavy grotesque (for example any extra-condensed 900-weight sans) replaces Fkscreamertrial, a neutral grotesque replaces Fkgrotesktrial, and any monospace replaces the mono companion.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do work with the six named colors: cream, near-black, pink, green, sand and grey.
- Do set headlines in Fkscreamertrial 900, uppercase and very large, with line height below font size.
- Do use flat color fields for each period and show archival photographs small and centred.
- Do keep body and labels in the grotesk and mono faces at 12 to 17px.
- Don't add gradients or heavy shadows; none were measured on visible elements.
- Don't use the pink-red for body text; it is a surface and button fill.
- Don't treat the circle at the top left corner as a component.

## Iteration Guide
1. Start on #f1eada with #101010 text, and use #e8465e and #10935b as full-width panels.
2. Set titles in Fkscreamertrial 900 at 124.444px (83.1987px on mobile), uppercase, tight leading.
3. Build the chapter index as a four-column grid with black rules and CH.n labels.
4. Keep buttons small: 8.8832px radius, 14.2208px uppercase text.
5. Keep photographs small, archival and centred on flat fields.
6. Replace trial fonts with licensed files before shipping.
