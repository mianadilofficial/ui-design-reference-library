## Overview

ORIGIN Property's English home page (https://origin.co.th/en/, title "ORIGIN PROPERTY | Condos Near BTS/MRT & Investment Condos in Bangkok") is the entry point of a Thai residential developer: condominiums, single houses, twin houses and townhomes. The measured final URL is the English path of the brand's own site, so no language-splash re-measure was needed. The page is 1280px wide and 7692px tall. Navigation lists Condominiums, Home, About Origin, Investor Relations Origin, Investor Relations Britania, Sustainability and Promotions, a TH/EN toggle and Search. The section headings, in order, are "Start with Us, Start with Origin", "Find Your Perfect Project & Location", "Find the Right Project for You", "Quality Locations from Origin...", "A Lifestyle That Suits You", "News Origin" and "Blog Stories You'll Love", and a hero slide reads "Creative Living For Better Life".

The voice is friendly and service-led: filter chips named by buyer need ("Ready to Move In", "Near BTS / MRT", "New Projects", "Pet-Friendly Projects", "High ceiling", "Near airport"), a "Call Center 1498" contact and an "Origin Family" privileges group. The capture shows a floating white pill header (search at the left, the ORIGIN wordmark centred, a menu icon at the right), a rounded hero carousel with a lifestyle photograph (a couple with a poodle in a living room) and peeking dark slides, a coverflow of brand cards (Primo, Alpha, Origin Vertical) with a centred enlarged card, a pale peach search panel, and a row of project cards with blue "Ready to Move" labels. Photography is bright, people-and-pets lifestyle imagery; the hero script word "Creative" is set in orange handwriting beside bold white sans text.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system or brand-guideline page is linked from the page; the closest items are News (/en/news) and investor pages at https://investor.origin.co.th/corporate/company-history/ and https://investor.origin.co.th/corporate/board-of-directors. The shipped CSS is Bootstrap 5 (`--bs-*` variables, `--bs-border-radius-2xl` 2rem) with SweetAlert2 (`--swal2-*`) for dialogs, and a few site variables (`--tile-h` 600px, animate.css-style `--animate-duration` 1s). `--bs-primary` remains the default #0d6efd, so the brand orange and navy are not stored in the Bootstrap variables.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do set IBM Plex Sans Thai at weight 700 for headings and 400 for body.
- Do split headlines into navy (#0d1e3a) and orange (#f46e0b) halves.
- Do use the dark #121417 button with a 10px radius and a 6px 16px shadow for primary actions.
- Do keep imagery bright, lifestyle-led and rounded (22px on cards).
- Don't use Bootstrap's #0d6efd for links.
- Don't treat the cookie banner (Settings, Reject, Accept) as brand UI.
- Don't put orange on large fills other than active chips and arrows.
- Don't drop the semi-transparent nav; it is a pill, not a bar.

## Responsive Behavior

The 390px pass measured the h1 and h2 at 25.6px / 35.84px (weight 700, h2 box 358px wide) and the body paragraph at 15.2px / 26.6px in #bbbbbb on a dark section. The home-page extraction gives no breakpoint list, and how the nav, carousel, chips and card scroller rearrange at mobile width was not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from white, navy #0d1e3a headings, orange #f46e0b accent and #121417 buttons.
2. Use IBM Plex Sans Thai throughout; keep body at 15.2px / 26.6px.
3. Round cards at 22px, buttons at 10px, chips and nav at pill.
4. Add shadow only in the measured stacks listed above.
5. Measure further pages (`--slug=origin-property--page --url=<url> --force --no-motion`) before adding tokens; dark mode is not measured.
