## Overview

The measured page is the home page of BrokersThai, `https://www.brokersthai.com/` (title "BrokersThai | เว็บไซต์ที่รวบรวมข้อมูลโบรกเกอร์สำหรับคนไทย", `lang` th), a Thai-language site that reviews and ranks forex and CFD brokers for Thai readers. The page's own text says it launched in February 2026 and is run by the team behind CompareForexBrokers.com. This is the real site: it renders full content and ships its own stylesheet, with no parked, placeholder or redirected page involved.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Use Prompt for headings, buttons and navigation and Sarabun for body, with 1.75 line height on 16px Thai text.
- Reserve amber `#f59e0b` for the primary action, with navy `#0a2540` text and an 8px radius.
- Use `#1859d1` for links, with navy on hover, and `#e5e7eb` for rules.
- Keep cards white, 12px radius, 1px border and the faint card shadow.
- Show named people, methodology links and disclosures near claims.

**Don't**
- Don't add letter spacing to Thai text; none was measured.
- Don't use heavy shadows on cards; the measured shadow is `0 1px 2px` at 4 percent.
- Don't use the status red or green as brand accents; they are declared for data states.
- Don't use pill-shaped buttons; the measured button radius is 8px.
