## Overview

Burrow sells modular, tool-free furniture ("We Fixed What Furniture Gets Wrong", reads the homepage h1; "Modular pieces that adapt as your life evolves… Delivered in days, not months."). The storefront is a clean white page (`{colors.canvas}` — `#ffffff`, the colour covering the most pixels in the capture) with near-black `{colors.ink}` `#262626` text — the site's own `--default-text-color` — and a grid of warm, sunlit lifestyle photography.

Typography is two families. **Suisse Intl** (Swiss Typefaces) carries everything readable: weight 500 for headlines (40px/42.4px h1, 45–68px promo headlines), 400 for 14–18px body, 450 for product names. **DM Mono** (Colophon for DeepMind, open source) is the small-caps voice: uppercase eyebrows tracked 2px — "SMARTER BY DESIGN", "THE AUTUMN SALE", "MEET MERCER".

Buttons are compact rectangles with barely-there corners. Marketing CTAs are 44px-tall boxes with a 3px radius, 10px 15px padding and 14px text tracked 1px: sand `{colors.sand}` (`#eaddc5`) on light backgrounds ("Shop Bestsellers"), white on photography ("Shop Sale", "SHOP NOW"). The commerce action is a `#262626` "Add to cart – $1,787" button with a 2px radius. Sale prices are coral `{colors.coral}` (`#e46950`) beside the original price in grey `#a5a7a8`.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White canvas, `#262626` ink, a soft warm grey `#f5f3f1` for alternate bands.
- Suisse Intl 500 headlines with default tracking; DM Mono 500 uppercase eyebrows tracked 2px.
- Small-radius (2–3px) rectangular buttons — never pills.
- Sand `#eaddc5` CTA on light backgrounds, white CTA on images, `#262626` Add to cart.
- Coral `#e46950` for sale prices; rust `#883d2a` for the "Warehouse Sale" nav link; burgundy `#681919` announcement bar.
- Warm charcoal `#383633` footer and `#514f4d` newsletter band with cream `#f7eee3` text.
- Three-up image tile grid with 18px/500 captions under each photo.

### Imagery & Art Direction
- Lifestyle photography of real rooms with big windows and natural light — sofas facing sea views, terraces, wood-panelled media walls, arched dining rooms.
- People appear relaxed and candid (a woman lying on a sleeper sofa in a striped top).
- Tiles are landscape crops, square-cornered, in a three-column grid with small gutters.
- Feature blocks pair a large photo or looping video (with a pill-shaped pause control) on the left with a headline and grey paragraph on the right.

### Voice of Microcopy
- Confident, problem-solving headlines: "We Fixed What Furniture Gets Wrong", "We've rethought everything — starting with modularity.", "Looks Sharp. Lives Soft."
- Discount-forward tile captions: "Up To 35% Off Modular Seating", "30% Off Sleeper Sofas", "Up to 70% Off Warehouse".
- Short, practical CTAs: "Shop Bestsellers", "Shop Sale", "Add to cart – $1,787", "+ More options".
- Eyebrows in mono caps: "SMARTER BY DESIGN", "MODULAR FUNCTIONALITY", "DON'T JUST TAKE IT FROM US!".

### Principles
- **Headlines are medium (500), never bold**, with default tracking and ~1.05–1.1 line height.
- **Mono is for eyebrows only**, always uppercase and tracked 1.3–2px.
- **Body copy runs 14–18px at ~1.4 line height**, often in grey (`#686c6d`) under a dark headline.
- **CTA labels are 14px with 1px tracking**, capitalised.

### Note on Font Substitutes
Suisse Intl is commercial; the site's own declared fallbacks are Helvetica, Helvetica Neue, Arial and system-ui — use them as served. DM Mono is open source and can be used directly.

## Design System Architecture

Burrow publishes **no public design system**, token package or brand guidelines. Searches for "Burrow furniture brand identity rebrand typeface Suisse agency", "Burrow design system", "Burrow brand guidelines press kit" and "burrow.com press logo" returned press about its 2018 bespoke typeface by Placeholder with Grilli Type ([creativeboom.com](https://creativeboom.com/inspiration/placeholders-mid-century-inspired-typeface-for-furniture-brand-burrow-)) and agency portfolio pages, but no token values. The rest describes what is measurable on the site.

- **Token architecture**: 118 `:root` custom properties. A few are human-named (`--default-text-color: #262626`, `--navbar-height: 106px`, `--initial-navbar-height: 105px`, `--grid-item-width: 355px`, `--aside-width: 500px`, `--cart-aside-summary-height: 250px`). The rest are hashed, build-generated names (`--_1eel6vq12: #262626`, `--_1eel6vql: #eaddc5`, `--_1eel6vq6: #e46950`, `--_1eel6vq8: #f5f3f1`, `--_1eel6vq3j: DM Mono`, `--_1eel6vq1j: 55px`, `--_1eel6vq1e: 68px`, `--_1eel6vq4r: 768px`). Their values form a compact palette (10 variables equal `#262626`, 9 equal `#fff`), a type scale (11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 24, 25, 28, 30, 32, 35, 40, 45, 52, 55, 68, 72, 80px), weights 400/500, `DM Mono` as a font token, and breakpoints 768 / 1024 / 1200 / 1440 / 1620 / 1920px. The CSS references these via `var(--_1eel6vq…)` for font families and box-shadow rings.
- **Theming**: no dark or high-contrast mode observed.
- **Type & spacing**: Suisse Intl 500/400 with a DM Mono eyebrow layer; spacing values cluster on 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 40px.
- **Component model**: small-radius CTA in three colourways (sand, white, `#262626`), image tiles, product cards with chip rows and dual prices, carousel arrows, newsletter band.
- **Accessibility**: the footer links to an "Accessibility" page; the CSS contains a focus style `0 0 0 1px #fff, 0 0 0 4px #3d7bae, 0 0 0 5px #fff`. No published standard found.
- **Delivery**: no npm package, Figma kit or public repo found.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Keep the page white with `#262626` text and `#f5f3f1` alternate bands.
- Set headlines in Suisse Intl 500 and eyebrows in DM Mono 500 caps with 2px tracking.
- Use 3px-radius, 44px CTAs: sand on light, white on photos; 2px-radius `#262626` for Add to cart.
- Show sale prices in coral `#e46950` next to the grey `#a5a7a8` original price.
- End pages on the `#514f4d` newsletter band and `#383633` footer with cream text.

### Don't
- Don't use pill buttons or large radii (only the video control is rounded).
- Don't bold headlines to 700 except the white overlay labels on category images.
- Don't use coral for buttons; it marks discounts.
- Don't set body copy in DM Mono.
- Don't use GT America Burrow — it is not on the current site.
