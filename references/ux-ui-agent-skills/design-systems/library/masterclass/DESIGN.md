# MasterClass

## Overview

MasterClass is a subscription streaming school ("Learn from the best / Be your best") whose site behaves like a premium film service rather than an e-learning catalogue. The page is black — `#000000` — from header to footer, every word is white `#ffffff`, and the only saturated thing on the first screen is a crimson `#e32652` call to action that reads "Get MasterClass". Everything else is photography of celebrities lit like movie stills.

The homepage capture (top 2400px at 1280px wide) reads in this order:

1. A thin 40px utility strip on `#0d0d0e` with four 12px/600 product links (MasterClass, Certificates, On Call, Executive, For Teams).
2. The main bar: MasterClass logo (serif wordmark with the red "M" mark), a graphite `#222326` "Browse" dropdown button, a wide search field "What do you want to learn today?", then Gifts / View Plans / Log In text links and a crimson "Get MasterClass" button.
3. A hero built from a wall of instructor portraits in a 7-column grid; the lower rows fade into a dark maroon overlay (`#14020a`, the 3rd-largest background by area). Over it, bottom-left, the poster headline: "LEARN FROM THE BEST" in white and "BE YOUR BEST" in crimson, both set in condensed uppercase Sohne Schmal. Bottom-right, a price line "Starting at $10/mo (billed annually)." in 70% white, a full-width crimson button and a `#43454c` secondary button "Learn More About MasterClass", plus an 11px "30-day money-back guarantee".
4. A sticky purchase strip (avatars, "Starting at $10/month", crimson button).
5. "What's in every MasterClass membership?" — 32px/600 heading, a crimson button paired with an outlined "Gift" button (2px inset `#43454c` ring), and a right-hand checklist with line icons.
6. A centered 48px/600 two-line statement, "Meet the world's best. New classes added every month.", followed by a full-width rounded hero image of a featured class with a white "New" badge, a nameplate title and an outlined "Watch Trailer" button.
7. Another centered 48px statement, "A dose of inspiration, whenever you need it."

The art direction is cinematic: tight, warmly graded portraits, large photographic fields, and type that borrows film-poster condensed caps. The brand agency's own case study describes it this way — cinema, sophistication, "a single typeface and a signature color" at brand level, with each instructor receiving "a unique nameplate, font, and colorway" ([Gretel case study](https://gretelny.com/masterclass); [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/masterclass-brand-identity/)).

Microcopy is short, confident and second-person: "Be your best", "Get unlimited access to thousands of bite-sized lessons.", "A dose of inspiration, whenever you need it." Buttons are verb-first and capitalised ("Get MasterClass", "Learn More About MasterClass", "Watch Trailer").

Density is low: big headlines, wide gutters, one idea per band, generous 64–96px vertical spacing between sections.

### Principles

- Tracking is always positive on Sohne and grows with size (0.2px at 20px quote, 0.96px at 32px, 1.44px at 48px) — 0.03em on headings, matching `$mc-ls-headers: 0.03em` in the published [mc-components variables](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/base/_variables.scss).
- Only two Sohne weights appear: 400 for reading, 600 for everything that must be scanned (headings, buttons, labels).
- Sohne Schmal is reserved for uppercase poster lines; never use it for sentences.
- Ivar appears only for editorial/sub-brand titles in `#fffef2`.
- Button text is `text-transform: capitalize`, never uppercase.

### Note on Font Substitutes

Söhne, Söhne Schmal and Ivar are commercial fonts (Klim; Letters from Sweden). The site's own fallback stack is Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif. If no license is available, keep the stack the site declares (Helvetica/Arial) rather than introducing another family; for the Schmal poster lines the site itself ships an `Impact-fallback` face. Re-check tracking after substitution — the positive letter-spacing above is tuned for Söhne.

## Layout

### Spacing System

Measured paddings and gaps cluster on a 4px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96px. The most frequent paddings are 16px and 8px; the most frequent gap is 12px (button pairs, checklist rows). Root variables expose the scale as `--spacing-1` 4px … `--spacing-12` 96px and `--spacing-scale-*`.

### Grid & Container

- `--container-max-width: 1200px`; `--mc-maximum-content-width: 1264px`.
- At 1280px the content sits 40px in from each edge in the capture (logo, hero headline, sections).
- The legacy package documents a 12-column Bootstrap-derived grid with 16px gutters (32px at lg/xl) and container margins 16 / 32 / 64px ([variables.scss](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/base/_variables.scss)).
- Hero instructor wall: 7 portrait tiles per row with small gutters, edge to edge.
- Membership band: two columns — heading + buttons left, checklist right.

### Whitespace Philosophy

Sections are separated by 64–96px of black. Large statements are centered and stand alone in their band. Photography carries the visual weight; UI chrome is minimal and graphite.

## Elevation & Depth

MasterClass does not use drop shadows on its dark surfaces. Depth comes from:

- **Tonal steps** in the graphite ramp: `#000000` → `#0d0d0e` → `#191c21` → `#222326` → `#272c33` → `#303136` → `#43454c`.
- **Inset rings instead of borders**: `rgb(39, 44, 51) 0px 0px 0px 1px inset` (dark fields), `rgb(148, 154, 164) 0px 0px 0px 1px inset` (light fields), `rgb(67, 69, 76) 0px 0px 0px 2px inset` (tertiary button).
- **Photographic overlays**: the maroon `#14020a` fade on the hero and gradient scrims on class imagery.
- Site CSS also contains `0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.3)` and `0 0 16px 8px rgba(148,154,164,.1)` for floating layers (dropdowns/tooltips) — not visible in the capture.

### Decorative Depth

The purchase card on class pages uses `linear-gradient(rgba(135, 135, 135, 0.2), rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2))` with a 1px `#303136` border — a faint glass panel over black.

The underscore (`___` link text, and the logo's implied underline) is the brand's one graphic shape device ([Gretel](https://gretelny.com/masterclass)).

## Design System Architecture

### Is there a public system?

MasterClass publishes no public documentation site for its current tokens. What is public is its older npm component library, **mc-components**, authored by "Masterclass" <engineering@masterclass.com> under the `yankaindustries` GitHub org, latest version 2.2.2, last published May 2022 ([npm registry](https://registry.npmjs.org/mc-components); [README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/README.md)). The README states it "aims to provide primitive components to build user interfaces in MasterClass", is React + SCSS, and is documented in Storybook (the GitHub Pages docs link now returns 404). The live site has since moved to a larger `--mc-color-*` semantic token set that is not documented publicly; values in this file come from the live root variables, and where the old package and the live site disagree, the live site wins.

### Token architecture

- **Naming**: the package states "Prefixed all variables with mc — mc-font, mc-color, mc-contain, mc-asset" ([variables.scss](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/base/_variables.scss)).
- **Primitive tier (live)**: ramps such as `--mc-color-primary-100 … -900` (`#feb0c2` … `#a21536`), `--mc-color-neutral-90 … -1010`, `--mc-color-pink-*`, `--mc-color-purple-*`, `--mc-color-green-*`, `--mc-color-yellow-*`, `--mc-color-red-*`, each with an `-rgb` triplet twin (e.g. `--mc-color-primary-500-rgb: 227,38,82`).
- **Semantic tier (live)**: `--mc-color-primary-default`, `--mc-color-primary-hover`, `--mc-color-primary-pressed`, `--mc-color-primary-disabled`, `--mc-color-bg-page-default`, `--mc-color-bg-container-tint-dark`, `--mc-color-text-link-default-hover`, `--mc-color-element-focus`, `--mc-color-outline-error`.
- **Component/theme tier**: `--mc-theme-*` variables (`--mc-theme-badge-bg`, `--mc-theme-dropdown-border`, `--mc-theme-select-menu-bg`, `--mc-theme-carousel-arrow-bg`, `--mc-theme-checkout-separator-color`) — the same mechanism documented in [_theme-css-vars.scss](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/helpers/_theme-css-vars.scss).
- **Legacy discrepancy**: the package's `$mc-color-primary` is `rgb(242, 68, 98)` and its grays are `#191c21 #272c33 #31333b #596170 #949aa4 #dbdde1 #f3f3f3 #f8f8f8`; the live primary is `#e32652`. Several legacy grays still render live (`#191c21`, `#272c33`, `#596170`, `#949aa4`).
- The live root also contains Tailwind variables (`--tw-*`, `--spacing-*`, `--text-*`, `--radius-sm/md/lg`), so a utility layer sits alongside the `mc-` tokens.

### Theming

- `:root, .mc-theme-dark` is the default (white text on black); `.mc-theme-light, .mc-invert` flip to the light theme by reassigning `--mc-theme-*` custom properties ([_theme-css-vars.scss](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/helpers/_theme-css-vars.scss)). The white login modal is an instance of the light theme on the live site.
- No user-facing dark/light toggle; dark is the brand.
- Sub-brand colorways exist per product/instructor (Certificates' `#dcff00`/`#211d0d`; per-instructor colorways per [Gretel](https://gretelny.com/masterclass)).

### Type & spacing systems

- The package sets `html { font-size: 10px }` and builds a responsive step scale `--mc-scale-N` (`$mc-step-base: 10`, `$mc-step-scale: 4`) that decays at smaller breakpoints ([_base.scss](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/typography/_base.scss)). Live root still exposes `--mc-scale-1: 0.4rem`, `--mc-scale-8: 3.2rem`, `--mc-scale-12: 9.6rem` (i.e. 4px, 32px, 96px at a 10px root).
- Text classes `.mc-text-h1 … .mc-text-h8` map to `--mc-scale-9 … --mc-scale-3`; headers use line-height 1.25 and letter-spacing 0.03em (e.g. 32px → 40px / 0.96px, measured live).
- Breakpoints: `--mc-bp-sm 576px`, `--mc-bp-md 768px`, `--mc-bp-xl 1200px` (live); the package adds lg 960px.

### Component model

Official inventory exported by mc-components ([index.js](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/components/index.js)): Accordion, Background, Badge, Button, Carousel, Checkbox/CheckboxField, Dropdown (+Body/Content/Footer/Header/Item/Toggle), FormGroup, Fullscreen, ImageTile, Input/InputField, Loader, Modal (+Close/Content), Nameplate, PlayButton, Radio/RadioField, Select/SelectField, Separator, Sticky, Tags, Textarea, Tile (+Caption/Image/Overlay/Progress/Video), Toast, Video, VideoPlayer, plus icons and handler utilities. Classes are BEM with an `mc-` prefix (`.mc-badge--default`, `.mc-form-element__element`, `.mc-form-element--error`). Badge variants: default, dim, transparent ([_badge.scss](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/components/_badge.scss)). The legacy button mixin is uppercase with 0.12em tracking ([_buttons.scss](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mc-components@2.2.2/src/styles/components/buttons/_buttons.scss)); the live button is capitalize, 600, 0.16px — follow the live site.

### Accessibility

No published accessibility standard for masterclass.com was found. Measurable on the site: a visible "Skip To Main Content" link (14px/600), `.mc-sr-only` labels on the search field, a focus colour token `#3787ff` and focus-outline token `#ffffff`, and 44px minimum button height (48px for social auth and form fields).

### Delivery

npm `mc-components` (React, SCSS, Storybook), imported via `@import 'mc-components/dist/styles/scss/index'`. The README notes Sohne is licensed and swappable via `$mc-font-default`. No public Figma kit or versioned docs for the current system were found.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Keep the page `#000000` and all text `#ffffff`; use `#fffef2` only for nameplates/class titles.
- Use `#e32652` for the single purchase action per view; hover to `#d61a46`.
- Set buttons at 44px, 8px radius, 12px 24px padding, Sohne 16px/600 capitalize.
- Use Sohne Schmal uppercase for short poster lines only, with the crimson second line.
- Separate surfaces with graphite steps (`#222326`, `#272c33`, `#303136`, `#43454c`) and inset rings, not shadows.
- Let instructor photography fill the frame; overlay dark scrims for legibility.
- Keep positive letter-spacing on Sohne.

### Don't

- Don't introduce white or light page sections on marketing pages; light is reserved for modals.
- Don't put crimson on more than the primary action and the accent headline.
- Don't uppercase button labels (the live site uses capitalize).
- Don't use drop shadows on cards.
- Don't round buttons into pills — 8px only; 20px is for badges.
- Don't set long copy in Sohne Schmal or Ivar.
- Don't invent tints of `#e32652`; use the `--mc-color-primary-*` ramp.

## Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints

| Name | Width | Source |
|---|---|---|
| sm | 576px | `--mc-bp-sm` |
| md | 768px | `--mc-bp-md` |
| lg | 960px | mc-components `$mc-bp-lg` |
| xl | 1200px | `--mc-bp-xl` |

### Type at 390px (measured)

- Schmal section display: 64px → 46px (line-height 41.4px, -0.46px).
- Ivar display: 64px → 44px (48.4px).
- Statement heading: 48px → 34px (42.5px, 1.02px).
- Section h2: 32px → 20px (25px, 0.6px).
- Body stays 16px; the primary button stays 16px/600 with padding 12px 18px and 42px height, full width (350px).

### Touch Targets

Buttons 44px desktop / 42px mobile; form fields and social buttons 48px.

### Collapsing Strategy

Measured at 390px: the hero headline and the primary CTA share the same 350–356px content width (CTA full width at 350px, body copy 356px), i.e. Other reflow details were not measured.

### Image Behavior

Portraits and class images keep 8px corners and crop to fill; text overlays stay on dark scrims.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with `#000000` canvas, `#ffffff` Sohne 16px/400/24px, 0.32px tracking.
2. Add the header: 40px `#0d0d0e` utility strip, then logo + `#222326` Browse + search (8px radius, inset 1px `#272c33`) + text links + crimson CTA.
3. Build the hero from photography first, then overlay `#14020a`, then the Schmal headline (52px white over 96px `#e32652`).
4. Use one crimson button per band; pair it with `#43454c` secondary or the 2px-ring tertiary.
5. Section headings: 32px/600/40px, 0.96px; statements 48px/600/60px, 1.44px, centered.
6. Testimonials: `#272c33` cards, 8px, 48px 96px padding, 20px/400/32px quotes.
7. Check every hover: crimson → `#d61a46`; graphite → `#303136`; text links → underline.
8. At 390px, drop to the mobile sizes listed above and make CTAs full width.
9. Verify every new value against the live `--mc-color-*` variables before adding it.
