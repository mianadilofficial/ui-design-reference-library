# Lucid

## Overview

Lucid Software's home page (lucid.co, title "Transform Your Business With AI | Lucid") sells its visual collaboration suite — Lucidchart, Lucidspark and add-ons — to teams and enterprises. The measured page is 1280px wide and 8248px tall. A 60px light header (`#fafbfc` with a thin offset shadow) carries the "L. Lucid" logo, dropdown menus (Products, Solutions, Resources, Company) and "Enterprise", then "Log in", an outlined "Contact sales" and a dark "Sign up free".

The hero is centred and typographic: "Transform your business" in very light GraphikWide over "with AI" in heavy bright blue, a two-line grey-black intro and a square dark "Contact sales" button. Below sits an AI prompt demo — a light grey frame holding a white input ("Outline the process for resolving customer support tickets with swimlanes") over a dark "Generate →" bar. The page then drops into a deep indigo band with a subtle wave pattern: "Provide deep context for agentic AI" (the last words in bold teal), a "Learn more →" link, a small robot mascot and a grid of colourful template tiles (Retrospective, Workflow, Ownership, Network org, Email flow, Analytics, Idea board, Process, Org chart, Circle org).

Later sections: "Work acceleration for every team" with an accordion of team names beside a black-and-white photo cut out against an indigo diagram; "Purpose-built solutions" with product cards (Intelligent diagramming, Virtual whiteboarding, Product management and roadmapping) and add-ons; "The standard for enterprises" on a warm `#f7f4e4` band with photography; integrations; "The most used platform of its kind" with stats (100M+ individual users, 99% of Fortune 500, 180+ countries, #13 Okta 2026 most popular apps) on indigo; a logo strip; a dark `#282c33` closing band ("See and build the future with AI in Lucid"); and a five-column footer.

## Colors

The palette follows Lucid's published brand book: a cool-grey core with blue and indigo accents.

- **ink / primary `#282c33`** — Cool Grey 100, the brand's main colour: all body text (2909 characters), the filled buttons, the closing band and the logo. **on-primary `#ffffff`**.
- **primary-hover `#4c535d`** — Cool Grey 80, the hover fill of dark buttons.
- **canvas `#ffffff`** and **page-largest `#fafbfc`** (Cool Grey 0) — page surfaces; `#fafbfc` is the largest painted area, header and footer.
- **accent-blue `#1071e5`** — Blue 60, the brand book's primary accent: the "with AI" phrase in the hero.
- **link-blue `#005ed0`** — Blue 70, text links and menu links (197 characters).
- **indigo-90 `#2f3296`** — the deep indigo band backgrounds (second largest area); **indigo-80 `#3c41c2`** — section headings ("Work acceleration for every team") and indigo panels; `#1e2253` (Indigo 105) is a measured border colour.
- **teal-20 `#77e5d7`** — the bold "agentic AI" accent on indigo.
- **earth-5 `#f7f4e4`** — the warm enterprise band.
- **Cool greys** — `#3a414a` (Cool Grey 90, secondary text, 1024 characters), `#5d646f` (Cool Grey 70, small labels like "Products"), `#a9afb8` (Cool Grey 30, borders), `#ced4db` (Cool Grey 20, hover fill of white buttons), `#dfe3e8` (Cool Grey 10, borders), `#f2f3f5` (Cool Grey 5, nav-item hover fill and panels).

The brand book also names Indigo 60 `#635dff` and Pink 60 `#d916a8` as secondary accents, plus product palettes (Lucidchart orange `#f96b13`, Lucidspark red `#ff3d3d`). The template tiles in the indigo band use yellow, orange, pink, mint and lilac fills as illustration colours.

## Typography

Lucid uses **Graphik** (400, 500, 600, 700) and **Graphik Wide** (200, 400, 900), declared as `--fonts-graphik` and `--fonts-graphik-wide`. The brand book names Graphik the primary typeface for headlines and body, Graphik Wide the secondary face "for titles, subheads, and prominent features… used sparingly", and Self Modern a tertiary serif for pull quotes (not seen on the home page).

- **Display — GraphikWide 200, 64px / 64px, -0.2px**: "Transform your business"; the accent "with AI" is measured at 64px weight 900 in `#1071e5`, and "agentic AI" at 48px 900 in `#77e5d7`.
- **Heading — GraphikWide 200, 48px / 48px, -0.2px**: "Provide deep context for agentic AI".
- **Section heading — Graphik 700, 40px, -0.2px** in `#3c41c2` or `#282c33` ("Work acceleration for every team", "The standard for enterprises").
- **Body — Graphik 400, 20px / 27px, -0.2px**; 16px (the most common size, 2557 characters) and 24px for card and list text.
- **Button — Graphik 500, 20px / 20px** in the hero; 16px in the header.
- **Nav — Graphik 500, 16px / 24px**. **Overline — Graphik 400, 12px / 13.2px** in `#5d646f` ("Products", "Integrations").

Every size carries the same -0.2px tracking. The striking pairing is weight contrast inside one headline: hairline 200 against black 900. **Substitutes:** Graphik is a commercial face; Inter for Graphik and a wide grotesk such as Archivo Expanded for Graphik Wide.

## Layout

Content uses a container gutter of `--spacing-container-gutter: 3.75rem` (60px; content starts at x=60 in most bands) up to x=1220, with breakpoints named `--sizes-breakpoint-bp500`, `bp768`, `bp900`, `bp1024`, `bp1220`, `bp1440` and `bp1728`. The hero is centred (headline 1024px wide); later sections are left-aligned with headings above two- or three-column content. Product cards sit in rows of three; the footer has five columns (Get Started, Products, Solutions, Company, Resources).

Spacing: paddings of 8px (most frequent), 4px, 24px, 12px, 60px, 16px, 64px, 80px, 104px and 120px; gaps of 8px, 24px, 16px, 12px, 32px and 80px. The nav height is `--sizes-nav-height: 60px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Lucid's shadows are offset diagonally down-right, like a drop shadow on a diagram shape, and tinted with Cool Grey 100. Measured: `rgba(40, 44, 51, 0.2) 2px 2px 4px 0px` (3 elements, `--shadows-card-default` / `--shadows-elevation2`) and `rgba(40, 44, 51, 0.24) 1px 1px 2px 0px` on the header (`--shadows-elevation1`). The scale continues with `--shadows-elevation3: 4px 4px 6px 0 rgba(40,44,51,.16)`, `--shadows-elevation4` / `--shadows-card-hover: 6px 6px 8px rgba(40,44,51,.12)`, `--shadows-nav: 2px 2px 8px rgba(40,44,51,.2)`, and hard offset shadows `--shadows-10: 10px 10px 0 0 rgba(0,0,0,.3)`, `--shadows-15` and `--shadows-20`. `--shadows-card-list: 4px 4px 0 #ced4db` is a solid offset.

## Shapes

The interface is square: buttons, inputs, cards and the header have 0px radius. The only measured radii are 48px (18 elements) and one 50% circle. The CSS defines a radius scale (`--radii-r2`, `r4`, `r8`, `r12`, `r16`, `r24`, `r32`, `r48`) that the home page barely uses. Borders are 1px in `#282c33` on buttons and `#dfe3e8` / `#a9afb8` on dividers and inputs. Illustrations are flat diagram shapes in bright fills; photos are cut out and paired with diagrams. The logo is the "L." symbol with the Lucid wordmark in Cool Grey 100.

## Design System Architecture

Lucid publishes a brand book: https://lucid.co/brand redirects to https://brandpad.io/lucid-software-brand-book, which covers Story, Values, Personality, Voice and tone, Visual principles, Logo, Color palette, Typography, Layout, Illustrations, Motion, Cinematography, Photography, Elements and patterns, and Product presentation. It defines the core colours (Cool Grey 100 `#282c33`, Cool Grey 80 `#4c535d`, Cool Grey 30 `#a9afb8`, Cool Grey 10 `#dfe3e8`, Cool Grey 5 `#f2f3f5`, White), accents (Blue 60 `#1071e5`, Indigo 60 `#635dff`, Pink 60 `#d916a8`), product palettes, the three typefaces, and logo rules (the Lucid logo appears only in Cool Grey 100 and White). The website implements this as CSS variables with numbered ramps — `--colors-cool-grey-0` … `-100`, `--colors-blue-0` … `-100`, `--colors-indigo-0` … `-105`, `--colors-earth-0` … `-100`, plus green, teal, lime, orange, fuchsia and purple — and named scales for radii (`--radii-r*`), shadows (`--shadows-elevation1`–`4`, `--shadows-card-*`), sizes (`--sizes-s64`, `--sizes-s104`) and fonts (`--fonts-heading`, `--fonts-body`). The voice is described as "smart, bold, and trustworthy": always informative, friendly, confident and smart; never robotic, superfluous, boastful or condescending.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Build on Cool Grey 100 `#282c33` for text, buttons and dark bands.
- Pair a hairline GraphikWide 200 phrase with a heavy 900 phrase in Blue 60 `#1071e5` (or Teal 20 `#77e5d7` on indigo).
- Keep buttons and cards square with 1px borders.
- Use diagonal offset shadows tinted with `rgba(40, 44, 51, …)`.
- Alternate `#fafbfc`, white, warm `#f7f4e4` and deep indigo `#2f3296` bands.
- Write informative, friendly, confident copy.

**Don't**
- Don't round buttons or cards.
- Don't recolour the Lucid logo; use only Cool Grey 100 or White.
- Don't overuse Graphik Wide; keep it for titles and big statements.
- Don't use centred, symmetric blur shadows.
- Don't set Self Modern in all caps.

## Responsive Behavior

The extractor's 390px mobile pass recorded: the hero h1 at GraphikWide 400, 32px / 32px, -0.2px (330px wide), so the light 200 weight becomes 400 on mobile; the indigo-band h2 at the same 32px / 32px 400. Body copy drops to Graphik 400 16px / 21.6px. The mobile primary CTA is unchanged: "Contact sales", `#282c33`, white Graphik 500 20px/20px, 12px 24px padding, 46px tall. The header keeps the `#fafbfc` background and shows "Skip to Content" and "Menu"; the layout probe recorded 1 visible header link and a "Menu" toggle on mobile versus 48 links (menus included) and no toggle on desktop, with a maximum of 2 card columns on mobile versus 3 on desktop. Mobile font sizes: 16px, 20px, 32px, 12px and 40px. CSS breakpoints are 500px, 768px, 900px, 1024px, 1220px and 1440px. Section arrangement at mobile width was not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with a `#fafbfc` header (60px, `1px 1px 2px` offset shadow) and a white hero.
2. Set the headline in GraphikWide: a 200-weight phrase at 64px/64px and a 900-weight accent phrase in `#1071e5`; use -0.2px tracking everywhere.
3. Add a square `#282c33` button (12px 24px, 46px tall), hover `#4c535d`.
4. Follow with a deep indigo `#2f3296` band, a white 48px GraphikWide headline with a teal accent, and colourful flat template tiles.
5. Use Graphik 700 40px section headings in `#3c41c2` or `#282c33`, 20px/27px body text and `#005ed0` links.
6. Build cards square with `rgba(40, 44, 51, 0.2) 2px 2px 4px` shadows.
7. Close with a `#282c33` band, then a `#fafbfc` five-column footer.
