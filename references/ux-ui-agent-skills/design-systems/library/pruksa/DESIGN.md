## Overview

`https://www.pruksa.com/en` (page title "พฤกษา เรียลเอสเตท - Pruksa", lang en) is the English home page of Pruksa Real Estate, a Thai residential developer. It is a property-portal front page measured at 1280px: 1,961 visible elements, 4,380px scroll height. The page opens with a full-width promotional carousel in saturated green with a gold-and-red magician-hat campaign (Thai headline, "starts 1.19 MB*", dates 1 Oct 69 - 15 Dec 69, "0 baht" down-payment bursts), blue-and-yellow bulb-border framing and project renderings, with a sticky white header over it: the green "P" logo and "PRUKSA", then Townhome, Single detached house, Condo, Semi-detached house, Commercial buildings, Upcountry projects, Promotion, and at right Sell land and About pruksa, an account icon, a search icon and a hamburger.

Below the carousel the page switches to a calm warm-grey (`#f2f2f1`) ground: "FIND THE (RIGHT) PROJECT FOR YOU" in large uppercase, a white search field (placeholder "Type the project name or location you're interested in") with a green "ค้นหา" (search) button, and six square outlined type buttons (Townhome, Single detached house, Condo, Semi-detached house, Commercial buildings, Upcountry projects). Sections follow: PRUKSA HIGHLIGHT and PRUKSA INSPIRING STORIES (their cards did not render in the capture and are not described), PRUKSA RECOMMENDED PROJECTS with a dark `#444444` tab for the active type and a brand-filter row (ALL BRANDS, BAAN GREENHAUS, บ้านพฤกษา, PRUKSA VILLE, THE CONNECT, PATIO, THE PLANT CITI, NATURA, VILLETTE LITE), three project cards with a coloured price strip, a brand-communication banner ("LONG LIVE LONG LIFE" series with portraits of families) and a white four-column footer with Contact Center 1739, social icons and the copyright "© 2026 PRUKSA INC.".

Voice: bilingual Thai/English, direct and promotional, with section labels in English capitals and campaign copy in Thai.

A cookie-consent card (ยกเลิก / ยอมรับ buttons) appeared in the first capture; this document uses the re-measured page without it and does not treat the consent controls, the green chat-bot icon or the carousel arrows as brand tokens.

Measured from `https://www.pruksa.com/en` (`web/data/extracted/pruksa.json`).

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the ground warm grey `#f2f2f1` and text `#444444`.
- Do use `#6d981d` only for the search action and the active brand filter.
- Do set headings in uppercase DB Gill Siam X at 46px, regular weight; bold only for card titles.
- Do keep controls square with 1px `#444444` outlines.
- Don't use Bootstrap's `#007bff` or `#28a745` as brand colours.
- Don't round buttons or inputs; they measure 0px.
- Don't document the cookie card, the chat-bot icon or carousel arrows as brand UI.
- Don't mix card strip colours without checking the brand line they belong to.

## Responsive Behavior

The mobile pass recorded type: the h2 went from 46px/46px to 28px/28px (uppercase, `#444444`), and the mobile size distribution was 14px (1,775 characters), 16px (1,116), 20px (673), 12px (185), 28px (84) and 40px (44). The stylesheet declares Bootstrap breakpoints at 576px, 768px and 992px. Mobile nav form, card columns and layout were not measured.
