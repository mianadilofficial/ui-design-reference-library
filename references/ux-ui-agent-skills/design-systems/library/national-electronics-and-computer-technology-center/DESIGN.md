## Overview

nectec.or.th is the site of เนคเทค / NECTEC, the National Electronics and Computer Technology Center (ศูนย์เทคโนโลยีอิเล็กทรอนิกส์และคอมพิวเตอร์แห่งชาติ), shown in the page title as "เนคเทค สวทช. - NECTEC : National Electronics and Computer Technology Center". The footer reads "© ศูนย์เทคโนโลยีอิเล็กทรอนิกส์และคอมพิวเตอร์แห่งชาติ 2563". The measured page is 1280px wide and 3,887px tall with 679 visible elements, `lang` set to en-US, and a Thai-language interface.

The capture shows a white institutional portal. A slim top bar carries the NECTEC / NSTDA logo at the left, a five-item navigation (นวัตกรรมพร้อมใช้, งานวิจัย, เนคเทคเพื่อสังคม, แนะนำเนคเทค, ข่าวสาร), TH and EN language switches and a search icon. Under it a full-width hero carousel with left and right arrows shows an event poster (a "40 NECTEC" anniversary mark and a conference QR code). Below the hero are three content bands, each with a maroon Thai title, a grey one-line description, a right-aligned "More ..." link and a row of four cards: นวัตกรรมพร้อมใช้ (ready-to-use innovations), งานวิจัย (research) and เรื่องเด่นเนคเทค เพื่อสังคม (NECTEC for society).

Imagery is a mix of colourful illustrated product posters (solar-farm graphics, a smart-capsule render, an anemia-screening device, terahertz imaging) and documentary photographs of ceremonies and medical staff. Every card places its image flush at the top, a bold Thai title beneath it and a lighter grey summary of several lines. Text is Thai with embedded English product names such as "Smart Capsule" and "Pathumma LLM".

A dark tribute popup with a grayscale portrait and a dimming scrim covers the upper part of the capture. It is a memorial overlay, not brand UI, and its black and translucent fills are excluded from the tokens. The page's cookie notice was hidden by the extractor (`consentOverlaysHidden: cookie-notice`). The generator output was identical after a re-measurement with the batch extractor, so the numbers below are the same as the first pass.

## Design System Architecture

No public NECTEC design system, token package or brand guideline was linked from the pages used for this measurement, and no web search was run (none was warranted by the brief). The de-facto system is the WordPress GeneratePress theme: the extraction shows `--wp--preset--*` variables for colors, font sizes, spacing and shadows, and the theme's own `--accent`, `--contrast`, `--base`, `--base-2` and `--base-3` variables. NECTEC's styling is layered over that theme: Kanit as the loaded family, the maroon heading color and the footer band. Because the theme variables include WordPress defaults, only values that also appear in rendered elements are promoted to tokens.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:
- Set section titles in maroon `#760204` Kanit and descriptions in grey `#777777`.
- Build content rows from four equal cards with a 3px radius, soft 10px glow and a 10% grey wash.
- Keep body copy in Kanit weight 200 at 15px and card titles at weight 300 or 400.
- Close pages with the `#840000` footer and white text.

Don't:
- Don't use the black and translucent fills from the tribute popup as surfaces.
- Don't treat WordPress preset colors (vivid purple, pale pink) as brand colors.
- Don't add pill radii; none were measured.
- Don't introduce a primary button; the page has none.

## Responsive Behavior

On the mobile pass the h1 keeps 25px at weight 400 (370px wide, 25px high), the h2 keeps 17px at weight 300 and wraps to two lines (370px wide, 34px high), and body text keeps 15px at weight 200 with a 22.5px line height in a 310px-wide block (68px high). Breakpoints, nav collapse and card column count on mobile were not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start with a white canvas, `#222222` text, maroon `#760204` titles and a `#840000` footer.
2. Use Kanit throughout: 25px for page titles, 17px for sub-titles, 15px weight 200 for summaries.
3. Compose card rows with `#7d7c7c1a`, 3px radius and the 15% black 10px glow.
4. Keep links `#1e73be` and leave hover unspecified until measured.
5. Measure any new page with `node web/scripts/extract-tokens.mjs --slug=<slug>--<page> --url=<url> --force --no-motion` before adding tokens.
