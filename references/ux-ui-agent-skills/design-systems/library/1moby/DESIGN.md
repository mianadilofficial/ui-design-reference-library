## Overview

1Moby (บริษัท วันม๊อบบี้ จำกัด) is a Thai digital solutions company. Its home page, in Thai, is titled "1Moby | ตัวจริงด้าน Digital Solutions ของไทย" and headlines "ผู้นำด้าน Digital Solution และ AI-Driven Business". The page lists a Bangkok address (The Nine Tower Grand Rama 9), the tagline "One move, Endless potential.", and service families: Tech Consulting & System Integration, Enterprise SaaS & Solutions (for example Thaibulksms, Thaibulkmail, LINE Agency, Movider) and MarTech & Communication Platform. A statistic card reads "597+ โปรเจกต์" and "กว่า 19 ปี ในธุรกิจเทคโนโลยี".

The measured page (1280px wide, 8,095px tall, 1,123 visible elements) is a long, polished marketing page with a navy-to-cobalt-to-orange gradient hero, a floating white pill navigation bar, device mockups of client work, a grid of rounded white client-logo tiles, large ghosted "1MOBY" and "AWARDS" wordmarks as section backdrops, cobalt statistic cards, an awards gallery (Cannes Lions, ISO tiles) and a dark cobalt footer. Imagery is staged photography of the team at events plus 3D phone and screen renders in blue. The voice mixes Thai with English product terms (Digital & AI Solutions), confident and corporate-friendly.

A cookie-consent bar appeared in the first capture and was removed by re-extracting; it is not documented as brand UI.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system or brand guidelines were found: the home page (https://www.1moby.com) links a "Download Resource" page with expert solution materials, but no press kit or guideline document. The de-facto system is a Tailwind-based CSS layer: the root holds Tailwind utility variables (`--tw-ring-shadow`, `--tw-inset-shadow`, `--tw-gradient-from`) and named colour ramps (`--vibrant-blue-*`, `--blue-*`, `--orange-*`, `--yellow-*`, `--dark-grey-*`, `--black-*`, `--white-*`). Type uses a self-hosted MeroThai with a metric-matched "MeroThai Fallback" face, and Geometrica with a "Geometrica Fallback".

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use white pill buttons with a black label and a trailing black arrow chip as the main CTA on dark sections.
- Do use cobalt `#006bff` for statistic cards, key words in headings and links.
- Do set Thai headlines in MeroThai 700 and keep Thai line heights near 1.4.
- Do round cards at 20px and use soft cobalt-tinted shadows.
- Do pair a dark gradient hero with white content bands.
- Don't use the cookie bar, chat pill or other overlays as brand components.
- Don't use orange `#fc4c02` or amber `#ffa400` outside award or badge tiles.
- Don't set large Thai text with tight line height.

## Iteration Guide

2. Use MeroThai 700 for headings (60 / 48 / 24px) and 14px / 400 for body.
3. Build buttons as pills with a trailing arrow chip; white on dark, black on light.
4. Round cards at 20px; use the cobalt glow shadow `4px 4px 40px` at 8% blue.
5. Keep cookie and chat widgets out of the token set.
