## Overview

The measured site is www.thaipat.co.th/th/ (title "ไทยพัฒนาประกันภัย"), the Thai-language home page of Thaipat Insurance, whose logo shows an Eiffel-tower-like emblem and the words "ไทยพัฒนา ประกันภัย" in blue and red. The capture shows a white header with a large red hotline "02-253-4141" and the note "คอลเซ็นเตอร์ 24 ชม.", a navy navigation bar (หน้าแรก with a cyan active tab, เกี่ยวกับเรา, ผลิตภัณฑ์ของเรา, ข่าวสาร/กิจกรรม, บริการลูกค้า, ติดต่อเรา, พนักงาน, ตัวแทน) with a white search field and round TH and EN switches. A hero slider presents "ประกันอัคคีภัย" in navy and cyan Thai type over a photo of model houses with a red "อ่านเพิ่มเติม" button. Four product tiles with alternating cyan and navy headers (motor, fire, marine and transport, personal accident) each carry a short paragraph and a red read-more link with a chevron. A pale blue band "บริการลูกค้า" shows four white circles with cyan pictograms (branches, 24-hour roadside help, garages, network hospitals). Promotion banners, an articles carousel and a dark footer follow. A vertical strip of floating icon shortcuts and a back-to-top control appear at the right and are not documented as brand UI.

Measured facts about the capture: the page was loaded at a 1280px desktop viewport and its scroll height is 3011px; the document language is "TH" and the document title is "ไทยพัฒนาประกันภัย". The extractor counted 549 visible elements.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use navy #1a4a88 for the navigation bar and alternate it with cyan #28a9e2 for tile headers.
- Do use red #dc4637 sparingly for read-more links and the hotline.
- Do set section titles in THSarabunNew at 50px.
- Do put pictograms in white circles on the #edf2f8 band.
- Do not describe the floating side shortcuts or back-to-top button as brand UI.
- Do not invent a filled primary button colour; none was measured.
- Do not use pure red for large fills.
- Do not drop the hotline from the header.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start from `canvas` white, `ink` #000000 and `primary` #dc4637.
2. Use `nav-bar` and `accent-cyan` for structure, `surface-3` for soft bands.
3. Compose product tiles as navy or cyan header over white text with a red read-more link.
4. Keep Thai headings large and light-weight.
5. Keep the 24-hour call centre number prominent.

Before changing any token, re-measure the live site with the repository extractor (`node web/scripts/extract-tokens.mjs --slug=thaipat-insurance --force`) and run `node web/scripts/verify-designs.mjs --slug=thaipat-insurance`; a value that is not in the measurement or in a cited source must not be introduced.
