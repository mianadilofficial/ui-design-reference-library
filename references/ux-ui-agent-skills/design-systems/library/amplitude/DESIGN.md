# Amplitude

## Overview

Amplitude is a digital analytics and AI-agent platform, and amplitude.com is a clean, white, product-forward page: black geometric headlines, gray body copy, black pill buttons, Amplitude Blue held back for small calls to action and links, and generous product screenshots on pale #f5f7fa panels. A black announcement bar ("Measure the impact of your AI shipping with the North Star Playbook") carries a small blue "Start reading" button. The white header shows the Amplitude logo, Platform, Solutions, Resources and Pricing, and at right "Login", an outlined "Contact sales" and a black "Get started" pill.

The hero pairs a left-aligned headline, "A new era for product teams", in Gellix at 72px, weight 600, with -1px tracking, against a right-hand intro ("From analytics to feedback to code…") and two pills: black "Start for free" and outlined "Talk to sales". A small eyebrow chip above the headline links to a launch. Below, a tabbed product frame (Agent Analytics, Global Chat, AI-powered Replays, AI Data Governance) shows the Amplitude interface with an AI chat panel. Then follow a logo wall ("Trusted by teams shaping the future"), "One interface. All our capabilities." with prompt chips and an AI chat mock, "Set up Amplitude where you build" with a grid of agent-tool tiles (Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, Replit, Codex, Gemini, Figma Make, Lovable), a performance line chart with four big stats, "The platform for building self-improving products" four-up cards, customer video tiles (Fender, Backstage, Udemy), featured resources and a closing "The industry's most generous free plans" before a white link footer.

Copy is short and declarative ("A new era for product teams", "Hear what changed when our customers stopped guessing").

## Colors

The brand site (https://brand.amplitude.com/creative/color) names the primaries Amplitude Blue, Black and White ("lead with our primaries"), secondary accents Dark Blue #001A4F, Lilac #6980FF, Violet #A373FF, Pink #FF7D78 and Red #F23845 ("used sparingly"), and a gray ramp from Gray 100 #13171A to Gray 10 #F2F4F8. The typography guide states "Amplitude Blue is reserved for CTAs, links, and eyebrows" and that blue text on black must use #4083FF.

On the measured page:

- **Canvas #ffffff** and **ink #000000** — the page is white with black headlines; black also fills the announcement bar and the hero pills.
- **Primary #0052f2** — Amplitude Blue as rendered: the "Start reading" button fill and blue link text; hover lightens it (measured).
- **On-primary #ffffff** — labels on blue and black.

- **Blue-on-black #4083ff**, **accent-dark-blue #001a4f**, **accent-lilac #6980ff** and **accent-violet #a373ff** — official values for dark-background links and illustration accents.

A deep #10069f and #0a0f29 appear in the "Software Speedrun" resource artwork and dark media tiles.

## Typography

The brand guide names **Gellix** ("a geometrical sans-serif font", Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold) as the primary typeface, **Poppins** as the first substitute, Helvetica as the last resort and Noto Sans for unsupported languages; Bold is reserved "for key, succinct display headings", SemiBold is the default for titles, and Medium and Regular are for body copy.

On the page, every Gellix stack starts with "Twemoji Country Flags" (an emoji-flag face listed first), so measurement tools report that name; the rendered text is Gellix.

- **Display** — 72px / 72px, weight 600, -1px tracking.
- **Section headings** — 44px with -0.5px tracking ("One interface. All our capabilities."); 56px also appears.
- **Heading** — 28px / 31px, weight 600, -0.5px ("Trusted by teams shaping the future").
- **Subheading** — 22px / 24px, weight 600, -0.5px (card titles).
- **Body** — 18px / 24px, weight 400, in Gray 70. 16px with -0.4px tracking is the most common size by characters.

## Layout

At 1280px the content spans 68px to 1217px. The hero is split: headline left, intro and buttons right. Product frames run full content width; feature cards sit four across; agent-tool tiles run up to nine across; customer videos three across; resources are a large feature card beside a stacked list. Ten header items are visible on desktop.

Spacing: gaps of 12px, 6px and 8px dominate inside components; paddings of 16px, 24px and 48px are most common; 80px paddings separate sections. The spacing tokens step 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 and 17px.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat. The only measured box-shadow is a transparent 0px 4px 16px placeholder, so cards rely on 1px #d5d9e0 or #e2e4e9 borders and the pale #f5f7fa panel color for separation. Product screenshots carry their own internal shadows. Translucent panels (#f5f7facc, #f2f4f880) layer over imagery for a light glass effect.

## Shapes

4px is the most frequent radius (chips, small controls), followed by 9999px pills (all primary actions, tabs such as "MCP" and "SDK"). Cards and tiles use 13.5px; 16px for larger cards; 6px on the announcement button; 8px, 12px and 32px elsewhere; tab panels use 4px 4px 0px 0px and 0px 0px 4px 4px halves. Icons are filled brand logos on white tiles.

## Design System Architecture

Amplitude publishes a brand site at https://brand.amplitude.com/ with Philosophy, Content, Creative and References sections; the color page (https://brand.amplitude.com/creative/color) defines primaries, secondaries and a ten-step gray ramp, and the typography page (https://brand.amplitude.com/creative/typography) defines Gellix with Poppins and Helvetica fallbacks and weight roles. The marketing site's root variables expose only font hooks (`--font-ibm-plex-sans`, `--font-ibm-plex-serif`); component colors are compiled into utility classes, and the gray values in use (#50565b, #868d95, #d5d9e0) match the brand ramp's Gray 70, Gray 50 and Gray 20.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Lead with black, white and Amplitude Blue; keep blue for CTAs, links and eyebrows (brand rule).
- Set titles in Gellix SemiBold with slight negative tracking; reserve Bold for short display lines.
- Use the gray ramp for hierarchy: Gray 70 for body, Gray 50 for tertiary text.
- Make primary actions pills; pair a black pill with an outlined pill.
- Show the real product inside pale #f5f7fa frames.

**Don't**
- Don't lead with secondary colors, and don't use pink or red in quantity (brand rule).
- Don't color typography with secondaries unless brand approves (brand rule).
- Don't use #0052f2 for blue text on black; use #4083ff.
- Don't document the chat widget as brand UI.

## Responsive Behavior

The extractor's mobile pass at 390px measured: the h1 at 44px / 44px, weight 600, -1px tracking; the h2 at 28px / 31px with -0.25px; body text unchanged at 18px / 24px; the announcement button unchanged (14px, 32px tall). Three header items remain visible with a menu toggle labelled "Open navigation menu"; the announcement bar grows to 98px tall; content spans 22px to 366px. Media queries in the stylesheet are chiefly min-width 992px, min-width 768px, max-width 650px and min-width 1130px, with others from 380px to 1342px. Other mobile behaviour was not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Start white with black headlines and Gray 70 #50565b body copy.
2. Use Gellix (or Poppins, the official substitute) at 600 for titles: 72px display, 44px sections, 28px headings.
3. Build primary actions as 48px black pills; add an outlined pill for the secondary.
4. Use #0052f2 only for small CTAs, links and eyebrows.
5. Frame product screenshots on #f5f7fa panels with #d5d9e0 borders and 13.5–16px radii.
6. Keep the page flat — borders, not shadows.
7. Check the 44px mobile headline at 390px.
