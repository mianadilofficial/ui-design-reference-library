## Overview

ByteArk is a Thai company selling content delivery (CDN), live and on-demand video streaming and OTT platform services; the measured page is its Thai home page, https://www.byteark.com/ (title "ByteArk | Streaming Success, Igniting Futures", `lang="th"`; byteark.com redirects to the www host). The page is a product-marketing page for developers and media companies: a dark hero with a live-stream player mock-up, then light service sections with isometric illustrations, then feature cards and a dark footer. The desktop capture is 1,280px wide and 8,283px tall.

The look is a technology-blue brand set on navy. The first screen is a deep navy field (`#090a25`) with a subtle curved line texture, white bold Thai headlines with a blue highlight swash behind part of the text, and a translucent video-player mock-up with a red "LIVE" badge, a viewer count ("329 K") and a progress bar. Below it the page flips to a very light grey canvas (`#f9f9f9`) with large dark-slate headings (`#3b414d`), where one word of each heading is picked out in blue ("Video / OTT **Platform**", "**Event** Streaming", "Internet **TV**"). Illustrations are flat isometric devices (desktop monitor, laptop with phone and tablet, living-room TV) resting on soft blue blobs. Dark deep-navy and violet panels with rounded outer corners return later in the page, and the footer is a near-black `#111827`.

The capture, from the top: a navy header with the blue ark-shaped logo and "ByteArk" in a letter-spaced face, links "Network", "แพ็กเกจและราคา" (packages and pricing), "เอกสาร" (docs), "บทความ" (articles), "ติดต่อเรา" (contact us) in grey, a blue "เข้าสู่ระบบ" (log in) pill and a dark language select ("ภาษาไทย" with a globe); the hero headline "ปลดล็อกโลกการสตรีมมิ่งวิดีโอ สร้างประสบการณ์ที่ยอดเยี่ยมให้ผู้ชม" ("unlock the world of video streaming, create excellent experiences for viewers"); the player mock-up with a descriptive paragraph in white at its right; a gradient "ทดลองใช้งาน" (try it) pill and a ghost "ปรึกษาทีมงาน" (talk to the team) pill; and the section "บริการของเรา" (our services) in 60px bold with three alternating text-and-illustration rows.

Copy mixes Thai sentences with English product terms inline (CDN, Video Streaming, Video on Demand, OTT, Event Streaming, Internet TV). Headings are short noun phrases; paragraphs are two or three sentences.

## Design System Architecture

No public design system or brand-guideline page for ByteArk was found: https://www.byteark.com/ links to documentation (docs.byteark.com, for the product APIs), pricing, blog, contact and customer sites, but not to brand assets. The page is a Next.js app (`/_next/static/css/…` stylesheets) styled with Tailwind CSS: the `:root` holds Tailwind's `--tw-*` variables (for example `--tw-ring-color: rgba(59,130,246,.5)`, `--tw-bg-opacity: 1`) and Font Awesome 6 font variables.

The compiled CSS (https://www.byteark.com/_next/static/css/97b659fabc4ece72.css) shows the project's extended Tailwind palette through its utility classes: a custom `grey` scale (`grey-100` `#eef0f3`, `-200` `#d3d8dd`, `-300` `#adb4c0`, `-400` `#8d96a5`, `-600` `#646b76`, `-700` `#4e535e`, `-800` `#3b414d`, `-900` `#12161f`, `-1000` `#04090d`), a `primary` scale (`primary-100` `#2d83db`, `-200` `#0e5dad`, `-300` `#007eff`), a `secondary` violet (`#6957c5`), a `darkBlue` scale (`-100` `#01202f`, `-200` `#1a1734`, `-300` `#0d1c31`), and Tailwind's own `gray` scale for the neutral UI colours (`gray-900` `#111827` for the footer, `gray-300` `#d1d5db`, `gray-500` `#6b7280`). Breakpoints are Tailwind's `min-width` 640px, 768px, 1024px, 1280px and 1536px. The login button's `#2563eb` and its hover `#3b82f6` are not part of the custom `primary` scale above; `#3b82f6` equals the base of the page's `--tw-ring-color` (59, 130, 246).

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:
- Open on a navy hero (`#090a25`) with white bold headings, then move to a light `#f9f9f9` canvas with `#3b414d` headings and `#646b76` body copy.
- Highlight one word per heading in `#2d83db`.
- Use full-pill buttons: blue `#2563eb` for the login action, blue-to-violet gradient for the trial, 10% white ghost for secondary.
- Round cards to 20px, give them a 1px `#c8c4f180` border and an indigo-tinted shadow.
- Keep English product terms inline in Thai copy.
- Use flat isometric illustrations on soft blue blobs.

Don't:
- Don't introduce a second typeface; only AnantasonReno-Condensed was measured on the page.
- Don't use sharp-cornered buttons or cards.
- Don't use heavy black shadows; shadows are 5% to 10% alpha.

Measured at 390px: the hero heading is 25px/35px at weight 700 in a 345px-wide box, body copy is 20px/30px in a 348px-wide box, the trial pill is 189px by 45px, and the navigation bar is 60px tall on `#090a25` showing an "Open main menu" control in `#9ca3af`. Character counts by size on mobile: 20px 2,338, 17.5px 806, 22.5px 389, 15px 367, 37.5px 145, 16px 126, 25px 63, 30px 57, 60px 10. The stylesheet declares Tailwind `min-width` media queries at 640px, 768px, 1024px, 1280px and 1536px, plus `not all and (min-width: …)` rules at 640px, 768px and 1024px; what each rule changes on screen was not measured.
