# Gusto

## Overview

Gusto (gusto.com) sells payroll, benefits and HR software to small businesses, and its home page sounds confident and friendly rather than corporate. The site was measured at 1280px wide (page height 8752px) and at 390px. The hero is a full-bleed, saturated pink-to-red photographic gradient. A centered white serif headline, "Hire, pay, and grow like a pro.", sits on it with a short line of supporting text and two buttons: a deep-teal "Create account" and a translucent "How Gusto works".

Below the hero the page settles onto a warm off-white canvas (#fdfdfb). A product screenshot and a press-logo strip (Forbes Advisor, Newsweek and others) lead into the proof headline "500,000+ businesses run on Gusto.", which is surrounded by small photographs of business owners. Then a product story, "Payroll [icon] at the speed of… wait, that's it?", a phone mock-up announcing "All set!", a full-bleed photo band "Hiring tools. On your terms.", a "Small business success is no small thing." editorial row with a deep-teal testimonial card, an eight-tile product grid ("Your business does a lot. We do, too."), a stats panel (9 out of 10, 152 hrs/year, 4.5+ stars, 500k+ businesses), a deep teal "Ready to work with Gusto?" section with four illustrated link cards, a red "DO WHAT YOU DO BEST. We'll handle the rest." sign-off, and a dense white footer.

The personality comes from contrast. Headlines use the expressive display serif Boogy Brut at very large sizes with tight -2.5% tracking. The running text uses the neutral sans Matter in warm grey-browns (#56514e, #6f6965, #1c1b1a). Color is anchored by Gusto's teal family (#0f6469 buttons, #03393d and #00535c panels, #1e7676 link text), and the photography and illustrations add warm pinks, orchid (#eca4f7) and sunny tones. The microcopy is plainspoken and a little playful ("wait, that's it?", "Stack your system, then stack the team.").

## Colors

- **Canvas — #fdfdfb.** The largest measured background area (7669): a warm, barely-cream white behind most sections and the product cards.
- **Page body — #ffffff.** The `body` background, the header and the footer (area 7149).
- **Ink — #56514e.** Body paragraph color (1355 characters), a warm grey-brown.
- **Ink strong — #1c1b1a.** Headings on light grounds, nav, footer and link text (1241 characters).
- **Ink tertiary — #6f6965.** Supporting copy (1092 characters); the CSS names it `--gwb-color-neutral-tertiary-text`.
- **Ink inverse muted — #ffffffb3.** 70% white for secondary text on teal and photographic grounds.
- **Primary — #0f6469.** The fill of every measured teal CTA ("Create account", "Compare plans", "Get started"), with white #ffffff text.
- **Brand text — #1e7676.** Teal text links such as "See demo" and "Get in touch"; the CSS calls it `--gwb-color-brand-primary-text-default`. On hover these links darken to #00535c.
- **Surface deep — #03393d.** The darkest teal, used as the "Ready to work with Gusto?" band (area 1035) and as the text color of the white inverse button.
- **Surface teal — #00535c.** The testimonial card fill and hover text color; the CSS declares it `--gwb-ring-primary-color-border-default`.
- **Quote mint — #84dbc5.** The oversized quotation mark on the teal testimonial card.
- **Nav hover background — #e9f7f3.** The pale mint wash behind a nav item on hover.
- **Accent orchid — #eca4f7.** A pink-violet illustration ground (area 277).
- **Glass — #33333333.** The translucent fill of the hero's secondary button.
- **Hairline — #d1cecc.** The most common border color (9 elements); card border on photographs is #ffffff66.

The full-bleed pink/red hero is a photograph or gradient image, not a flat token. Pure black #000000 also covers a large area (942), behind photo and video bands.

## Typography

Two families, loaded as web fonts:

- **Boogy Brut** (loaded at weights 400, 500 and 700) for display headlines. The site declares Georgia, serif as the fallback, so a rebuild without the font should use a high-contrast serif fallback the same way.
- **Matter** (loaded as a variable font, 100–1000) for everything else. The site's fallback stack is `-apple-system, system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

Measured roles:

| Role | Family | Size / line height | Weight | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display (hero h1) | Boogy Brut | 100.388px / 105.407px | 400 | -2.5% |
| Heading (h2 "500,000+ businesses…") | Boogy Brut | 84px / 75.6px | 700 | -2.5% |
| Subheading (h3 "Payroll") | Boogy Brut | 64px / 64px | 700 | — |
| Body | Matter | 16px / 22.4px | 400 | — |
| Body small (card copy) | Matter | 14px / 19.6px | 400 | — |
| Button / link | Matter | 16px / 24px | 500 | — |

By character count the page reads mostly at 16px (2240), 14px (1792) and 18px (679), with 28px, 72px, 24px and 48px for card titles and display moments. Measured tracking: 0.32px on 14px and 16px Matter text (1090 and 218 characters), -2.5% on Boogy Brut at 48px, 72px, 84px and 100.388px, and -0.4px at 24px. Weights are 400 (4120 characters) and 500 (1365), with 600 and 700 used sparingly. The footer's column text measured 16px/24px, weight 500, with 0.32px tracking.

## Layout

The desktop header is 88px tall and white, with content from 144px to 1247px: the Gusto wordmark, then Products, Business types, For accountants, Resources and Pricing, then "See demo", "Sign in" and a teal "Create account" button on the right (10 visible header links measured). The hero runs full bleed from 88px to 1032px with centered content between 255px and 1025px.

Content sections alternate between centered statements (the 500,000+ proof block, "Your business does a lot") and asymmetric editorial rows (the payroll copy column against the phone mock-up, the three-column "Small business success" row). The product grid shows 4 cards per row (max 4 card columns measured). Spacing tokens are small: gaps of 8px (60 uses), 16px, 20px and 24px dominate, with 32px, 40px and 64px between larger blocks. Section padding reaches 96px and 128px. Card padding is 8px for product tiles and 24px for the testimonial card.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is minimal. Only one rendered shadow was measured: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2) 0px 4px 8px 0px`, on two elements. The design system's CSS also declares `--gwb-shadow-100: 0px 4px 12px rgba(28,28,28,0.08), 0px 2px 8px rgba(28,28,28,0.04)`, but that value was not measured as rendered on the home page. Most depth comes from layering: the deep-teal #03393d section against the off-white canvas, white product tiles with #d1cecc hairlines, photography cut into cards, and the phone mock-up overlapping a pink shape.

## Shapes

The measured radius family is varied and soft: 8px is by far the most common (109 elements — cards, nav items, tiles), 14px for buttons (18), 12px for the translucent hero button and others (18), 999px pills (6), 16px for the testimonial cards (5), 24px panels (including a `0 0 24px 24px` bottom-rounded shape and a `24px 24px 0 0` top-rounded one), 100px and 500px for large rounded image masks, and one 4px element. The CSS radius scale (`--border-radius-50` 2px through `--border-radius-2500` 100px, plus `--gwb-border-radius-350: 14px`) confirms 14px as the button corner.

- **Inverse button.** On the deep-teal section: white #ffffff fill, #03393d text, 14px radius, 42px tall ("Sign up for free", "Create free account").

- **Text link.** "Discover payroll" style: #1c1b1a Matter 16px/16px weight 500 with an 8px bottom padding for the underline/arrow. Teal links ("See demo", "Get in touch") use #1e7676 and darken to #00535c on hover.
- **Nav item.** #1c1b1a text in an 8px-radius hit area. On hover the text and border turn #00535c and a #e9f7f3 mint background fills in (measured on Pricing, Products, Business types, For accountants and Resources).
- **Product card.** #fdfdfb fill, 8px radius, 8px padding, 14px/19.6px body text, illustration at top and a bold title. Used in the four-column product grid.
- **Testimonial card.** #00535c fill, 16px radius, 24px padding, a 48px Georgia quotation mark in #84dbc5 and large white quote text, then the customer name and business.
- **Stats panel.** Big serif numerals ("9 out of 10", "152 hrs/year") with small grey descriptions on the right, divided by hairlines.
- **Footer.** White, multi-column (Pricing, Built for you, For Accountants, Get Started, Services, Company, Licenses, Support, Resources, Tools), Matter text in #1c1b1a.

## Design System Architecture

On this site Gusto's design tokens ship as CSS custom properties with a `--gwb-` prefix, alongside an unprefixed set with the same structure. We found no public design-system documentation, so the structure below comes from the shipped CSS (400 root variables measured):

- **Color primitives and semantics**: `--gwb-color-*` (117 variables) and `--color-*` (102), e.g. `--gwb-color-brand-primary-text-default: #1e7676`, `--gwb-color-brand-primary-text-hover: #00535c`, `--gwb-color-brand-primary-border-elevated: #60ccb6`, `--gwb-color-neutral-tertiary-text: #6f6965`, `--divider-neutral-color-border-default: #e7e4e2`, and reserved ramps such as `--gwb-color-intelligence-*` and `--gwb-color-reserved-*`.
- **Component tokens**: `--gwb-button-*` and `--button-*` (77 together), e.g. `--gwb-button-primary-inverse-color-background: #ffffff`, `--gwb-button-primary-inverse-color-text: #00535c`, `--gwb-button-primary-inverse-color-background-hover: #e1f4ef`, `--button-primary-error-color-background-active: #a9283e`. Inputs, tables and focus rings follow the same pattern (`--gwb-input-*`, `--gwb-table-*`, `--gwb-ring-primary-color-border-default: #00535c`).
- **Scales**: `--gwb-spacing-*` / `--spacing-*` in rem (e.g. `--spacing-1600: 4rem`), `--gwb-type-size-*` (0.75rem to 2rem), `--gwb-type-letterSpacing-*`, and `--gwb-border-radius-*` / `--border-radius-*` (2px to 100px).

The naming follows a `category-role-variant-property-state` pattern (`button-secondary-inverse-color-border-disabled`). We checked Gusto's GitHub organisation (https://github.com/Gusto) through the GitHub API and found no design-system or token repository among its public repositories; the closest is `react-masked-field`, "A masked input component built in React". The about page (https://gusto.com/about) returned HTTP 403.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Set headlines in Boogy Brut at large sizes with -2.5% tracking, and keep body copy in Matter.
- Use warm neutrals for text (#1c1b1a, #56514e, #6f6965) on the #fdfdfb canvas.
- Use #0f6469 teal with white text for the primary action, and give buttons 14px corners.
- Switch to white buttons with #03393d text on the deep-teal sections.
- Use 8px corners on cards and nav hit areas, and 16px on feature or testimonial cards.
- Give nav items the #e9f7f3 mint hover with #00535c text.
- Pair the type with real photography of small-business owners and bright, saturated illustration.

**Don't**
- Don't set body copy in pure black; the measured text palette is warm brown-greys.
- Don't use the serif for UI text, buttons or long paragraphs.
- Don't add heavy shadows; the page relies on color blocks and hairlines.
- Don't use #0000ee or other browser-default link colors. One nav anchor computed #0000ee, a browser default rather than a brand color.
- Don't square off the buttons or cards; every measured interactive surface has a radius.

## Responsive Behavior

Measured at 390px:

- **Hero h1** drops from 100.388px/105.407px to **52px/54.6px**, still Boogy Brut 400 at -2.5%.
- **Section h2** drops from 84px/75.6px to **52px/46.8px**, weight 700.
- **Body text** stays 16px/22.4px in #56514e.
- **Primary CTA**: "Create free account" is 56px tall and 326px wide, with the same #0f6469 fill and 14px radius.
- **Header**: the mobile nav element measured 88px tall on white, with 7 visible header links and a menu toggle labelled "menu" (10 visible links on desktop).
- The mobile hero band's content runs from 16px to 374px.
- Most frequent mobile text sizes by character count: 16px, 14px, 18px and 28px, with 40px and 52px for headings.

Breakpoint values, the mobile menu's open state and tablet layouts were not measured.

## Iteration Guide

1. Set the canvas to #fdfdfb with white #ffffff header and footer, and text in #1c1b1a / #56514e / #6f6965.
2. Load Boogy Brut and Matter. Build the type ramp: 100.388px display, 84px and 64px headings, 16px/22.4px body, 16px/24px weight-500 buttons.
3. Build the teal button set: #0f6469 primary (56px hero, 42px inline), white inverse on #03393d, translucent #33333333 glass in the hero, all with 14px corners (12px for glass).
4. Lay out sections that alternate centered serif statements with editorial two- and three-column rows, then a four-column card grid.
5. Use #00535c and #03393d for the deep feature bands and the testimonial card, with #84dbc5 for quote accents.
6. Add the nav hover (#e9f7f3 background, #00535c text, 8px radius) and the #1e7676 → #00535c link hover.
7. When you need a token that isn't here, look in the `--gwb-*` custom properties first and measure it on gusto.com before adding it.
