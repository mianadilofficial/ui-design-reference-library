# Viriyah Insurance

## Overview
The Viriyah Insurance Public Company Limited (`https://www.viriyah.co.th/en`, measured at `https://www.viriyah.co.th/en/`, title "The Viriyah Insurance Public Company limited") is a Thai general insurer. The extractor found 558 visible elements on a 1280px-wide, 6717px-tall page. Its own copy states "1947 February 3: Viriyah Insurance Company Limited was incorporated under the name Asiatic Distributing Company Limited with 5 million Baht paid-up capital", "79 years of pride in the truly Thailand's company", "more than 6,000 quality personnel" and "more than 160 Branches".

The capture shows a corporate, trust-signalling design: a white header with the bilingual logo (a blue-and-gold emblem with the Thai name and "VIRIYAH INSURANCE"), grey-weight nav links "Products", "Services", "Customer Service", "Agents", a search button, a language switch "EN", a claim-service hotline "1557", and two flush rectangular tabs at the right end of the bar: gold "Contact us" and royal-blue "Online Insurance". Copy is warm, institutional and benefit-led. A black tribute pop-up with a monochrome portrait and Thai mourning text covers the top of the first capture; it is a temporary overlay, not part of the brand system, and was left out of the measurements (the second extraction pass excludes the overlay scrim).

## Do's and Don'ts
Do
- Anchor pages on royal blue #1434a2 with gold #fcaf17 for the one contact tab.
- Use full-bleed blue bands with white headings in DBSathornMedium 48px.
- Set flush rectangular tabs (80px high, 0 radius) at the end of the header.
- Use 20px-radius bordered product cards and 4px timeline cards.
- Pair real photography of buildings and people with short benefit headlines.

Don't
- Don't style pop-ups or tribute overlays as brand components.
- Don't use Bootstrap's palette (#ffc107, #28a745, #20c997) as brand colors.
- Don't round the header tabs.
- Don't use letter-spacing; none is set.
