## Overview

Carasti Thailand (https://carasti.com/THL/en, title "Monthly & Long-Term Car Rental in Thailand - All-Inclusive | Carasti Thailand") is the Thai edition of a car-subscription service that runs the same template for several countries; its country switcher links the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Thailand and Singapore versions, and the footer reads "© 2026 Carasti Holding LTD.". The page sells new cars for one flat monthly fee, with insurance and maintenance included. It is in English, with a Thai-language switch ("ภาษาไทย") beside a Thai flag in the header.

The first screen is a 70px translucent white header (the "carasti" wordmark in blue with a circular mark, then "Browse Cars", "How it Works", "FAQs", "Carasti for Business", "Blog", "Contact Us", a search icon and the language link) above a two-column hero. The left column holds the h1 "Cars Made Easy - The New Way to Drive Cars" in 53.376px bold slate, the line "Monthly & Long-Term Car Rental in Thailand", four bullets ("Order Your New Car in 2 Minutes", "Delivered To Your Doorstep", "Insurance & Maintenance Included", "Flexible Terms From 3 to 36 Months") and a blue "Browse Cars Now" button. The right half is a full-height photograph of a family receiving the keys beside a car. Floating LINE and "Need Help? Chat with us" buttons overlap the photograph in the capture; they are chat widgets, not brand UI, and are excluded here.

Below, "Find Your Perfect Car in Thailand" introduces a row of four car tiles on light grey with a "View all" link and arrow. Each tile shows a cut-out car photograph, a small corner badge ("Top Pick", "NEW 0km", "Get Today"), a green "Discounted" tag on some, the model name ("Mazda 2 S Grade 2024", "Ford Everest 2.0L Turbo Sport 4x2 6A...", "Toyota Yaris Ativ Entry 2019", "Toyota Hilux Revo Smart Cab 2022"), a grey line such as "2024 · Petrol", and a price with a struck-through original ("฿ 13,999/Month"). A split section "How it Works" pairs a photograph of a smiling passenger holding a phone with three steps ("Choose Your Car", "Ready in No Time", "Drive and Decide") and a blue "Find My Car" button. A band of car-manufacturer logos follows, then, per the opened page text, "Drive Brand New Cars in Thailand – No Loans, No Down Payments" with "Experience Carasti" and "Contact Us", an "All-Inclusive, One Simple Price – Just Add Fuel!" comparison, testimonials, FAQs and a footer with App Store and Google Play badges. The voice is upbeat and plain: short benefit lines and reassurance about debt and depreciation.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the page white with grey panels and let `{colors.primary}` blue mark the single action in each area.
- Do set headings in bold (700), card and step titles in 600, paragraphs in 400.
- Do use 6px corners on buttons and 10px on tiles.
- Do show the price as "฿ 13,999/Month" with the original struck through, and tag savings in `{colors.green-discount}`.
- Don't use heavy shadows; the header and outlined button carry only faint ones.
- Don't recolor the blue; hover goes one step darker to `{colors.primary-hover}`.
- Don't treat the LINE and chat buttons as part of the interface.
- Don't copy the template placeholders (for example "{{country}}" in the language switcher labels) that the opened page text shows.
